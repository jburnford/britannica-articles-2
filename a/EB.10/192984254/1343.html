CATALAUNI, called also Durocatalauni; a town

Catalauni of Gallia Belgica: Catalauni, the people. A name rather of the lower age than of classical antiquity. Now Chalons sur Marne, in Champagne. E. Long. 4. 35. N. Lat. 48. 55.
