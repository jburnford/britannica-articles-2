CARLOCK

article · 260 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARLOCK, in commerce, a sort of isinglass, made with the sturgeon's bladder, imported from Archangel. The chief use of it is for clarifying wine, but is also used by the dyers. The best carlock comes from Astracan, where a great quantity of sturgeon is caught.
