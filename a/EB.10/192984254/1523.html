CELETES

article · 328 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CELETES, or CELETR (from κῆλη, a race-horse) in antiquity, denote single or saddle-horses, by way of contradistinction from those yoked or harnessed together, called bigarii, quadrigarii, &c. The same denomination is also given to the cavaliers or riders on horseback; and hence some deduce celeres, the name of Romulus's guard.
