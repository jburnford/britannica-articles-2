CAUSTICS

article · 359 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: PHARMACY

CAUSTICS, is an appellation given to substances of so hot and fiery a nature, that, being applied, they consume, and as it were burn, the texture of the parts, like hot iron.

Caustics are generally divided into four sorts; the common stronger caustic, the common milder caustic, the antimonial caustic, and the lunar caustic. See PHARMACY and CHEMISTRY Index.
