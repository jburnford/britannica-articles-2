CAGE

article · 206 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAGE, in carpentry, signifies an outer work of timber, inclosing another within it. In this sense we say, the cage of a wind-mill. The cage of a staircase denotes the wooden sides or walls which enclose it.
