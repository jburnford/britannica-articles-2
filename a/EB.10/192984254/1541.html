CELLARS

article · 383 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CELLARS, in modern building, are the lowest rooms in a house, the ceilings of which usually lie level with the surface of the ground on which the house is built; or they are situated under the pavement before the house, especially in streets and squares.

Cellars, and other places vaulted under ground, were called by the Greeks hypogæa: the Italians still call them fundi deli case.
