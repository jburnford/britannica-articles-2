CAMPS, FRANCIS DE

article · 212 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAMPS, FRANCIS DE, abbot of Notre Dame at Sigy, was born at Amiens in 1643; and distinguished himself by his knowledge of medals, by writing a history of France, and several other works. He died at Paris in 1723.
