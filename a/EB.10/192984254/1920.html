CHAROUX

article · 178 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHAROUX, a town of France, in the Bourbonnois, seated on an eminence, near the river Sioule. It has two parishes, which are in different dioceses. E. Long. 3. 15. N. Lat. 46. 10.
