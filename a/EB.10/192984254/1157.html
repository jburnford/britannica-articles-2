CARUNCULA, or CARUNCLE, in Anatomy, a term denoting a little piece of flesh, and applied to several parts of the human body. Thus,

Carunculae Myrtiformes, in Anatomy, fleshy knobs about the size of a myrtle berry, supposed to owe their origin to the breaking of the hymen. See ANATOMY Index.
