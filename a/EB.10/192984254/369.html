CALDERINUS, DOMITIUS

article · 254 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALDERINUS, DOMITIUS, a learned critic, born at Calderia near Verona. He read lectures upon polite literature at Rome with great reputation; and was the first who ventured to write upon the most difficult of the ancient poets. He died very young in 1477.
