Brothers of CHARITY

sub_entry · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Brothers of CHARITY, a sort of religious hospitalers, founded about the year 1297, since denominated Billetsins. They took the third order of St Francis, and the scapulary, making the three usual vows, but without begging.
