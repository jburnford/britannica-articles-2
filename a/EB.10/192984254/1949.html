CHATEAU-du-Loire

sub_entry · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHATEAU-du-Loire, a town of France, in Maine, famous for sustaining a siege of seven years against the Count of Mans. It is seated on the river Loire, in E. Long. 0. 25. N. Lat. 47. 40.
