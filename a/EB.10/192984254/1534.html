CELLA

article · 223 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CELLA likewise signified the adyta, or inmost and most retired parts of temples, wherein the images of the gods to whom the edifices were consecrated were preserved. In this sense we meet with cella Iovis, cella Concordiae.
