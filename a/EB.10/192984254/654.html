CANDISH

CANDISH, a considerable province of Asia, in the dominions of the Great Mogul, bounded by Chytor and Malva on the north, Orixia on the east, Decan on the south, and Guzerat on the west. It is populous and rich; and abounds in cotton, rice, and indigo. Bram-pore is the capital town.
