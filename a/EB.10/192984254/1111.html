CARRUCAGE

article · 189 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARRUCAGE, Carucage, or Caruage, in husbandry, denotes the ploughing of ground, either ordinary, as for grain, hemp, and flax; or extraordinary, as for wood, dyers weed, rape, and the like.
