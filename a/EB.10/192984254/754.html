CANTABRUM

article · 189 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CANTABRUM, in antiquity, a large kind of flag used by the Roman emperors, distinguished by its peculiar colour, and bearing on it some word or motto of good omen, to encourage the soldiery.
