CAPRA Saltans

sub_entry · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAPRA Saltans, in Meteorology, a fiery meteor or exhalation sometimes seen in the atmosphere. It forms

an inflected line, resembling in some measure the caperings of a goat; whence it has its name.
