CHIARI, JOSEPH

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHIARI, JOSEPH, a celebrated Italian painter, was the disciple of Carlo Maratti; and adorned the churches and palaces of Rome with a great number of fine paintings. He died of an apoplexy in 1727, aged 73.
