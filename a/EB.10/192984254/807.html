CAPARASON

article · 274 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAPARASON, or CAPARISON, the covering or clothing laid over a horse; especially a sumpter horse, or horse of state. The word is Spanish, being an augmentative of cope, caput, head.

Anciently the caparasons were a kind of iron armour, wherewith horses were covered in battle.
