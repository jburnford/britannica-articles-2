CASUALTY

article · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CASUALTY, in Scots Law. Casualties of a superior, are those duties and enrolments which a superior has a right to demand out of his vassal's estate, over and besides the constant yearly duties established by the red-dendo of his charter upon certain casual events.
