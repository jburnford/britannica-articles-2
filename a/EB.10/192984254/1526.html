CELEUSTES

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CELEUSTES, in Ancient Navigation, the boat-swain or officer appointed to give the rowers the signal, when they were to pull, and when to stop. He is also denominated epopeus, and by the Romans, porticus; sometimes simply hortator.
