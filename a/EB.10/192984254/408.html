CALIGO

article · 161 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALIGO, or CALIGATIO, in Medicine, an opacity, or cloudiness of the anterior surface of the crystalline lens of the eye, causing a dimness or suffusion of sight.
