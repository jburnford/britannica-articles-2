Crypto-CALVINISTS

sub_entry · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Crypto-CALVINISTS, a name given to the favourers of Calvinism in Saxony, on account of their secret attachment to the Genevan doctrine and discipline. Many of them suffered by the decrees of the convocation of Torgow, held in 1576. The Calvinists in their progress

Calvinists progress have divided into various branches, or lesser sects.
