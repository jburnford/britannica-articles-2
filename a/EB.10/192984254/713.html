Expectative CANONS

sub_entry · 202 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Expectative CANONS, were such as, without having any revenue or prebend, had the title and dignities of canons, a voice in the chapter, and a place in the choir; till such time as a prebend should fall.
