CHARGED

article · 203 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHARGED, in Heraldry, a shield carrying some impress or figure, is said to be charged therewith; so also, when one bearing, or charge, has another figure added upon it, it is properly said to be charged.
