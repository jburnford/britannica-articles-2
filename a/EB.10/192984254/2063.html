CHIAPA el Real

sub_entry · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHIAPA el Real, a town of Mexico, in a province of the same name, with a bishop's see. Its principal trade consists in chocolate-nuts, cotton, and sugar. W. Long. 98. 35. N. Lat. 16. 20.
