CAMPICURSIO

article · 300 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAMPICURSIO, in the ancient military art, a march of armed men for several miles, from and back again to the camp, to instruct them in the military pace. This exercise was nearly akin to the decursio, from which it only differed, in that the latter was performed by horsemen, the former also by foot.
