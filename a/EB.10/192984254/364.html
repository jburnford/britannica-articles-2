CALCULUS Tiburtinus

sub_entry · 392 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALCULUS Tiburtinus, a sort of figured stone, formed in great plenty about the cataracts of the Anio, and other rivers in Italy; of a white colour, and in shape oblong, round, or echinated. They are a species of the striae lapideae, or striaclites, and generated like them; and so like sugar plums, that it is a common jest at Rome to deceive the inexperienced by serving them up as desserts.
