CHALDRON

article · 256 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHALDRON, a dry English measure, consisting of thirty-six bushels, heaped up according to the sealed bushel kept at Guildhall, London: but on ship-board, twenty-one chaldrons of coals are allowed to the score. The chaldron should weigh two thousand pounds.
