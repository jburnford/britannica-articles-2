BUYING the Smallpox

sub_entry · 362 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUYING the Smallpox, is an appellation given to a method of procuring that disease by an operation similar to inoculation; frequent in South Wales, where it has obtained time out of mind. It is performed either by rubbing some of the pus taken out of a pustule of a variolous person on the skin, or by making a puncture in the skin with a pin dipped in such pus.
