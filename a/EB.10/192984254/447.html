CALLOO

article · 206 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALLOO, a fortress in the Netherlands, in the territory of Waes, on the river Scheldt, subject to the house of Austria. The Dutch were defeated here by the Spaniards in 1638. E. Long. 4. 10. N. Lat. 51. 15.
