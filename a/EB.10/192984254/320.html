CALAMARIE, in Botany, an order of plants in the Fragmenta methodi naturalis of Linnaeus; in which he has the following genera, viz. bobartia, scirpus, cyperus, eriophorum, carex, sphaerostachys, flagellaria, juncus. See BOTANY.
