CASSUMAR, in the Materia Medica, a root resembling that of zedoary.

It is cardiac and sudorific, and famous in nervous cases; it is also an ingredient in many compositions, and is prescribed in powders, boluses, and infusions. Its dose is from five to fifteen grains.
