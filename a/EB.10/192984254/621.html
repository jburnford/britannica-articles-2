CAN-Hook

sub_entry · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAN-Hook, an instrument used to sling a cask by the ends of the staves: it is formed by fixing a broad and flat hook at each end of a short rope; and the tackle by which the cask to sling may be hoisted or lowered, is hooked to the middle of the rope.
