BURSA

article · 427 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BURSA, or BURSER, (Bursarius), is used in middle-age writers for a treasurer or cash-keeper. In this sense we meet with burfals of colleges. Conventual burfals were officers in monasteries, who were to deliver up their account yearly on the day after Michaelmas. The word is formed from the Latin burfa, whence also the English word purse; hence also the officer, who in a college is called burfals, in a ship is called purser.
