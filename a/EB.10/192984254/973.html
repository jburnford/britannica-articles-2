CARDINAL Numbers

sub_entry · 162 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARDINAL Numbers, in Grammar, are the numbers one, two, three, &c. which are indeclinable; in opposition to the ordinal numbers, first, second, third, fourth, &c.
