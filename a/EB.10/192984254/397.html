CALIBER, or CALLIPER

sub_entry · 173 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALIBER, or CALLIPER, properly denotes the diameter of any body; thus we say, two columns of the same caliber, the caliber of the bore of a gun, the caliber of a bullet, &c.
