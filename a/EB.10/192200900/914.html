NECROPOLIS

article · 277 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NECROPOLIS, a suburb of Alexandria in Egypt. It signifies "the City of the Dead;" wherein there were temples, gardens, and superb mausoleums. Here Cleopatra is said to have applied the asp to her breast, to prevent being led in triumph by Augustus, who endeavoured to save her.
