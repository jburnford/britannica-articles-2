MURCIA, the Pagan goddess of idleness. The name is taken from mureus or mureidus, an obsolete word, signifying a dull, slothful, or lazy person.—The statues of this goddess were always covered with dust and moss, to express her idleness and negligence. She had a temple in Rome, at the foot of the Aventine mount.
