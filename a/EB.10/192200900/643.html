MURGI, or MURGIS, in Ancient Geography, the last town of Bætica, next the Tarraconensis: the Urce of Ptolemy. Now Muxara, a port-town of Granada, on the Mediterranean. W. Long. 1° 50'. N. Lat. 37° 6'.
