MILTHORP

MILTHORP, a port town of Westmoreland, at the mouth of the Can, eight miles south from Kendal. It is the only sea port in the county; and goods are brought hither in small vessels from Grange in Lancashire. Here are two paper mills. It has a market on Friday, and a fair on Old May day; and there is a good stone bridge over the river Betha, which runs through the town.
