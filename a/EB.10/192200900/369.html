MONTFORT

MONTFORT, a town of Germany, in the circle of Suabia, on the confines of Tirol, 16 miles south of Lindow, and the lake Constance. It is capital of a country of the same name, which has been almost all purchased by the house of Austria. E. Long. 9. 51. N. Lat. 47. 22.
