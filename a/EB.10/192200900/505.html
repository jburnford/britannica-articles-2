MOTIVE

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MOTIVE, is sometimes applied to that faculty of the human mind, by which we pursue good and avoid evil. Thus Hobbes distinguishes the faculties of the mind into two sorts, the cognitive and motive.
