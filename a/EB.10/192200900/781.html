NAKIB

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NAKIB, in the oriental dignities, the name of an officer who is a deputy to the cadiliskier, or, as he may be called, the lord high chancellor of Egypt, appointed by the grand signior. His office is to carry the standard of Mahomet.
