MUREX

article · 227 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MUREX, a caltrap or iron instrument, with sharp points projecting in every direction, used by the Romans as a defence against the enemy's horse; so called, probably from some resemblance to the spines on the shell of the murex.
