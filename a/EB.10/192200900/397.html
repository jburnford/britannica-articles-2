MOON-Eyes

sub_entry · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MOON-Eyes, among horses, when the weakness of the eye increases or decreases according to the course of the moon; so that in the wane of the moon his eyes are muddy and troubled, and at new moon they clear up. This observation is probably inaccurate.
