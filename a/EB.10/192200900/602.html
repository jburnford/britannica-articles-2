MULVIA

article · 204 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MULVIA, a river of Barbary in Africa, which rises in the mountains of Atlas, and divides the empire of Morocco from that of Algiers, and then falls into the Mediterranean, to the westward of Marfalquiver.
