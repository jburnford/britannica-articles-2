NEALING of steel

sub_entry · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: TEMPERING

NEALING of steel, is the heating it in the fire to a blood-red heat, and then taking it out, and letting it cool gently of itself. This is done to make it softer, in order to engrave and punch upon it. See TEMPERING and ENGRAVING.
