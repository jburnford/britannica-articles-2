MUSEIA

article · 318 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MUSEIA, were Grecian festivals in honour of the Muses, celebrated with games every fifth year, particularly by the Thespians. The Macedonians also observed a festival of the same name in honour of Jupiter and the Muses, which lasted for nine days, and was celebrated with stage plays, songs, and poetical compositions.
