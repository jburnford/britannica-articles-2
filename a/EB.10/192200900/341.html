MONTALCINO, a small populous town of Italy in Tuscany, and in the territory of Siena, with a bishop's see. It is seated on a mountain, 17 miles south-east of Siena, and 44 south-east of Florence. E. Long. 11. 30. N. Lat. 43. 7.
