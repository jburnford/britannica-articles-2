MINDEN (the principality of), in Germany, lies in
the circle of Westphalia, to the north of the county
of Ravensburg, and along each side of the river Weser.

It is about 22 miles square, and Minden and Peter- shagen are the principal places. It was formerly a bishopric, but is now secularized; was afterwards ceded to the elector of Brandenburg, and is now annexed to the new kingdom of Westphalia.
