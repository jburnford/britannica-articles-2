Rhenish MILSTONE

sub_entry · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Rhenish MILSTONE, a stone which has been classed among volcanic products, on account of its appearance, which is a blackish gray, porous, and very much resembling a lava of Mount Vesuvius.
