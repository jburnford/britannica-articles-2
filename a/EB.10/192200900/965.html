NEOMAGUS

NEOMAGUS, (Ptolemy); NOVIOMAGUS, (Antonine); a town of the Regni in Britain: now thought to be Guildford in Surrey, (Lhuyd); or Croydon, (Talbott). But Camden takes it to be Woodcote, two miles to the south of Croydon, where traces of an ancient town are still to be seen.
