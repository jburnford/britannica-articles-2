MONADELPHIA, (from mons, alone, and adelphie, a brotherhood;) a "single brotherhood;" The name of the 16th class in Linnaeus's sexual system, consisting of plants with hermaphrodite flowers; in which all the stamens are united below into one body or cylinder, through which passes the pistillum.
