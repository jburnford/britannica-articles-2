NEGINOTH. This term is read before some of the Psalms, as Psalm lxvii. It signifies string instruments of music, to be played on by the fingers, or women musicians; and the titles of these Psalms where this word is found, may be thus translated, A psalm of David to the master of music, who presides over the string instruments.
