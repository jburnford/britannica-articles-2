MINAGNGHINIM, a pulsative instrument of music, among the Hebrews, which was a square table of wood, fitted with a handle; over this table was

Minaghi stretched an iron chain, or hempen cord, passing through
min balls of wood or brass, which struck against the table,
Mindanao when the instrument was shaken, and occasioned a clear
found, which might be heard at a great distance.
