MINOR

article · 327 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MINOR, in Musick, is applied to certain concords, which differ from or are lower than others of the same denomination by a lesser semitone or four commas.— Thus we say, a third minor, or lesser third, or a sixth major and minor. Concords that admit of major and minor, i. e. greater and less, are said to be imperfect concords.
