MONTMORENCY

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONTMORENCY, a town of France, with the title of a duchy, remarkable for the tombs of the dukes of this name. It is seated on a hill, near a large valley, fertile in fruits, especially excellent cherries. E. Long. 2. 24. N. Lat. 48. 59.
