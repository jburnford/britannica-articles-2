MONTESA

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONTESA, a very strong town of Spain, in the kingdom of Valencia. It is the seat of an order of knighthood of the same name; and is five miles from Xativa. W. Long. 0. 30. N. Lat. 39. 0.
