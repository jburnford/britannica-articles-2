SAN FERNANDO

article · 314 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SAN FERNANDO, near the entrance of the Golfo
Dolce, in 15 degrees 18 minutes north latitude, has
lately been fortified by the Spaniards, for the purpose
of checking the Musquito-men, logwood-cutters, and
bay-men. It is a very good harbour, with safe an-
chorage from the north and east winds, in eight fath-
oms water.
