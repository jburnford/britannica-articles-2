RHOEADEÆ

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHOEADEÆ (rhoeas, Linnaeus's name, after Dioscorides, for the red poppy), the name of the 27th order in Linnaeus's fragments of a natural method, consisting of poppy and a few genera which resemble it in habit and structure. See Botany Index.
