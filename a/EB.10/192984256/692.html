SCALADO, or SCALLADE

sub_entry · 198 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SCALADO, or SCALLADE, in the art of war, a furious assault made on the wall or rampart of a city, or other fortified place, by means of ladders, without carrying on works in form, to secure the men.
