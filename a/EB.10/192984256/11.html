RHOMBOIDES

article · 256 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHOMBOIDES, in Anatomy, a thin, broad, and obliquely square fleshy muscle, situated between the basis of the scapula and the spina dorsa; so called from its figure. Its general use is to draw backward and upward the subspinal portion of the basis scapulae.
