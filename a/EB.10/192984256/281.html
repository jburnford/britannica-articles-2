Rock RUBY

sub_entry · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Rock RUBY, the amethystozontas of the ancients, is found in Syria, Calcutta, Cananor, Cambaya, and Ethiopia. It is the most valued of all the varieties of garnets, and is frequently sold as a ruby under the name of rubinus Rufficum.
