ROLANDRA

article · 630 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ROLANDRA, a genus of plants belonging to the syngenesia class; and in the natural method ranking under the 49th order, Compositeæ. The common calyx consists of distinct flosculi, between each of which are short squamæ, the whole forming a round head. The partial calyx is bivalved. The corolla is small and funnel-shaped, the tube small as a thread, the laciniæ short and acute. The stamens are five; the style bifid. It has no other seed-vessel except the partial calyx, which contains a long three-sided seed. Of this there is only one species, viz. the Argentea, a native of the West Indies, and found in copse and waste lands.
