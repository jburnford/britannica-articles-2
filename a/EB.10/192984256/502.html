SAMIAN EARTH

article · 406 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SAMIAN EARTH, in the materia medica, the name of two species of marl used in medicine, viz. 1. The white kind, called by the ancients collyrium samium, being alstringent, and therefore good in diarrhoeas, dysenteries, and hemorrhages; they also used it externally in inflammations of all kinds. 2. The brownish white kind, called after samius by Dioscorides; this also stands recommended as an alstringent.
