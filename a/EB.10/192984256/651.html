SAUCISSON

article · 234 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SAUCISSON, is likewise a kind of falcine, longer than the common ones; they serve to raise batteries and to repair breaches. They are also used in making epaulements, in stopping passages, and in making traverses over a wet ditch, &c.
