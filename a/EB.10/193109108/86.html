FORTAVENTURA

article · 286 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FORTAVENTURA, one of the Canary islands, 50 miles long, and from 8 to 22 broad, fertile in corn and excellent fruits; and remarkable for its numerous breed of goats and prodigious quantity of goat-milk cheese. The number of inhabitants amounts to 10,000. W. Long. 14. 32. N. Lat. 28. 4.
