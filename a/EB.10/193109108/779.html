GENTILIS, ALBERICUS, professor of civil law at
Oxford; an Italian by birth. He had quitted Italy
with his father, on account of religion. He wrote fe-
veral works; three books, in particular, De jure belli,
which have not been unserviceable to Grotius. He
died at London in 1608.
