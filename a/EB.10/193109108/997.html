GLUCINA

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GLUCINA, in Chemistry, an earthy substance which was discovered by Vauquelin in 1798, in analyzing the emerald of which it forms a component part. For an account of its properties and combinations, see CHEMISTRY, No 1165.
