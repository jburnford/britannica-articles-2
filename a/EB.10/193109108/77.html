FORAGE

article · 296 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FORAGE, in the military art, denotes hay, oats, barley, wheat, grass, clover, &c. brought into the camp by the troopers, for the sustenance of their horses.

It is the business of the quartermaster general to appoint the method of forrage, and post proper guards for the security of the forragers.
