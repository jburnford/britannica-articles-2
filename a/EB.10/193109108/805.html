GEORGIA

article · 202 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GEORGIA, a cluster of barren islands in the South sea, to the eastward of the coast of Terra del Fuego, in lat. 54° 30' S. and long. 37° W. One of these islands is 90 miles in length, and 30 in breadth.
