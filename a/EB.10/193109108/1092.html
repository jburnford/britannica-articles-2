GOOD Bearing

sub_entry · 392 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GOOD Bearing (bonus gestus), signifies an exact carriage or behaviour of a subject towards the king and the people, whereunto some persons upon their misbehaviour are bound: and he that is bound to this, is said to be more strictly bound than to the peace: because where the peace is not broken, the surety de bono

gestu may be forfeited by the number of a man's company, or by their weapons.
