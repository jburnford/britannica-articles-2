GEMINI

GEMINI, in Astronomy, the TWINS; a constellation or sign of the zodiac, the third in order, representing Castor and Pollux; and it is marked thus, ♊. The stars in the sign Gemini, in Ptolemy's catalogue, are 25; in Tycho's, 25; in Hevelius's, 38; in the Britannic Catalogue, 85.
