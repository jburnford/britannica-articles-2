FREDERICK

article · 322 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FREDERICK, is the name of two counties, and of several townships in America, such as the county of Frederick in Maryland, which contains 30,791 inhabitants, in which are included 3641 slaves. It is also the name of a county in Virginia, 30 miles long and 20 broad, with a population of 19,681 souls, including 4250 slaves.
