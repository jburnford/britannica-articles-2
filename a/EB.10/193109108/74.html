FORNACALIA

article · 196 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FORNACALIA, or FORNICALIA, in Roman antiquity, a festival instituted by Numa, in honour of Fornax, the goddess of ovens; wherein certain cakes were made, and offered in sacrifice before the ovens.
