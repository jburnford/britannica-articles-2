FRIGID

article · 200 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRIGID (frigidus), in a general sense, denotes the quality of being cold. It is frequently applied to a jejune style, that is unanimated by any ornaments, and consequently without any force or vigour.
