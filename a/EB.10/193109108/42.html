FORGE for red-hot Balls

sub_entry · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FORGE for red-hot Balls, is a place where the balls are made red hot before they are fired off: it is built about five or six feet below the surface of the ground, of strong brick-work, and an iron grate, upon which the balls are laid, with a large fire under them.
