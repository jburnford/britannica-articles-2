FRETTS

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRETTS, in Mineralogy, a term used by our miners to express the worn side of the banks of the rivers in mine countries, where they search for the shod stones or grews washed down from the hills, in order from thence to trace out the running of the shoad up to the mine.
