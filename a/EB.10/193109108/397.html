FUNDI, in Ancient Geography, a town of Latium, on the Via Appia, near Cajeta; enjoying all the privileges of Roman citizens, except the right of suffrage and of magistracy. Now Fondi; a city of Naples, on the confines of the pope's dominions. E. Long. 14. 20. N. Lat. 41. 35.
