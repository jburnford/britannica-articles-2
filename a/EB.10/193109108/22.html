FOREMAST

article · 284 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FOREMAST of a SHIP, a large round piece of Foremast, Forst. timber, placed in her fore part or fore-castle, and carrying the fore-sail and fore-top-sail yards. Its length is usually \frac{5}{8} of the main-mast, and the fore-top-gallant-mast is \frac{1}{4} the length of the fore-top.
