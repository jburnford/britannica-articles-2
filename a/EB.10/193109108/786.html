GENUS

Cross-references: LOGIC

GENUS, among metaphysicians and logicians, denotes a number of beings which agree in certain general properties common to them all: so that a genus is nothing else but an abstract idea, expressed by some general name or term. See LOGIC and METAPHYSICS.
