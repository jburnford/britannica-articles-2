FUSANUS

article · 217 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FUSANUS, in Botany, a genus of plants belonging to the polygamia class. The hermaphrodite calyx is quinquefid; there is no corolla; there are four stamens; the germen beneath; there are four stigmas; the fruit a plum.
