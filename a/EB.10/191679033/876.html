BUNTINGFORD, a town of Hertfordshire, with a market on Mondays, and two fairs, on June 29th, and November 30th, for pedlars wares. It is a good VOL. IV. Part II.

thoroughfare town, but small, and is accounted only a large hamlet. W. Long. o. 6. N. Lat. 51. 55.
