BRABEUTES

article · 309 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRABEUTES, or BRABEUTA, in antiquity, an officer among the Greeks, who presided at the public games, and decided controversies that happened among the antagonists in the gymnastical exercises. The number of brabeutes was not fixed; sometimes there was only one, but more commonly they amounted to nine or ten.
