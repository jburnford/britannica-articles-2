BOYER

article · 219 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOYER, in Navigation, a kind of Flemish sloop, or small vessel of burden, having a boltsprit, a castle at each end, and a tall mast; chiefly fit for the navigation of rivers, and in many of its parts resembling a smack.
