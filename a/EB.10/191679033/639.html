Foster-BROTHERS

sub_entry · 223 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Foster-BROTHERS, those who sucked the same nurse. The French call them fratres du lait, or brothers by milk; which is most properly used in respect of a person who sucked a nurse at the same time with the nurse's own child.
