BRODERA, or BRODRA, a town of Asia, in the empire of the Great Mogul. It stands in a large sandy plain, on the little river Wasset; and is fortified, after the old way, with pretty good walls and towers. It is inhabited by Banians and callico-weavers. The country about it produces plenty of gum-lac and indigo. E. Long. 72. 30. N. Lat. 22. 10.
