BRASSICAVIT

article · 196 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRASSICAVIT, or BRACHICAVIT, in the manège, is a horse whose forelegs are naturally bended archwise: being so called by way of distinction from an arched horse whose legs are bowed by hard labour.
