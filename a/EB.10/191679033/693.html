BRUNSBUTTL

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRUNSBUTTL, a sea-port town of Germany, in the circle of Lower Saxony, and duchy of Holstein, seated at the mouth of the river Elbe, in E. Long. 8. 42. N. Lat. 44. 30. It is subject to Denmark.
