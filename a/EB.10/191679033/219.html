BOWYERS, artificers whose business is to make bows: in which sense, bowyers stand distinguished from fletchers, who made arrows.

The bowyers company in London was incorporated in 1620: and consists of a master, two wardens, twelve assistants, and 30 on the livery. See ARCHERY.
