BREMEN-Verd

BREMEN-Verd, a town of Germany, in the circle of Lower Saxony, and duchy of Bremen. It is an open town, seated on the river Oost, and was formerly the place of residence of the archbishop. E. Long. 8. 35. N. Lat. 53. 58.
