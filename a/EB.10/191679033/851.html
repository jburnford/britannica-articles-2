BULL'S EYE

article · 173 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BULL'S EYE, among seamen, a small, obscure, sub-lime cloud, ruddy in the middle, that sometimes appears to mariners, and is the immediate forerunner of a great storm at sea.
