BRESCIA

BRESCIA, a strong and handsome town of Italy, with a bishop's see and good citadel. It is the capital of Bresciano in the territory of Venice, and is seated in an agreeable plain on the river Garza, in E. Long. 10. 5. N. Lat. 45. 31.
