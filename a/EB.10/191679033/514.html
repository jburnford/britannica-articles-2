BRIESCIA

article · 567 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRIESCIA, a palatinate in the duchy of Lithuania, in Poland. The name given to it by some is Polefia. It is bounded on the north by Novogrode, and Troki; on the west, by those of Bielsko and Lublin; on the south by that of Chelm and Upper Volhnia; and on the east, by the territory of Rziczica. This province is of considerable extent from east to west, and is watered by the rivers Bug and Pripefe: it is full of woods and marshes; and there are lakes that yield large quantities of fish, that are salted by the inhabitants, and sent into the neighbouring provinces.
