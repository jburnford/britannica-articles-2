BREAST-Plate

sub_entry · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BREAST-Plate, in the manège, the strap of leather that runs from one side of the saddle to the other, over the horse's breast, in order to keep the saddle tight, and hinder it from sliding backwards.
