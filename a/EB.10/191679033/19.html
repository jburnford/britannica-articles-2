BORAK

article · 435 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BORAK, among Mahometans, a fabulous animal, supposed to be of the middle kind between an ass and a mule, whereon their prophet was carried in his nocturnal flight from Jerusalem into the heavens. This animal the Arabians call Al Borak, q. d. shining. The night when the journey was performed is called Lailat al Meeraga, i. e. the night of ascension; and the flight itself Al Mevra; concerning which there is a multitude of traditions.
