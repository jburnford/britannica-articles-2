BROWSE

article · 274 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BROWSE, the tops of the branches of trees, whereon beasts feed. This is sometimes also called brouce and brutle; probably from the French brou, which signifies the same thing.

BROWSE more properly denotes the food which deer find in young coples, continually sprouting anew.
