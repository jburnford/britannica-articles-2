BOTTOMY. A cross bottomy, in Heraldry, terminates.

nates at each end in three buds, knots, or buttons, resembling, in some measure, the three-leaved grass; on which account Segoin, in his Tresor Heraldique, terms it croix treffée. It is the badge of the order of St Maurice. See HERALDRY Plates.
