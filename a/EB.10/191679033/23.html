BORBORITES

article · 290 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BORBORITES, in church history, a sect of Gnostics, in the second century, who, besides embracing the errors of these heretics, denied the last judgment. Their name comes from the Greek Borboros, "filth;" on account of a custom they had of daubing their faces and bodies with dirt and filth.
