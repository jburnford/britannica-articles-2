BUNIAS, in Botany, a genus of the 39th natural order, Siliquosae, belonging to the tetradynamia class of plants, for which there is no English name. The siliclea is deciduous, four-sided, muricated, or flagreened with unequal pointed angles. There are eight species; all of them annual plants, but none of them possessed of any remarkable property.
