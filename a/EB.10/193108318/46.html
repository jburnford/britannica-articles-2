ASTORGA

article · 304 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASTORGA, a very ancient city of Spain, in the kingdom of Leon, with a bishop's see, is seated on the river Tuerta, and well fortified both by art and nature. It stands in a most agreeable plain, about 150 miles north-west of Madrid. There are excellent trout in the river. W. Long. 6. 20. N. Lat. 42. 20.
