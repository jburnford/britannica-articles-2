Military BLOW

sub_entry · 403 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Military BLOW, ulapa militaris, that given with a sword on the neck or shoulder of a candidate for knighthood, in the ceremony of dubbing him. The custom seems to have taken its rise from the ancient ce-

remony of manumission. In giving the blow, the prince used the formula Ego bonus miles, "Be a valiant soldier;" upon which the party rose a complete knight, and qualified to bear arms in his own right.
