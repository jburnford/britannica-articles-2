ASTIGI, in Ancient Geography, a colony, and conventus juridicus, of Baetica, furnished Augusta Firma, situated on the Singulus, which falls into the Baetis; called also Colonia Astigitana (Pliny): Now Ecyca, midway between Seville and Cordova. W. Long. 5°. N. Lat. 37. 20.
