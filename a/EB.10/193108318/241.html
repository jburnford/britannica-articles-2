AUDITORIUM

article · 382 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUDITORIUM, in the ancient churches, was that part of the church where the audientes stood to hear and be instructed.

The auditorium was that part now called navis ecclesie. In the primitive times, the church was so strict in keeping the people together in that place, that the person who went from thence in sermon-time, was ordered by the council of Carthage to be excommunicated.
