BASINGSTOKE

article · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BASINGSTOKE, a corporation town of Hampshire in England, and a great thoroughfare on the western road. It is seated on a small brook, in W. Long. 1. 10. N. Lat. 51. 20.
