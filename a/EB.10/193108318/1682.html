Nuptial BENEDICTION

sub_entry · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Nuptial BENEDICTION, the external ceremony performed by the priest in the office of matrimony. This is also called sacerdotal and matrimonial benediction, by the Greeks ἱεροσύνη and νυμφία. The nuptial benediction is not essential to, but the confirmation of, a marriage in the civil law.
