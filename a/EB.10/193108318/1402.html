BEARDED

article · 430 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BEARDED, denotes a person or thing with a beard, or some resemblance thereof. The faces on ancient Greek and Roman medals are generally bearded. Some are denominated pogonati, as having long beards, e. g. the Parthian kings. Others have only a lanugo about the chin, as the Seleucid family. Adrian was the first of the Roman emperors who nourished his beard; hence all imperial medals before him are beardless; after him, bearded.
