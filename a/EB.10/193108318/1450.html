BEAUJOLOIS

article · 300 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BEAUJOLOIS, a district of France, now included in the department of Rhone and Loire, is bounded on the south by Lyons Proper, on the west by Forez, on the north by Burgundy, and on the east by the principality of Dombes. It is 25 miles in length, and 20 in breadth: Ville Franche is the capital town.
