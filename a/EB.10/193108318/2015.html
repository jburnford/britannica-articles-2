BILLET

article · 317 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BILLET, in Heraldry, a bearing in form of a long square. They are supposed to represent pieces of cloth of gold or silver; but Guillelmus thinks they represent a letter sealed up, and other authors take them for bricks. Billet signifies that the escheveon is all over strewed with billets, the number not ascertained.
