ATLANTIDES

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ATLANTIDES, in Astronomy, a denomination given to the Pleiades, or seven stars, sometimes also called Vergilia. They are thus called, as being supposed by the poets to have been the daughters either of Atlas or his brother Hesperus, who were translated into heaven.
