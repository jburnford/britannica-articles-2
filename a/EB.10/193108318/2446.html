BOCCARELLA

article · 233 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOCCARELLA, in the glass-manufacture, a small hole or aperture of the furnace, one of which is placed

on each side of the Bocca, almost horizontally with it. Out of them the servitors take coloured or finer metal from the piling pot.
