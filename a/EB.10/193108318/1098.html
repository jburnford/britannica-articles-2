BARRING A VEIN

article · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARRING A VEIN, in Farrery, an operation performed upon the veins of a horse's legs, and other parts of his body, with intent to stop the course, and lessen the quantity, of the malignant humours that prevail there.
