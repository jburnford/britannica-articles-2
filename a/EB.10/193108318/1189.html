BASQUES

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BASQUES, a small territory of France, towards the Pyrenean mountains. It comprehends Labourd, Lower Navarre, and the district of Soule, which, with Bearn, form the department of the Lower Pyrenees.
