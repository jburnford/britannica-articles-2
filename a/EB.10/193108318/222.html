AUBIGNE, a town of France, in the department

of Cher, situated on the river Verre, in a flat and agreeable country. It is surrounded with high strong walls, wide ditches, and high counterscarps. The castle is within the town, and is very handsome. E. Long. 2. 20. N. Lat. 47. 29.
