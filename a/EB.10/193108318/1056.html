BARNARD Castle

sub_entry · 219 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARNARD Castle, seated on the river Tees in the county of Durham, is a town and barony belonging to Vane earl of Darlington. It is indifferently large, and has a manufactory of stockings. W. Long. 1. 45. N. Lat. 54. 35.
