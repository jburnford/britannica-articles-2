BANDERET

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BANDERET, a general, or one of the commanders in chief of the forces.—This appellation is given to the principal commanders of the troops of the canton of Bern in Switzerland, where there are four banderets, who command all the forces of that canton.
