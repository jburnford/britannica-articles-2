BESOZZI

article · 473 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BESOZZI, or BEZUTUS, AMBROGIO, a painter of considerable eminence, was born at Milan in 1648. He worked some time under Gioffo Danedi, called Montalti: he afterwards went to Rome, where he studied from the antiques and the pictures of the greatest masters; and at last perfected himself in the school of Ciro Ferri. His great excellency consisted in painting architecture, friezes, imitations of bas-relieves, and other decorations. He died at Milan in 1706, aged 58 years.
