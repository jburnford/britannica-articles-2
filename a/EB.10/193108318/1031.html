BARKHAMSTEAD

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARKHAMSTEAD, or BERKHAMSTEAD, a town of Hertfordshire in England, seems to have been the site of a Roman town. It had formerly a strong castle built by the Normans, but it has long since been demolished. W. Long. o. 35. N. Lat. 45. 49.
