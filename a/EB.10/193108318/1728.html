BERCHEROIT

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BERCHEROIT, or BERKOITS, a weight used at Archangel, and in all the Russian dominions, to weigh such merchandises as are heavy and bulky. It weighs about 364lb. English avoirdupois weight.
