AUTHENTIC

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUTHENTIC, something of acknowledged and received authority. In law, it signifies something clothed in all its formalities, and attested by persons to whom credit has been regularly given. Thus we say, authentic papers, authentic instruments.
