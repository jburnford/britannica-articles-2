BOLTER

article · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOLTER, or BOULTER, a kind of sieves for meal, having the bottoms made of woollen, hair, or even wire. The bakers use bolters which are worked by the hand; millers have a larger sort, wrought by the motion of the mill.
