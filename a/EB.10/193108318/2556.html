BOLT-Rope

sub_entry · 271 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOLT-Rope, in naval affairs, a rope passing round the sail, to which the edges of it are sewed, to prevent the sail from tearing: the bottom part of it is called the foot-rope; the side leeches; and if the sail be oblong or square, the upper part is called the head-rope.
