BETUE

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BETUE, or BETAW, a territory of the Low Countries in the duchy of Guelderland, between the rivers Rhine and Leck. The ground is very moist, and the rains often render the roads impassable. It is divided into the Upper and Lower.
