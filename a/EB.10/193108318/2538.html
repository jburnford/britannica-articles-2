BOLLARDS

article · 223 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOLLARDS, large posts set into the ground on each side of a dock. On docking or undocking ships, large blocks are lashed to them; and through these blocks are received the transporting hawsers to be brought to the captains.
