BALLATOONS

article · 213 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BALLATOONS, large heavy luggage-boats used for carrying wood by the river from Afracan and the Caspian sea to Moscow. These will carry from 100 to 200 tons, and have from 100 to 120 men employed to row them along.
