AUGRE

article · 180 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUGRE, or AWGRE, an instrument used by carpenters and joiners to bore large round holes; and consisting of a wooden handle, and an iron blade terminated at bottom with a steel bit.
