AUBAGNE

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUBAGNE, a town of Provence in France, situated on the river Veauve, on the road from Marseilles to Toulon. The states formerly held their session at this place. E. Long. 5. 52. N. Lat. 43. 17.
