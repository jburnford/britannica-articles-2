AZEKA

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AZEKA, in Ancient Geography, a city of the Amorites, in the lot of Judah; situated between Eleuthero-polis and Zelia (Jerome); where the five kings of the Amorites and their army were destroyed by hailstones from heaven, (Joshua).
