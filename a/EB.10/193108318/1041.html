BARLET-Water

sub_entry · 252 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARLET-Water, is a decoction of either of these, reputed soft and lubricating, of frequent use in physic. This well known decoction is a very useful drink in many disorders; and is recommended, with nitre, by some authors of reputation, in flow fevers.
