BELLUNO

BELLUNO, a town of Italy, in the Venetian territories, and capital of the Bellunese. It is a bishop's see; and is situated among the Alps, on the river Piave, between the towns Cadora and Trevign, in E. Long. 12. 15. N. Lat. 46. 9.
