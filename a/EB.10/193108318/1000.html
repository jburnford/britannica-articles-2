BARDARIOTÆ

article · 323 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARDARIOTÆ, in antiquity, were a kind of ancient guard attending the Greek emperors, armed with rods, wherewith they kept off the people from crowding too near the prince when on horseback. Their captain, or commander, was denominated primivergius.—The word was probably formed from the bardie, or housings on their horses.
