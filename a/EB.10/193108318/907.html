Solemn BAPTISM

sub_entry · 178 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Solemn BAPTISM, that conferred at stated seasons; such, in the ancient church, were the Paschal baptism,

and that at Whit-sun-tide. This is sometimes also called general baptism.
