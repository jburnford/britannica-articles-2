BLACK-Leather

sub_entry · 229 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: LEATHER

BLACK-Leather, is that which has passed the curriers hands, where, from the ruff as it was left by the tanners, it is become black, by having been scoured and rubbed three times on the grain-side with copperas water. See LEATHER.
