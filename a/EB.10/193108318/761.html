BALL and Socket

sub_entry · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BALL and Socket, an instrument made of brass, with a perpetual screw, so as to move horizontally, vertically, and obliquely; and is generally used for the managing of surveying or astronomical instruments.
