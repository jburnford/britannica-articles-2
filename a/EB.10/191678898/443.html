EROTIC

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EROTIC (derived from 1205, "love;") whence 1205-7005), is applied to any thing which has a relation to the passion of love.

In medicine we find the phrase delirium eroticum used for a kind of melancholy contracted through excess of love.
