FINE

article · 213 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FINE, sometimes signifies a sum of money paid for entering lands or tenements let by lease; and sometimes a pecuniary mulct for an offence committed against the king and his laws, or against the lord of the manor.
