EYE of a Block

sub_entry · 304 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EYE of a Block, in naval affairs, that part of the rope-strap which is fastened to some necessary place in the ship: the strap is a sort of wreath or rope formed into a ring, and fixed round the block for the double convenience of strengthening the block and fastening it in any place where it is wanted.
