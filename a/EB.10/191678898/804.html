EXTERMINATION

EXTERMINATION, in general, the extirpating or destroying something.

EXTERMINATION or EXTERMINATING, in Algebra, is used for taking away. Thus algebraists speak of exterminating surds, fractions, and unknown quantities out of equations. See MacLaur. Algebr. Part I. Chap. 12. where we have some general theorems for the exterminating unknown quantities in given equations.
