FOLLICULUS

article · 260 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FOLLICULUS, (from follis, "a bag,") a species of feed-vessel first mentioned by Linnaeus in his De flava Plantarum, generally consisting of one valve, which opens from bottom to top on one side, and has no future for fastening or attaching the seeds within it.
