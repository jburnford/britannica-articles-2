Fichant FLANK

sub_entry · 356 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Fichant FLANK, is that from whence a cannon playing, fires directly on the face of the opposite bastion.

Rasant or Razzant FLANK, is the point from whence the line of defence begins, from the conjunction of which with the curtain, the shot only rasset the face of the next bastion, which happens when the face cannot be discovered but from the flank alone.
