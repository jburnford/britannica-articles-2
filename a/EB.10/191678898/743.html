EXODUS, a canonical book of the Old Testament; being the second of the Pentateuch, or five books of Moses.

It is so called from the Greek ἐξόδος, the "going out" or departure of the children of Israel from the land of Egypt; the history of which is delivered in this book, together with the many miracles wrought on that occasion.
