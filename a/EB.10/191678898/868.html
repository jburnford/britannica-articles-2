FABRIC Lands

sub_entry · 216 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FABRIC Lands, those formerly given towards rebuilding and repairing of cathedrals and other churches; for anciently almost every body gave more or less, by his will, to the fabric of the parish church where he dwelt.
