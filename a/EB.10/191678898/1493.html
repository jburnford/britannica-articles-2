FLY-Boat

sub_entry · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FLY-Boat, or Flight, a large flat-bottomed Dutch vessel, whose burden is generally from 600 to 1200 tons. It is distinguished by a very high stern, resembling a Gothic turret, and by very broad buttocks below.
