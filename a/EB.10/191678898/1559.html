FONTICULUS

article · 176 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FONTICULUS, or FONTANELLA, in Surgery, an issue, seton, or small ulcer, made in various parts of the body, in order to excite irritation, or to produce the discharge of matter.
