FLOOKING

article · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FLOOKING, among miners, a term used to express a peculiarity in the load of a mine. The load or quantity of ore is frequently intercepted in its course

by the crossing of a vein of earth or stone, or some different metallic substance; in which case the load is moved to one side, and this transient part of the load is called a flocking.
