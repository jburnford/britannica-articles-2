EUBAGES

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EUBAGES, an order of priests or philosophers among the ancient Celtae or Gauls: some will have the eubages to be the same with the druids and faronidæ of Diiodorus; and others, that they were the same with what Strabo calls EVATES.
