FLAGEOLET

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FLAGEOLET, or FLAGEOLET, a little flute, used chiefly by shepherds and country people. It is made of box or other hard wood, and sometimes of ivory; and has six holes besides that at the bottom, the mouth-piece, and that behind the neck.
