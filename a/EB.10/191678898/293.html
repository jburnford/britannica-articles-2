EPIDENDRUM

EPIDENDRUM, a genus of plants, belonging to the gynandria class; and in the natural method ranking under the seventh order, Orchidææ. See BOTANY Index.—This is the plant which produces the fruit called vanilla, and which is used in perfuming chocolate, tobacco, and snuff.
