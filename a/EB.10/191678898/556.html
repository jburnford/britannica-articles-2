EUCRASY

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EUCRASY, (of eu, well, and krasis, temperature), in Medicine, an agreeable well-proportioned mixture of qualities, whereby a body is said to be in good order, and disposed for a good state of health.
