FANUM

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FANUM, among the Romans, a temple or place consecrated to some deity. The deified men and women among the heathens had likewise their fana; even the great philosopher Cicero erected one to his daughter Tullia.
