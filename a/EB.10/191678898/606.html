EUPHORION of CHALCIS, a poet and historian, born in the 126th Olympiad. Suetonius says that Tibertius composed verses in imitation of Euphorion, Rianius and Parthenius; with whom he was charmed to such a degree, that he ordered their writings and their pictures to be kept in all the public libraries, among the ancient and celebrated authors.
