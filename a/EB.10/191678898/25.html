ELEPHANTINE

article · 398 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ELEPHANTINE, in Roman antiquity, an appellation given to the books wherein were registered the transactions of the senate and magistrates of Rome, of the emperors or generals of armies, and even of the provincial magistrates; the births and classes of the people and other things relating to the census.

They are supposed to have been so called, as being made of leaves of ivory or elephants tusks.
