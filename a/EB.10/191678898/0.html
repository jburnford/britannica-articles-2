ELECTRIDES

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ELECTRIDES, anciently islands in the Adriatic sea, which received their name from the quantity of amber (elestrum) which they produced. They were at the mouth of the Po, according to Apollonius of Rhodes; but some historians doubt of their existence.
