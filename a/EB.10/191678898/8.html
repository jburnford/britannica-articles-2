ELEMOSYNARIUS

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ELEMOSYNARIUS, in our old writers, is used for the almoner or peculiar officer who received the elemosynary rents and gifts, and distributed them to pious and charitable uses. There was such an officer in all religious houses. The bishops also used to have their almoners, as now the king has.
