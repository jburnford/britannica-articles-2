ERECTION

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ERECTION, in a general sense, the art of raising or elevating any thing; as the erection of a perpendicular, &c. It is also used in a figurative sense; as the erection of a bithropic, marquise, &c.
