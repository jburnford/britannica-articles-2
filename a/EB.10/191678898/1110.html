FER de Moulin, Milrinde, Inke de Moulin

sub_entry · 157 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FER de Moulin, Milrinde, Inke de Moulin, in Heraldry, is a bearing supposed to represent the iron ink, or ink of a mill, which sustains the moving millstone.
