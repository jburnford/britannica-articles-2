FASCIA

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FASCIA, in Architecture, signifies any flat member having a considerable breadth and but a small projection, as the band of an architrave, larmier, &c. In brick buildings, the juttings out of the brick beyond the windows in the several stories except the highest are called fascias or fasciae.
