ERGOT

Cross-references: FARRIERY

ERGOT, in Farriery, is a stub, like a piece of soft horn, about the bigness of a chestnut, placed behind and below the pastern joint, and commonly hid under the tuft of the fetlock. See FARRIERY Index.
