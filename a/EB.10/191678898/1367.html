FLAG-Stone

sub_entry · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FLAG-Stone, a kind of sand-stone of a flaty structure, on account of which it is much employed for the purpose of paving foot-paths or the floors of apartments in which wood is unsuitable.
