FLUENT

Cross-references: FLUXIONS

FLUENT, or FLOWING QUANTITY, in the doctrine of fluxions, is the variable quantity which is considered as increasing and decreasing; or the fluent of a given fluxion, is that quantity whose fluxion being taken, according to the rules of that doctrine, shall be the same with the given fluxion.
