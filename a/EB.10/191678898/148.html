EMPORIÆ, a double city of the Hither Spain, near the Pyrenees; separated by a wall; one part occupied by the Greeks of Phocæa, whence originally are the Massilienses; the other, by native Spaniards, to whom was added by Augustus a Roman colony. Now Ampurias, in Catalonia. E. Long. 2. 50. N. Lat. 42. 15.
