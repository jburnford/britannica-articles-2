ARCHON

article · 375 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHON is also applied by some authors to diverse officers, both civil and religious, under the eastern or Greek empire. Thus bishops are sometimes called archontes; and the same may be said of the lords of the emperor's court. We also read of the archons of the antimenstra, archon of archons, grand archon, archon of churches, archon of the gospel, archon of the walls, &c.
