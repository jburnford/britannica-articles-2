ASCODUTÆ

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASCODUTÆ, in antiquity, a sect of heretics in the second century, who rejected all use of symbols and sacraments, on this principle, That incorporeal things cannot be communicated by things corporeal, nor divine mysteries by any thing visible.
