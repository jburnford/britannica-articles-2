AMOEBÆUM/EUM

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMOEB/EUM, in ancient poetry, a kind of poem representing a dispute between two persons, who are made to answer each other alternately; such are the third and seventh of Virgil's eclogues.
