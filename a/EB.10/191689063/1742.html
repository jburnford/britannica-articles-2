ASSITHMENT

article · 164 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASSITHMENT, a wiregold, or composition, by a pecuniary mulct; from the preposition ad, and the Sax. fithe, vice; quod vice supplicii ad expiandum delictum solvitur.
