ARMORIST, a person skilled in the knowledge of armour.

ARMORUM CONCUSSIO; the clashing of arms practised by the Roman armies previous to an engagement, and intended to strike a panic into their enemies; it always followed the classicum and the barritus. See CLASSICUM and BARRITUS.
