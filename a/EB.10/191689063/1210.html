ARCTURUS

article · 355 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCTURUS, in Astronomy, a fixed star, of the first magnitude, in the constellation Arctophylax, or Bootes. The word is formed of ageros, bear, and ura, tail, q. d. bear's tail, as being very near it. This star was known to the ancients, as in the following verse of Virgil:

Arcturum, pluviasque Hyades, geminasque Triones.

See also Job ix. 9. xxxviii. 32.
