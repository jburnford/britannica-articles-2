ANIMATED, or ANIMATE

sub_entry · 153 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANIMATED, or ANIMATE, in a general sense,

denotes something endowed with animal life. It also
imports a thing to be impregnated with vermine or ani-
mules.
