ANAMELECH

article · 292 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANAMELECH, an idol of the Sepharvites, who are said in Scripture to have burned their children in honour of Adramelech and Anamelech.—These idols probably signified the sun and moon. Some of the rabbins represent Anamelech under the figure of a male, others under that of a quail or pheasant.
