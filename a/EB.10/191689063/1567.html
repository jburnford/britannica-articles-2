ARVIL SUPPER

article · 342 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARVIL SUPPER, a feast or entertainment made at funerals, in the north part of England. Arvil bread is the bread delivered to the poor at funeral solemnities; and arvil, arval, arfol, are used for the burial or funeral rites; as,

Come, bring my jerkin, Tibb, I'll to the arvil;
Yon man's dea scuy seoun, it makes me marvel.

Yorksh. Dial. p. 53.
