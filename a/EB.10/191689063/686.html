ANTIOPÉ

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTIOPÉ, in fabulous history, the wife of Licus, king of Thebes, who, being despoiled by Jupiter in the form of a satyr, brought forth Amphion and Zethus.—Another Antiope was queen of the Amazons; and, with the assistance of the Scythians, invaded the Athenians; and was vanquished by Theseus.
