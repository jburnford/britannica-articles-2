ARTEMISIUM, a town of Oenotria, (Stephanus): now
S. Agatha, in the Hither Calabria, on the river Pisaurus,
or la Foglia, distant eight miles from the Tuscan sea.—
Another of the Conestani, in Spain, (Strabo); other-
wise called Dianium: now Denta, on the sea coast of
Valencia.
