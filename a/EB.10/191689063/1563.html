ARUCI, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Celtici, in the north of Lusitania, (Antonine, Inscription), called also Aruci Novum, to distinguish it from the following: Now supposed to be Moura, a small city of Portugal, near the confluence of the Ardia and Guadalquivir.
