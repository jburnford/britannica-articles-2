ASCII

article · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASCII, among Geographers, an appellation given to those inhabitants of the earth, who at certain seasons of the year have no shadow: Such are all the inhabitants of the torrid zone, when the sun is vertical to them.
