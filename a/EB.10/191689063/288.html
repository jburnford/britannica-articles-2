ANCUS MARTIUS

article · 319 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANCUS MARTIUS, the fourth king of the Romans, succeeded Tullus Hostilius, 639 years before Christ. He defeated the Latins, subdued the Fidenates, conquered the Sabines, Volsci, and Veientes, enlarged Rome by joining to it Mount Janiculum, and made the harbour of Ostia. He died about 615 years before the Christian era.
