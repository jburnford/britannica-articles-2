ARROW-Makers

sub_entry · 292 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARROW-Makers, are called fletchers; and were formerly, as well as bowyers, persons of great consequence in the commonwealth.

Arrow-heads and quarrels were to be well boched or brafed, and hardened at the points with steel; the doing of which seems to have been the business of the arrowsmith.
