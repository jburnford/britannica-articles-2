ASCESIS

article · 381 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASCESIS is also used by philosophers, to denote an exercise conducive to virtue, or to the acquiring a greater degree of virtue. This is particularly denominated the philosophical ascesis, because practised chiefly by philosophers, who make a more peculiar profession of improving themselves in virtue; on the model whereof the ancient Christians introduced a religious asceticism.
