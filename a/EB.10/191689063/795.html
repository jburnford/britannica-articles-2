APENRADE

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

APENRADE, a town of Denmark in the duchy of Sleswick, seated at the bottom of a gulf in the Baltic sea, between Flensburg and Hadschieben. It is 25 miles north from Sleswick. E. Long. 9. 28. N. Lat. 55. 4.
