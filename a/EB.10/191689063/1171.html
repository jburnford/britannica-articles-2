ARCHIATER, ARCHIATRUS

article · 172 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHIATER, ARCHIATRUS, properly denotes chief physician of a prince who retains several. The word is formed of αρχή, principium, "chief;" and ατρος, medicus, a "physician."
