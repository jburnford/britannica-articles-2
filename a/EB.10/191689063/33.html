AMMA

article · 298 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMMA, among ecclesiastical writers, a term used to denote an abbess or spiritual mother.

AMMAN or AMMANT, in the German and Belgic polity, a judge who has the cognizance of civil causes.

Ammania — It is also used among the French for a public notary, or officer, who draws up instruments and deeds.
