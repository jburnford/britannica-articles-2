AROMATA

AROMATA, in Ancient Geography, a town of Lydia, famous for its generous wines; and hence the appellation, (Strabo). Also the name of a trading town and promontory of Ethiopia, at the termination of the Sinus Avalites of the Red sea, (Arrian.)
