AQUILO

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AQUILO, is used by Vitruvius for the north-east wind; or that which blows at 45° from the north toward the east point of the horizon.—The poets gave the name aquilo to all stormy winds dreaded by the mariner.
