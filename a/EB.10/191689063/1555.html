ARTOBRIGA, a town of Vindelicia (Ptolemy): Now Altsburg, in Bavaria, on the Danube, below Ingolstadt (Aventinus): but Cluverius supposes it to be Lebanus, on the Salzach, below Laufen, in the archbishopric of Salzburg.
