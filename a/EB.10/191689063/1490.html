ARRIERE Ban

sub_entry · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARRIERE Ban, in the French customs, was a general proclamation, whereby the king summoned to war all that held of him, both his vassals, i. e. the noblesse, and the vassals of his vassals.
