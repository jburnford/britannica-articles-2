ARCHITECT

article · 281 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHITECT, a person skilled in architecture, or the art of building; who forms plans and designs for edifices, conducts the work, and directs the several artificers employed in it. The word is derived from αρχις, princeps, and τεκτιν, faber, "workman;" q. d. the principal workman.
