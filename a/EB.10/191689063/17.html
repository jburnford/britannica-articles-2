AMICABLE Benchers

sub_entry · 247 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMICABLE Benchers, in Roman antiquity, were, according to Pitiscus, lower and less honourable seats allotted for the judices pedanei, or inferior judges, who upon being admitted of the emperor's council, were dignified by him with the title amici.
