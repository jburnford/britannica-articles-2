AQUA Extingua

sub_entry · 233 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AQUA Extingua, or Extinguished Water, is aquafortis into which some river water has been poured, in order to qualify it, and render it less corrosive. Its use is to get the silver from the aquafortis that served to part gold from it.
