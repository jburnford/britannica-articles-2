AMHAR, or AMHARA, a province of Abyssinia, said to extend 40 leagues from east to west. It is considered as the most noble in the whole empire, both on account of its being the usual residence of the A-

byssinian monarchs, and having a particular dialect different from all the rest, which, by reason of the emperors being brought up in the province, is become the language of the court and of the politer people. Here is the famed rock Amba-gelhen, where the young monarchs were formerly confined. See AMBA and ABYS-SINIA.
