ANNALIS GLAVUS

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANNALIS GLAVUS, the nail which the prætor, consul, or dictator, drove into the wall of Jupiter's temple annually upon the ides of September, to show the number of years. But this custom was superseded by reckoning years by consuls. The ceremony was sometimes performed to avert the plague, &c.
