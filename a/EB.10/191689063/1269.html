ARGENTATIMILITES

article · 294 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARGENTATIMILITES, in antiquity. Livy, lib. vi. speaks of argentati milites, as distinguished from aurati. Aquinas supposes these to have been similar to the argyralpides and chrysaspides; but the descriptions do not quadruple. Livy only represents the argentati as clothed in white linen coats.
