ANTANDROS

article · 330 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTANDROS, in Ancient Geography, a town of Myria, on the sea-coast, at the foot of Mount Alexandria, a part of Mount Ida, (Strabo, Ptolemy): it was a town of the Leleges, (Strabo); anciently called Edonis, then, Cimmeris, (Pliny, Stephanus). It takes its name from Antandros, a general of the Aolians: it is now called S. Dimitri.
