ANN

article · 204 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANN, or ANNAT, in Scotts Law, is half a year's stipend, which the law gives to the executors of ministers of the church of Scotland, over and above what was due to the minister himself for his incumbency.
