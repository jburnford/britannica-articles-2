AOSTRA

article · 262 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AOSTRA, a territory of Piedmont, with the title of a duchy. It is a valley 30 miles in length, and extends from the pass of St Martin's, near the frontiers of Yvree, to St Bernard. It abounds in pastures, and all sorts of fruits. The capital is of the same name.
