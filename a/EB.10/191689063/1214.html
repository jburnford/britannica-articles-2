ARDASSES

article · 247 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARDASSES, in Commerce, the coarsest of all the silks of Persia; and as it were the refuse of each kind. In this sense, they say, the legis, the houfets, the choufe, and the payas ardasses, to signify the worst of those four sorts of Persian silks.
