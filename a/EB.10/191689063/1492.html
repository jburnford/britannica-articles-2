ARROBAS

article · 252 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARROBAS, or AROBAS, a weight used in Spain, Portugal, and the foreign dominions of both. The arrobas of Portugal is also called arata, and contains thirty-two Lisbon pounds; that of Spain contains twenty-five Spanish pounds. In Peru it is called arufe.
