ANDECAVI, (Tacitus); ANDEGAVI, (Pliny); ANDES, (Cæsar); ANDI, (Lucan): A people of Gallia Celtica, having the Turones to the east, the Namnetes to the west, the Pictones to the south, and the Aulerici Crenomani to the north: now Anjou.
