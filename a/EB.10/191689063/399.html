ANGON

article · 321 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANGON, in the ancient military art, a kind of javelin used by the French. They darted it at a considerable distance. The iron head of this weapon resembled a fleur-de-luce. It is the opinion of some writ-

ers, that the arms of France are not fleurs-de-luce, but the iron point of the angon or javelin of the ancient French.
