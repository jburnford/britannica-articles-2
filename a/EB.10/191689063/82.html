AMPHILOCHIA, in Ancient Geography, the territory of the city of Argos in Acarnania: Amphilo-chium, (Thucydides); called Amphilochi (from the people), in the lower age, (Stephanus.) A town also of Spain, in Galicia, built by Teucer, and denominated

nated from Amphilochus one of his companions, (Strabo): now Orense. W. Long. 8. 20. Lat. 42. 36.
