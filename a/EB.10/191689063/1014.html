AQUÆ Augustæ

AQUÆ Augustæ (Ptolemy); Aquæ Tarbellicæ (Antonine); Aquæ Civiæ, in the Notitia. Now Acqs, or Dax, a town in Gascony, on the river Adour, famous for its baths. W. Long. 1. 40. N. Lat. 43. 56.
