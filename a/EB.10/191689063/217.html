ANAPHORA

article · 200 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANAPHORA, in Rhetoric, the repetition of the same word or words in the beginning of a sentence or verse: Thus Virgil,

Pan etiam Arcadia mecum se judice certet,
Pan etiam Arcadia dicat se judice victum.
