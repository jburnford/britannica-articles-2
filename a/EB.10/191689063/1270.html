ARGENTEUIL

article · 299 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARGENTEUIL, a town of the Isle of France, seated on the river Seine, five miles north-west of Paris. It is a very beautiful place, with fine vineyards. In the environs are quarries of flucco. In the Benedictine priory they pretend to have the seamless coat of Christ. E. Long. 2. 28. N. Lat. 48. 52.
