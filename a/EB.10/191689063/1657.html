ASINIUS POLLIO

article · 278 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASINIUS POLLIO, a Roman consul and orator, distinguished himself under Augustus by his exploits and his literary works. He is frequently mentioned with praises by Horace and Virgil, and is said to have collected the first library at Rome. He died at Fregene, at 80 years of age.
