ANTACIDS

article · 287 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTACIDS, in Pharmacy, an appellation given to all medicines proper to correct acid or sour humours. Under the class of Antacids come, 1. Absorbents: as chalk, coral, sea-shells, haematites, and steel filings. 2. Obtundents; as oils and fats. 3. Immuntants; as lixivious salts and soaps.
