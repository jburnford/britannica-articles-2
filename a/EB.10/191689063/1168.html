ARCHETYPE

article · 286 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHETYPE, the first model of a work, which is copied after to make another like it. Among minters, it is used for the standard weight by which the others are adjusted. The archetypal world, among Platonists, means the world as it existed in the idea of God before the visible creation.
