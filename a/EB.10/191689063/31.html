AMITERNUM

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMITERNUM, a town of the Sabines, in Italy (Livy, Pliny); now extinct. The ruins are to be seen on the level ridge of a mountain, near St Vittorino, and the springs of the Aternus; not far from Aquila, which rose out of the ruins of Amiternum.
