AMNESTY

article · 334 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMNESTY, in matters of policy, denotes a pardon granted by a prince to his rebellious subjects, usually with some exceptions; such was that granted by Charles II. at his restoration.—The word is formed from the Greek αμνηστια, the name of an edict of this kind published by Thrasylbulus, on his expulsion of the tyrants out of Athens.
