MEDULLA OSSIIUM, or MARROW of the bones. See ANATOMY, No 5.

MEDULLA cerebrī and cerebrēllī, denotes the white soft part of the brain and cerebellum, covered on the outside with the cortical substance, which is of a more dark or ashy colour. See ANATOMY, No 131—133.
