MERSEY Island

sub_entry · 281 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MERSET Island, an island of Essex, at the mouth of the Coln, south of Colchester. It was seized by the Danes in the reign of King Alfred, for their winter quarters. It had eight parishes, now reduced to two, viz. East and West Mersey. There was formerly a blockhouse on the island.
