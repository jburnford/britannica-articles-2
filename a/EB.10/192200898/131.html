MEDIOLANUM Aulercorum

sub_entry · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MEDIOLANUM Aulercorum, in Ancient Geography, a town of Gallia Celtica, which afterwards took the name of the Ebroicorum Civitas (Antonine); corrupted to Civitas Ebroicorum, and this last to Ebroica; whence the modern appellation Evreux, a city of Normandy. E. Long. 1. 12. N. Lat. 49. 21.
