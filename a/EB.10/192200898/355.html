METASTASIS

Cross-references: ANATOMY

METASTASIS, in Medicine, a transposition or settlement of some humour or disease in some other part; and sometimes it signifies such an alteration of a disease as is succeeded by a solution.

METATARSUS (μετα, beyond, and ταρσος, the tarsus), in anatomy, that part of the human skeleton containing the middle of the foot. See ANATOMY Index.
