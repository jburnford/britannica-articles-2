MAYNOOTH

article · 260 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MAYNOOTH, or MANOOTH a post town in the county of Kildare, and province of Leinster, in Ireland, near 12 miles from Dublin. Though not very large, it is regularly laid out, and consists of good houses. Here is a charter school, which was opened 27th July 1759.
