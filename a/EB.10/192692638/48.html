PASSPORT

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PASSPORT is also a licence obtained for the importing or exporting of merchandizes deemed contraband, and declared such by tariffs, &c. as gold, silver, precious stones, ammunition of war, horses, corn, wool, &c. upon paying duties.
