PAVOR

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PAVOR, a Roman deity, whose worship was introduced by Tullus Hostilius, who, in a panic, vowed a shrine to him, and one to Pallor, Palenesi; and therefore are found on the coins of that family.
