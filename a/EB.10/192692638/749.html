PHRENITIS, the same with PHRENSY; an inflammation of the meninges of the brain, attended with an acute fever and delirium. See MEDICINE, No 176; also an account of a strange degree of phrensy which attacked Charles VI. of France, in the article FRANCE, No 88, 90.
