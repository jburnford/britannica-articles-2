PHARMACOCHÉMIA

article · 287 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PHARMACOCHÉMIA, an old term denoting that part of the chemical art which treats of the preparation of medicines; by way of distinction from that chemistry, which is wholly employed about the transmutation of metals by means of the philosopher's stone; this being called spagirico-chemia.
