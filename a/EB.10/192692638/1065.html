PLEDGE

article · 210 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PLEDGE (Plegius), in common law, a surety or gage, either real or personal, which the plaintiff or demandant is to find for his prosecuting the suit.

The word is sometimes also used for FRANK Pledge, which see.
