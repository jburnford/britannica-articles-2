PERCHE

article · 333 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PERCHE, a territory of Orleans in France, 35 miles long, and 30 broad; bounded on the north by Normandy; on the south, by Maine and Dunois; on the east, by Beauce; and on the west, by Maine. It takes its name from a forest, and is pretty fertile. The inhabitants carry on a pretty good trade; and the principal town is Bellefontaine.
