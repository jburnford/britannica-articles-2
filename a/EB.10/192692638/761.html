PHYLACTERY

article · 200 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PHYLACTERY, in the general, was a name given by the ancients to all kinds of charms, spells, or cha-

acters, which they wore about them, as amulets, to preserve them from dangers or diseases. Phylactery
