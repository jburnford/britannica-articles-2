PHILADELPHUS

article · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PHILADELPHUS, in antiquity, was a title or sur-
name borne by several ancient kings; formed from the
Greek φίλος, "friend, lover," and ἀδελφός, "brother;"
q. d. one who loves his brother or brethren. See
PTOLEMY and EGYPT.
