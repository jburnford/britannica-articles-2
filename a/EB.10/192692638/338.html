PENTANDRIA

Cross-references: BOTANY

PENTANDRIA (from πέντε, five, and ἀνδρ, a man or husband); the name of the fifth class in Linnaeus's sexual method, consisting of plants which have hermaphrodite flowers, with five stamens or male organs. See BOTANY Index.
