PINNACLE

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PINNACLE, in Architecture, the top or roof of a house, terminating in a point. This kind of roof among the ancients was appropriated to temples; their ordinary roofs were all flat, or made in the platform way.
