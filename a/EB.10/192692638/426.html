PERISCII

article · 285 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PERISCII, in Geography, the inhabitants of either frigid zone, between the polar circles and the poles, where the sun, when in the summer signs, moves only round about them, without setting; and consequently their shadows in the same day turn to all the points of the horizon. Perjury.
