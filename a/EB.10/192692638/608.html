PHANUEL

article · 184 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PHANUEL, of the tribe of Asher, the father of a holy widow and prophetess called Anna, who was in the temple when our Saviour was presented there by his parents (Luke ii. 36, 37, 38.).
