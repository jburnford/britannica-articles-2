PERITONÆUM

article · 207 chars · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: ANATOMY

PERITONÆUM, in Anatomy, is a thin, smooth, and lubricous membrane, investing the whole internal surface of the abdomen, and containing most of the viscera of that part as it were in a bag. See ANATOMY Index.
