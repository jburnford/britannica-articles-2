PERIPNEUMONY

PERIPNEUMONY, Περιπνευμονία, formed from per, "about," and πνευμονία, "lungs," in Medicine, an inflammation of some part of the thorax, properly of the lungs; attended with an acute fever, and a difficulty of breathing. See MEDICINE, No 184.
