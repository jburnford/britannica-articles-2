PLENUM

article · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PLENUM, in Physics, denotes, according to the Cartesians, that state of things wherein every part of space is supposed to be full of matter, in opposition to a VACUUM, which is a space supposed devoid of all matter.
