Shell-SILVER

sub_entry · 161 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Shell-SILVER, is prepared of the threads of silver leaf,
or of the leaves themselves, for the use of painters, after
the same manner as shell-gold. See Shell-GOLD.
