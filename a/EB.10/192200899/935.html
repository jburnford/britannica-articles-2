SPAR, in Mineralogy, a name given chiefly to some of the crystallized combinations of lime, as the carbonate and the fluuate; the former being called simply lime spar, the latter fluor spar, or Derbyshire spar, from the name of the place where it is found in greatest abundance. See MINERALOGY.
