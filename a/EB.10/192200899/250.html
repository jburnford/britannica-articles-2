SEQUANI

article · 284 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEQUANI, a people anciently forming a part of Gallia Celtica, but annexed to Belgica by Augustus, separated from the Helvetii by Mount Jura, with the Rhine on the east (Strabo), bordering on the Aedui and Segustiani to the south, and Lingones to the west (Tacitus). Now Franche Comte.
