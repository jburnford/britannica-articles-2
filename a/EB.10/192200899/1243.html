STREWS

article · 383 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STREWS (from the French estuves, i. e. thermae, balneum), those places which were permitted in England to women of professed incontinency; so called, because dissolute persons are wont to prepare themselves for venereous acts by bathing; and hot baths were by Homer reckoned among the effeminate sort of pleasures. These stews were suppressed by King Henry VIII. about the year 1546.
