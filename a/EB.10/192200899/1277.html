STIMULI

article · 259 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STIMULI, in Botany, a species of armature or offensive weapon, with which some plants, as nettle, casti-

da, acalypha, and tragia, are furnished. Their use, says Linneus, is by their venomous punctures to keep off naked animals that would approach to hurt them.
