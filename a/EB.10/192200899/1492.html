SUET, SEBUM

article · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SUET, SEBUM, or Sebum, in Anatomy, the solid fat found in several animals, as sheep, oxen, &c. but not in the human species. See the article FAT.—It is of the sebum that tallow is made.
