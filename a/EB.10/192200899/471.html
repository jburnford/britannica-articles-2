SHIFTERS

article · 198 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SHIFTERS, on board a man of war, certain men who are employed by the cooks to shift and change the water in which the flesh or fish is put, and laid for some time, in order to fit it for the kettle.
