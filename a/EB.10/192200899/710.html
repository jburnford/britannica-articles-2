SLIGO

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SLIGO, the only market town in the county, contains 8000 inhabitants, and enjoys a considerable trade, is seated on a bay of the same name, 30 miles west of Killalla, and 110 north-east of Dublin. W. Long. 8. 26. N. Lat. 54. 13.
