To SHOE an Anchor

sub_entry · 258 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

To SHOE an Anchor, is to cover the flukes with a broad

broad triangular piece of plank, whose area or superficies is much larger than that of the flukes. It is intended to give the anchor a stronger and firmer hold of the bottom in very soft and oozy ground.
