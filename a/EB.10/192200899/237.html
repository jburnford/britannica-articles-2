SEPTARIÆ

article · 238 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEPTARIÆ, in Natural History, an old term for a variety of iron-stone, called also ludus Helmontii. This mineral is of a round compressed form, and is internally divided by septa or thin partitions of lime spar or pyrites; hence the name.
