STEMMATA

article · 204 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STEMMATA, in the history of insects, are three smooth hemispheric dots, placed generally on the top of the head, as in most of the hymenoptera and other classes. The name was first introduced by Linnaeus.
