SEMI-FLOSCULUS

article · 211 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEMI-FLOSCULUS, in Botany, a term used to express the flowers of the syngenesia class. These semi-flosculi are petals, hollow in their lower part, but in their upper flat, and continued in the shape of a tongue.
