SEXAGENARY

article · 455 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEXAGENARY, something relating to the number sixty: thus sexagenary or sexagesimal arithmetic is a method of computation proceeding by fixties; such is that used in the division of a degree into sixty minutes, of the minute into sixty seconds, of the second into sixty thirds, &c. Also sexagenary tables are tables of proportional parts, showing the product of two sexagenaries that are to be multiplied, or the quotient of the two that are to be divided.
