SENTICOSÆ

SENTICOSÆ (from sentis, a "briar or bramble"); the name of the 35th order in Linnaeus's fragments of a natural method, consisting of rose, bramble, and other plants, which resemble them in port and external structure. See BOTANY, Natural Method.
