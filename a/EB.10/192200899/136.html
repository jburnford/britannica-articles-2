SEGEBERG

article · 289 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEGEBERG, a town of Germany, in the duchy of Holstein, and in Wargia; with a castle standing on a high mountain, consisting of limestone, large quantities of which are carried to Hamburg and Lubeck. It belongs to Denmark, and is seated on the river Treve, in E. Long. 20. 9. N. Lat. 54. 0.
