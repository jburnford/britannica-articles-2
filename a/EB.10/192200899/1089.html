SQUADRON

article · 254 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SQUADRON, in military affairs, denotes a body of horse whose number of men is not fixed; but is usually from 100 to 200.

SQUADRON of Ships, either implies a detachment of ships employed on any particular expedition, or the third part of a naval armament.
