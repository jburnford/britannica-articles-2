SEDGMOOR

article · 159 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEDGMOOR, a large and rich tract of land in Somersetshire, memorable for the defeat of the duke of Monmouth, in 1685. It lies between Somerton and Bridgewater.
