STOURBRIDGE

article · 425 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STOURBRIDGE, or STURBICH, the name of a field near Cambridge, noted for its famous fair kept annually on the 7th of September, and which continues for a fortnight. The commodities are, horses, hops, iron, wool, leather, cheese, &c. This place is also noted

noted for an excellent species of clay capable of resisting an intense heat. It is used in making pots for glass-houses, fire-bricks, &c.; and is sold at an high price.
