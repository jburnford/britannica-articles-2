SIRLET, FLAVIUS

article · 183 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SIRLET, FLAVIUS, an eminent Roman engraver on precious stones: his Laocoön, and representations in miniature of antique statues at Rome, are very valuable and scarce. He died in 1737.
