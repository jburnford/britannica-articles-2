PANNICULUS CARNOSUS

article · 217 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PANNICULUS CARNOSUS, in Comparative Anatomy, a robust fleshy tunic, situated in beasts between the skin and the fat; by means of which they can move their skin in whole or in part. It is altogether wanting in mankind.
