PAREIRA FLAVA

article · 227 chars · page image at NLS ↗

PAREIRA FLAVA, in the Materia Medica, a kind of oblong and large root brought from the Brazils.—It is certainly a diuretic of no mean character, and is said to have done great service in nephritic cases, pleurisy, and quinsies.
