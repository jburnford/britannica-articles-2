OKEHAM, the capital of Rutlandshire, in England, seated in a rich and pleasant valley, called the vale of Catmus. It is well built, contains 1613 inhabitants, has a good church, a free-school, and an hospital. W. Long. o. 45. N. Lat. 52. 40.
