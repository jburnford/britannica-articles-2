NUMERICAL

article · 368 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NUMERICAL, NUMEROUS, or Numerical, something belonging to numbers; as numerical algebra is that which makes use of numbers, instead of letters of the VOL. XV. Part I.

alphabet.—Also numerical difference is that by which Numerical one man is distinguished from another. Hence a thing is said to be numerically the same, when it is so in the strictest sense of the word.
