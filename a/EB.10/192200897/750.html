OSTERVALD, JOHN FREDERIC, a famous Protestant divine, was born at Neufchattel in 1663; and made such rapid progress in his studies, that he became master of arts at Saumur before he was 16 years of age. He afterwards studied at Orleans and at Paris. At his return to Neufchattel in 1699, he became pastor of the church there; and contracted a strict friendship with the celebrated John Alphonse Turretin of Geneva, and the illustrious Samuel Werenfels of Basel. The union of these three divines, which was called the Triumvirate of the divines of Switzerland, lasted till his death. He died in 1747, regretted by all who knew him.
