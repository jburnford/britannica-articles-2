PALATIUM Dioclefanii; the villa of Dioclefan, near Salone, where he died, (Eusebius). Afterwards called Spalatium; which rose to a considerable city from the ruins of Salone; situated in Dalmatia on the Adriatic. Now Spallato, or Spalatro.
