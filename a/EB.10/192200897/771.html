OTHO

article · 259 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

OTHO, a tribune of the people, who, in Cicero's consulship, made a regulation to permit the Roman knights at public spectacles to have the 14 first rows after the seats of the senators. This was opposed with virulence by some, but Cicero ably defended it, &c.
