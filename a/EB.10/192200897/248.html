NUBECULA, LITTLE CLOUD

NUBECULA, LITTLE CLOUD, in Medicine, a term sometimes used for a disease in the eye, wherein objects appear as through a cloud or mist.

The nubecula seems to arise from certain gross particles detained in the pores of the cornea, or swimming in the aqueous humour, and thus intercepting the rays of light.
