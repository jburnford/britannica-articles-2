ONTARIO

ONTARIO, a lake of North America, in the country of the Iroquois, 150 miles in length, and from 21 to 44 in breadth. Many rivers run into this lake: and from

Ontario from it the great river St Lawrence proceeds. It communicates with lake Erie by a river 33 miles in length, on which is the remarkable cataract of NIAGARA.
