NUZZER

article · 399 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NUZZER, or NUZZERANAH; a present or offering from an inferior to a superior. In Hindostan no man ever approaches his superior for the first time on business without an offering of at least a gold or silver rupee in his right hand; which, if not taken, is a mark of disfavour. Nuzzaranah is also used for the sum paid to the government as an acknowledgement for a grant of lands or any public office.
