PANORMUS

PANORMUS (Ptolemy), a port of Attica; its name denoting it to be capacious.—Another, of Epirus (Strabo, Ptolemy); a large harbour in the heart of the Montes Cerauni, below the citadel Chimæra.—A third of Ionia (Strabo); near Ephesus, with the temple of the Ephesian Diana.
