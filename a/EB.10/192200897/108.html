NOBILIARY

article · 310 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NOBILIARY, in literary history, a book containing the history of the noble families of a nation or province: such are Chorier's Nobiliary of Dauphiné, and Caumartin's Nobiliary of Provence. The Germans are said to be particularly careful of their Nobiliaries, in order to keep up the dignity of their families.
