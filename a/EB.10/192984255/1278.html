CONOVUM

article · 271 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONOVIVUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Ordovices, in Britain. From its ruins arose, at the distance of four miles, Aberconvey, the mouth of the Convey, in Caernarvonshire; and on the spot where Conovivum stood is an hamlet, called Caerhean, the old town (Camden).
