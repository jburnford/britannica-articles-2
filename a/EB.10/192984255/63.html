CHORD

article · 171 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHORD, or CORD, primarily denotes a slender rope or cordage*. The word is formed of the Latin, cor-dia, and that from the Greek, χορδα, a gut, whereof strings may be made.
