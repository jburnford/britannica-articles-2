CONTRAFISSURE

article · 190 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONTRAFISSURE, in Surgery, a kind of fracture, or fissure, in the cranium, which sometimes happens on the side opposite to that which received the blow, or at least at some distance from it.
