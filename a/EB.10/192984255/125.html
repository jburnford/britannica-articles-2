CHRYSOLARUS, EMANUEL

article · 495 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHRYSOLARUS, EMANUEL, one of those learned men in the 14th century who brought the Greek literature into the west. He was a man of rank; and descended from an ancient family, said to have removed with Constantine from Rome to Byzantium. He was sent into Europe by the emperor of the east to explore the assistance of Christian princes. He afterwards taught at Florence, Venice, Pavia, and Rome; and died at Constantinople, in 1415, aged 47. He wrote a Greek grammar, and some other small pieces.
