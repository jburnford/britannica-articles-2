CHYME

article · 379 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHYME, or CHYMUS, in the common signification of the word, denotes every kind of humour which is incrassated by concoction; under which notion it comprehends all the humours fit or unfit for preserving and nourishing the body, whether good or bad. It frequently imports the finest parts of the chyle, when separated from the feces, and contained in the lacteal and thoracic duct.
