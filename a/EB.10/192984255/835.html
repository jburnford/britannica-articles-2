COLONIA Trajana, (Antonine, Peutinger); a town

of Belgica, furnished also Uspia, (Antonine); and Tricesima, from being the station of the thirtieth legion, (Amian). Now Kellen, a village of the duchy of Cleves, a mile from the Rhine.
