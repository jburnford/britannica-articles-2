CIVIL War, a war between people of the same state, or the citizens of the same city.

Civil Year || Civil Year, is the legal year, or annual account of time, which every government appoints to be used within its own dominions; and is so called in contradistinction to the natural year, which is measured exactly by the revolution of the heavenly bodies.
