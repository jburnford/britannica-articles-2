CHINON

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHINON, an ancient town of Tourrain in France, remarkable for the death of Henry II. king of England, and for the birth of the famous Rabelais. It is seated on the river Vienne, in a pleasant and fertile country, in E. Long. o. 18. N. Lat. 47. 2.
