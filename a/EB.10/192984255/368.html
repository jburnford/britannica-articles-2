CLARENNA, (Tabulae), in Ancient Geography, a town of Vindelicia, at the confluence of the Lyeus and Danube. Now Rain, a town of Bavaria, on the south side of the Danube, at the confluence of the Lech. E. Long. 11. o. N. Lat. 48. 45.
