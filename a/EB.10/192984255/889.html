Funeral COLUMN

sub_entry · 219 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Funeral COLUMN, that which bears an urn, wherein are supposed to be inclosed the ashes of some deceased hero; and whose shaft is sometimes overspread with tears and flames, which are symbols of grief and of immortality.
