COSMOLABE

article · 344 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COSMOLABE (from κοσμος, world, and λαβω, I take), an ancient mathematical instrument, serving to measure distances both in the heavens and on earth. The Cosmolabe is in a great measure the same with the astrolabe. It is also called pantacosm, or the universal instrument, by L. Morgard, in a treatise written expressly upon it, printed in 1612.
