CLARUS

CLARUS, or CLAROS, in Ancient Geography, a town of Ionia, famous for an oracle of Apollo. It was built by Manto, daughter of Tiresias, who fled from Thebes after it had been destroyed by the Epigoni. She was so afflicted with her misfortunes, that a lake was formed with her tears, where she first founded the oracle. Apollo was from thence furnished Clarius, also an island of the Ægean sea, between Tenedos and Scios.
