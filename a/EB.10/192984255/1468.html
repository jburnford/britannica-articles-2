COPAL

Cross-references: VARNISHING

COPAL, improperly called gum copal, is a gum of the resinous kind brought from New Spain, being the concrete juice of the Rhus Copallinum. It is employed as a varnish. See VARNISHING and CHEMISTRY Index.
