Hollow COLUMN

sub_entry · 436 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Hollow COLUMN, that which has a spiral staircase withinside for the convenience of ascending to the top; as the Trajan column, the staircase whereof consists of 185 steps, and is illuminated by 43 little windows, each of which is divided by tambours of white marble. The monument, or fire-column at London, has also a staircase; but it does not reach to the top. These kinds of columns are also called columnae, coelideae, or cochlidae.
