CLERK of the Nichils or Nihil

CLERK of the Nichils or Nihil, an officer of the exchequer, who makes a roll of all such sums as are nichilled by the sheriff upon their estreats of green wax, and delivers them into the remembrancer of the treasury, to have execution done upon them for the king. See NIHIL.
