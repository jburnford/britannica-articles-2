CONTRADICTORY PROPOSITIONS, are opposite

sites, one of which imports a mere and naked denial of the other.

Seeming contradictory is when the members of a period quite disagree in appearance and sound, but perfectly agree and are consistent in sense: thus,

Cowards die many times before their death:
The valiant never taste of death but once.

Shakespeare.
