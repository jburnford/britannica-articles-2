COUNT-CORPORATE

article · 306 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COUNT-CORPORATE, is a title given to several cities, or ancient boroughs, on which our monarchs have thought fit to bestow extraordinary privileges; annexing to them a particular territory, land, or jurisdiction; and making them counties of themselves, to be governed by their own sheriffs and magistrates.
