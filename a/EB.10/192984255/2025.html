CROFT

article · 310 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CROFT, a little close adjoining to a dwelling-house, and inclosed for pasture or arable land, or any other purpose.—In some ancient deeds, crofta occurs as the Latin word for a "croft;" but cum tosis etcroftis is more frequent. Croft is translated in Abbo Floriencensis by predium, a "farm." croft is also used
