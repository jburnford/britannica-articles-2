CORNICULUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Sabines, to the east of Crustumerium, towards the Anio. It was burnt down by Tarquin; but restored

again, after the expulsion of the kings, (Florus). Now called il Monte Genaro, (Hofstenius).
