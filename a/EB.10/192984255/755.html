COLLATION

article · 314 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COLLATION, in Scotts Law, that right which an heir has of throwing the whole heritable and moveable estates of the deceased into one mass, and sharing it equally with the others in the same degree of kindred, when he thinks such share will be more than the value of the heritage to which he had an exclusive title.
