CLEOMENES II

article · 190 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CLEOMENES II. succeeded his brother Agesipolis II. He reigned 34 years in the greatest tranquillity, and was father to Acrotatus and Cleonymus. He was succeeded by Areus I. son of Acrotatus.
