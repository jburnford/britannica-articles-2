CHIUM VINUM

article · 398 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHIUM VINUM, Chian Wine, or wine of the growth of the island of Chios, now Scio, is commended by Dioscorides as affording good nourishment, fit to drink, less disposed to intoxicate, endued with the virtue of restraining defluxions, and a proper ingredient in ophthalmic.

thalmic medicines. Hence Scribonius Largus directs the dry ingredients in collyria for the eyes to be made up with Chian wine.
