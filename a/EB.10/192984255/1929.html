CRAZE MILL

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CRAZE MILL, or CRAZING Mill, a mill in all respects like a grist-mill to grind corn, and so called by the tin miners, who use it to grind their tin, which is yet too great after trampling.
