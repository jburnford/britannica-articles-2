CLAVUS

article · 216 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CLAVUS, in antiquity, an ornament upon the robes of the Roman senators and knights, which was more or less broad, according to the dignity of the person; hence the distinction of tunica angusticlavia and lati-clavia.
