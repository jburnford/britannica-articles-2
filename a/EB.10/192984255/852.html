COLOSTRUM

article · 332 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COLOSTRUM, the first milk of any animal after bringing forth young, called in some places beefings. It is remarkable that this milk is generally cathartic, and purges the meconium; thus serving both as an aliment and medicine.

An emulsion prepared with turpentine dissolved with the yolk of an egg, is sometimes called by this name.
