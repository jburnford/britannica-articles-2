CLAMPETIA

CLAMPETIA, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Bruttii, one of those which revolted from Hannibal (Livy;) called Lampetia by Polybius. Now Amantia, or Mantia, a town of Calabria Ultra, near the bay of Euphemia. E. Long. 16. 20. N. Lat. 39. 15.
