COMPOSITUS FLOS

article · 356 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COMPOSITUS FLOS, in Botany, an aggregate flower, composed of many stosculi sessiles, on a common entire receptaculum, with a common perianthium, and whose antheræ being five in number unite in the form of a cylinder; the stosculi are monopetalous, and under each of them is a monospermous germen. Compound flowers are either ligulati, tubulosi, or radiati.
