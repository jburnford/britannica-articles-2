COMMONTY

article · 340 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: LAW INDEX

COMMONTY, in Scots Law, sometimes signifies lands belonging to two or more common proprietors; sometimes a heath or moor, though it should belong in property to one, if there has been a promiscuous possession upon it by pasturage; and the act 1695 mentions commonities belonging in property to the king and to royal boroughs.
