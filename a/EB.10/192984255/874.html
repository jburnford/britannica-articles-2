COLUMBARIA

article · 267 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COLUMBARIA, in Ancient Geography, an island like a rock on the west of Sicily, opposite to Drepanum; said by Zonares to have been taken from the Carthaginians by Numerius Fabius the consul. Now Columbara, with a very strong and almost impregnable citadel (Cluverius).
