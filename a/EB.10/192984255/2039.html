CRONIUS, in Chronology, the ancient name of the Athenian month Hecatombaeon; which was the first

first of their year, and answered to the latter part of our June and beginning of July.—There were feasts called Croniones celebrated at Athens in this month, in honour of Saturn, answering to the Saturnalia of the Romans.
