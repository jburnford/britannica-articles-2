COBURG

article · 314 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COBURG, a town of Germany in the circle of Franconia, and capital of a territory of the same name, with a famous college, a fort, and a castle. This town, with its principality, belongs to the house of Saxony, and the inhabitants are Protestants. It is seated on the river Itch, in E. Long. 11. 18. N. Lat. 50. 22.
