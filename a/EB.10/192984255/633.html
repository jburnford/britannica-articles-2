COCKSWAIN

article · 317 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COCKSWAIN, or COCKSON, an officer on board a man of war, who hath the care of the boat or sloop, and all things belonging to it. He is to be always ready with his boat's gang or crew, and to man the boat on all occasions. He sits in the stern of the boat, and steers; and hath a whistle to call and encourage his men.
