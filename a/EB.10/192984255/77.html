CHOROIDES

article · 380 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHOROIDES is particularly used for the inner membrane which immediately invests the brain; so called as being intermingled with a great number of blood-vessels, like the chorion: but more usually denominated the pia mater or minime tenuis.

Plexus or Lacis CHOROIDES, is a knot of veins and arteries in the anterior ventricle of the brain, woven out of the branches of the carotid.
