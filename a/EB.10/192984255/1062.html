COMPLICATION

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COMPLICATION, in general, denotes the blending, or rather interweaving, of several different things together: thus, a person afflicted with several disorders at the same time, is said to labour under a complication of disorders.
