CHROASTACES

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHROASTACES, an old term in Natural History, applied to gems, and comprehending all those of variable colours, as viewed in different lights and in different positions; of which kinds are the opal and the asperia or cat's eye.
