CORCELET

article · 341 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CORCELET, in Entomology, is that part of the body of an insect which is analogous in its situation to the breast in other animals. Many have called it the breast in

these also, but improperly; because the breast of other animals is the place of the lungs and trachea, but these organs are in the fly class distributed through the whole body.
