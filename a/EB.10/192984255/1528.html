CORCYRA Nigra

sub_entry · 261 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CORCYRA Nigra, an island in the Adriatic, on the coast of Dalmatia (Pliny); called Melena by the Greeks, to distinguish it from the island in the Ionian sea. The epithet Nigra was added, from its woods of tall trees with which it is almost covered. Now Curzola.
