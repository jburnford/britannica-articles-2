CIRCLE

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CIRCLE, in Logic, or Logical CIRCLE, is when the same terms are proved in orbem by the same terms; and the parts of the syllogism alternately by each other, both directly and indirectly.
