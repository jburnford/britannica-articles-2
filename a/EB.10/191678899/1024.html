TYPE

article · 339 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TYPE (typos), an impression, image, or representation of some model, which is termed the antitype. In this sense the word occurs often in the writings of divines, who employ it to denote that prefiguration of the great events of man's redemption which they have found or fancied in the principal transactions recorded in the Old Testament.
