WAYHTES, or WAITS

sub_entry · 325 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WAYHTES, or WAITS, a word which is used only in the plural number, and signifies hautboys. It is now applied to the performers on these and other musical instruments, by a transition from the instruments themselves, and particularly to those performers who parade the streets by night, about the Christmas season of the year.
