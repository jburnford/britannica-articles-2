ZEMBLA, NOVA

ZEMBLA, NOVA, a very large island lying in the Northern ocean, to the north of Russia, from which it is separated by the strait of Waigata. It has no inhabitants except wild beasts, particularly white foxes and bears. In 1595 a Dutch vessel was cast away on the coast, and the ship's company were obliged to winter here.
