VENTER

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VENTER, signifies the belly; but it is also used for the children by a woman of one marriage: there is in law a first and second venter, &c. where a man hath children by several wives; and how they shall take in descents of lands.
