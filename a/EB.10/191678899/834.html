TREMOR

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TREMOR, an involuntary shaking, chiefly of the hands and head, sometimes of the feet, and sometimes of the tongue and heart.—Tremors arising from a too free use of spirituous liquors require the same treatment as palsies.
