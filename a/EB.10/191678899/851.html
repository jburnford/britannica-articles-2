TRIANDRIA

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TRIANDRIA, (from τρίς, "three," and άνδρ, "a man or husband"), the name of the third class in Linnaeus's sexual system, consisting of plants with hermaphrodite flowers, which have three stamens or male organs.
