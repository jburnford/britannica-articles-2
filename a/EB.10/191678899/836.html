TRENT, BISHOPRIC OF

article · 378 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TRENT, BISHOPRIC OF, a province of Germany, in the circle of Austria, near the frontiers of Italy; is bounded on the north by Tirol; on the east by the Feltrino and Bellunese; on the south, by Vincentino, the Veronese, Bresciano, and the lake de Garda; and on the west, by the Bresciano and the lake de Garda. The soil is said to be very fruitful, and to abound in wine and oil.
