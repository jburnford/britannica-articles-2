TARANTO, the ancient TARENTUM, a sea-port town of Italy, in the kingdom of Naples, and in the Terra de Otranto. It is a strong and populous place, with an archbishop's see, and the title of a principality. It is seated on a peninsula, and is defended by a strong castle; but the harbour is choked up. E. Long. 17. 29. N. Lat. 40. 35.
