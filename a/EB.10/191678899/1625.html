WHITSUN-FARTHINGS

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WHITSUN-FARTHINGS, otherwise called Smokethurtings or Quadrantes Pentecostales, a composition for offerings which were anciently made in Whitsun-week by every man in England, who occupied a house with a chimney, to the cathedral church of the diocese in which he lived.
