TRANPOSITION

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TRANPOSITION, in Grammar, a disturbing or dislocating the words of a discourse, or a changing their natural order of construction, to please the ear by rendering the texture more smooth, easy, and harmonious.
