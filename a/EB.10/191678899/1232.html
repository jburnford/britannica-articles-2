VIOLATION

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VIOLATION, the act of violating, that is, forcing a woman, or committing a rape upon her.—This term is also used in a moral sense, for a breach or infringement of a law, ordinance, or the like.
