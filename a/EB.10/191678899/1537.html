WEBSTER, ALEXANDER, D. D

WEBSTER, ALEXANDER, D. D. was the son of James Webster, minister of the Tolbooth church in Edinburgh, and born in that city about the year 1707. He was only 13 years of age at the death of his father, and of course could derive little from parental instruction or example.
