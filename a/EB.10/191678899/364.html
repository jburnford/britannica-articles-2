TEARS

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TEARS, a lymph or aqueous humour, which is limpid, and a little saltish: it is separated from the arterial blood by the lachrymal glands and small glandulous grains on the inside of the eyelids.
