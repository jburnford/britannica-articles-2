THEODOSIUS I

article · 298 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: CONSTANTINOPLE

THEODOSIUS I. called the Great, was a native of Spain. The valour he had shown, and the great services he had done to the empire, made Gratian, when

attacked by the Goths and Germans, to admit him as a partner in the government. He received the purple in 379, aged 43. See CONSTANTINOPLE, No 77-88.
