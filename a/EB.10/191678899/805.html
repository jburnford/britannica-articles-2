TRANSVERSE

article · 203 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TRANSVERSE, something that goes across another from corner to corner: thus bends and bars in heraldry are transverse pieces or bearings; the diagonals of a parallelogram or a square are transverse lines.
