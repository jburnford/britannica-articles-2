WHITSUNDAY Isle

sub_entry · 249 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WHITSUNDAY Isle, one of the New Hebrides, which lies about four miles to the south, runs in the same direction, and is of the same length, having more sloping exposures than Aurora: it appears to be better inhabited, and to contain more plantations.
