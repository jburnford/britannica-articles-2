VERGERS

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VERGERS, certain officers of the courts of king's bench and common pleas, whose business it is to carry white wands before the judges. There are also vergers of cathedrals, who carry a rod tipped with silver before the bishop, dean, &c.
