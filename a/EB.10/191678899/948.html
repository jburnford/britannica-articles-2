TUBE

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TUBE, in general, a pipe, conduit, or canal; a cylinder, hollow within-side, either of lead, iron, glass, wood, or other matter, for the air or some other matter to have a free conveyance through it.
