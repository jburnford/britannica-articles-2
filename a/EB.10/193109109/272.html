GUARANTEE, or WARRANT, in Law, a term relative to warrant or warrantor, properly signifying him whom the warrantor undertakes to indemnify or secure from damage.

GUARANTEE is more frequently used for a warrantor, Q. 2 or

Guarantee or a person who undertakes and obliges himself to see a second person perform what he has stipulated to the third. See WARRANTY.
