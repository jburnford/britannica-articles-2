HEAD-Sails

sub_entry · 502 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEAD-Sails, a general name for all those sails which are extended on the foremast and bowsprit, and employed to command the fore part of the ship: such are the foresail, fore-top-sail, fore-top-gallant-sail, jib, fore-stay-sail, and the sprit-sail, with its topsail. This term is used in opposition to after-sails, viz. all those which are extended on the mizen-mast, and on the stays between the mizen and main-masts.

HEAD-to-wind; the situation of a ship or boat, when her head is turned to windward.
