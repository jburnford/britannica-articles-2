HIRCUS

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HIRCUS, in Astronomy, a fixed star of the first magnitude, the same with Capella. It is also made use of by some writers for a comet, encompassed as it were with a mane, apparently rough and hairy.
