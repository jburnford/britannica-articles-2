GRUINALES

article · 189 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GRUINALES (from grus, "a crane"), the name of the fourteenth order in Linnaeus's Fragments of a Natural Method, consisting of geranium, and other genera allied to it in habit and structure.
