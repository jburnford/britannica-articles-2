HURLY-BURLY

article · 210 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HURLY-BURLY, in vulgar language, denotes con-
fusion or tumult, and is said to owe its origin to two
neighbouring families, Hurleigh and Burleigh, which
filled their part of the kingdom with content and vio-
lence.
