HIRSBERG

article · 154 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HIRSBERG, a town of Silesia, in the territory of Jauer, famous for its mineral baths. It is seated on the river Bosar, in E. Long. 15. 50. N. Lat. 50. 44.
