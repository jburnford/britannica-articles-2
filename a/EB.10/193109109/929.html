HEMEROTROPHIS

article · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEMEROTROPHIS, in antiquity, a measure of capacity, the same with the choenix. It was so called from its holding one day's food. The word is compounded of ἡμέρα, a day, and τροφά, food.
