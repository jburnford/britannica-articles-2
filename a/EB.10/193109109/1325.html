HOMBERG, a town of Germany, in the circle of the Upper Rhine, and landgraviate of Hesse, seated ten miles north of Frankfurt, and gives title to one of the branches of the house of Hesse, who is its sovereign. E. Long. 8. 32. N. Lat. 50. 15.
