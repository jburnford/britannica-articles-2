HALLEIN, a town of Germany, in the circle of
Bavaria, and archbishopric of Salzburg; seated on the
river Saltza, among the mountains, wherein are mines
of salt, which are the chief riches of the town and
country. E. Long. 12. 15. N. Lat. 47. 33.
