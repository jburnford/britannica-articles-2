HADERSLEBEN, a sea-port town of Denmark,

Hæretico. in the duchy of Sleswic, with a strong citadel, built upon a small island. It is seated on a bay of the Baltic sea, and has a well frequented harbour. E. Long. 9. 30. N. Lat. 55. 18.
