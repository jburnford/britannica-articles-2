HORSE is also a term used in various arts and manufactures, for something that helps to sustain their work from the ground, for the more commodious working at it.

The horse used by tanners and skinners, also called the leg, is a piece of wood cut hollow and roundish, four or five feet long, and placed aslope; upon which they pare their skins to get off the dirt, hair, flesh, &c.
