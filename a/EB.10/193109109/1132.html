HEXANDRIA, in Botany, (from ἱξ, fix, and ἄνδρ, a man); the name of the sixth class in Linnaeus's sexual method, consisting of plants with hermaphrodite flowers, which are furnished with six stamens or male organs, that are of an equal length. See BOTANY Index.
