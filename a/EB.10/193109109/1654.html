HYDRARGYRUM

article · 206 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HYDRARGYRUM, a name given to mercury, or quicksilver. The word is formed of hýdr, aqua, "water," and argyros, argentum, "silver;" q. d. water of silver, on account of its resembling liquid or melted silver.
