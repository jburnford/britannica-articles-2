GROTSKAW, a town of Turkey in Europe, in
the province of Servia, where a battle was fought be-
tween the Germans and Turks, in the year 1739, in
which the Germans were forced to retreat. It is 15
miles S. from Belgrade. E. Long. 21. 0. N. Lat. 45. 0.
