HORNEPIE

article · 216 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HORNEPIE is also the name of an English air, probably derived from the above instrument. The measure of this air is triple time, with six crochets in a bar; four of which are to be beat with the hand down and two up.
