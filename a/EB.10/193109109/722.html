HATCHES

article · 271 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HATCHES also denote flood-gates set in a river, &c. to stop the current of the water, particularly certain dams or mounds made of rubbish, clay, or earth, to prevent the water that issues from the stream-works and tin-walks in Cornwall from running into the fresh rivers.
