GUILD-HALL, or Gild-Hall

sub_entry · 274 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GUILD-HALL, or Gild-Hall, the great court of judicature for the city of London. In it are kept the mayor's court, the sheriff's court, the court of hustings, court of conscience, court of common council, chamberlain's court, &c. Here also the judges sit upon nisi prius, &c.
