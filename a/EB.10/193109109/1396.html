HOPLOMACHI

article · 214 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HOPLOMACHI, ὁπλομάχαι, (composed of ἵππος, armour, and μάχημαι, I fight), in antiquity, were a species of gladiators who fought in armour; either completely armed from head to foot, or only with a cask and cuirass.
