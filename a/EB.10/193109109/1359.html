MAIDS of Honour

sub_entry · 195 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MAIDS of Honour, are young ladies in the queen's household, whose office is to attend the queen when she goes abroad, &c. In England they are fix in number, and their salary 300l. per annum each.
