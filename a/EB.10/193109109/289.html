Rear GUARD

sub_entry · 311 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Rear GUARD, that party of the army which brings up the rear on a march, generally composed of all the old grand guards of the camp. The rear-guard of a party is frequently eight or ten horse, about 500 paces behind the party. Hence the advance-guard going out upon a party, form the rear-guard in their retreat.
