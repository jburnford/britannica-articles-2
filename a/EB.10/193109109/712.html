HASTING-PEAR

article · 285 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HASTING-PEAR, a name given by the gardeners to a species of pear, called also by some the green chissel pear. This is a moderately large pear, and is longish towards the pedicle; its skin is thin, and of a whitish green; the pulp is melting, and of a sugary flavour. It ripens in July.
