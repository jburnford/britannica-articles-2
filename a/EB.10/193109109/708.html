HASSOCK

article · 247 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HASSOCK, a bas made of rushes, to kneel or rest the feet upon in churches.

HASP and STAPLE, in Scots Law, the symbol commonly used in burgh tenements for entering and infecting an heir, by delivering into his hands the hasp and staple of the door.
