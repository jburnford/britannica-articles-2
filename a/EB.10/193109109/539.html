HALTERES

article · 267 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: ENTOMOLOGY

HALTERES, or POISERS, in Entomology, two small round bodies, supported on stalks and attached to the insect under the wings of dipterous flies, as in the tipula genus. They are supposed by some naturalists to be the rudiments of another pair of wings. See ENTOMOLOGY.
