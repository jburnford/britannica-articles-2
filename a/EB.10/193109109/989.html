HEPATOSCOPIA

article · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEPATOSCOPIA, (formed of ἥπαρ, liver, and σκοπία, I consider), in antiquity, a species of divination, wherein predictions were made by inspecting the livers of animals.
