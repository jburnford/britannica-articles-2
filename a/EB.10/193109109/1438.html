HORNBY, a town of England, in Lancashire, seated on a branch of the river Lune, and beautified with a handsome parochial chapel. The inhabitants are estimated at 414, and are chiefly employed in the cotton manufacture. W. Long. 2. 33. N. Lat. 54. 3.
