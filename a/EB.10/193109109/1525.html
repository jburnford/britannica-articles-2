HOUSING, or HOUSE-Line

Cross-references: BOLT-R

HOUSING, or HOUSE-Line, in the sea-language, a small line, formed of three fine strands or twists of hemp, smaller than rope-yarn. It is chiefly used to seize blocks into their strops, to bind the corners of the sails, or to fasten the bottom of a sail to its bolt-rope, &c. See BOLT-Rope.
