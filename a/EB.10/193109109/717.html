HATS

article · 302 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HATS are also made for women's wear, not only of the above stuffs, but of chips, straw, or cane, by plaiting, and sewing the plaits together; beginning with the centre of the crown, and working round till the whole is finished. Hats for the same purpose are also woven and made of horse-hair, silk, &c.
