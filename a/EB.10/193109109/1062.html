HERNE, a town of Kent, ſix miles from Canterbury, 12 from Margate, and 14 from Feverſham. The church is a large ancient ſtructure, with a tower of ſint, and has ſix ſtalls of the cathedral kind, with diviſions of the choir from the nave by a carved ſcreen of oak. The church is 113 feet long. The ſtone front is very ancient. Here the great Dr Ridley, the Engliſh martyr, was vicar. Here is a commodious bay, frequented by colliers, &c.
