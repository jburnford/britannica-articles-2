HENNEBON

article · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HENNEBON, a town of France, in the department of Morbihan, and situated on the river Elavet. It enjoys a considerable trade, and contains 3000 inhabitants. It is 62 miles west from Paris, in W. Long. 3. 11. N. Lat. 47. 48.
