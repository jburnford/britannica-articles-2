HONFLEUR

article · 220 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HONFLEUR, a considerable sea-port town of France, in the department of Calvados, with a good harbour, and trade in bone-lace, and with 9000 inhabitants. It is seated on the river Seine, in E. Long. 0. 19. N. Lat. 25. 49.
