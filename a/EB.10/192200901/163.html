LISSUS

LISSUS, in Ancient Geography, the last town of Illyricum, towards Macedonia, situated on the Drilo. It had a capacious port, the work of Diomysius the Tyrant, who led the colony thither, enlarged and walled it round, (Diodorus Siculus). Now called Alessio, in Albania, on the Drino, near the gulf of Venice. E. Long. 20. N. Lat. 42.
