MARENGO

article · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MARENGO, a village of France which has risen to distinction in consequence of the bloody battle between the Austrians and French, which was fought on the 14th June 1800, in which the French say, 15,000 of the former were killed, wounded, and made prisoners.—MARENGO is also the name of a department of France, including part of Lombardy.
