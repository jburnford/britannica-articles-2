LIVONICA-TERRA

article · 164 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LIVONIA-TERRA, a kind of fine bole used in the shops of Germany and Italy. It is found in Livonia, from whence it takes its name. It is in the form of little cakes.
