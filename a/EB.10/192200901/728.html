MAJOR, in Law

sub_entry · 183 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MAJOR, in Law, a person who is of age to manage his own affairs. By the civil law a man is not a major till the age of 25 years; in England, he is a major at 21, as in Normandy at 20.
