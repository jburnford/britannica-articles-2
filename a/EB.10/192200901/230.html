LOCARNO

LOCARNO, a town of Switzerland, capital of a bailiwick of the same name, seated at the north end of the lake Maggiore, near the river Magie. It carries on a great trade; and the country abounds in pastures, wine, and fruits. E. Long. 8. 41. N. Lat. 46. 6.
