LITUS

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LITUS, was also an instrument of music in use in the Roman army. It was straight, excepting that it had a little bending at the upper end like a litus or sacred staff of the augurs; and from the similitude it derived its name.
