LUTE

article · 210 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LUTE, or LOTING, among chemists, a mixed, te-
nacious, ductile substance, which grows solid by drying,
and, being applied to the juncture of vessels, stops them
up so as to prevent the air from getting in or out.
