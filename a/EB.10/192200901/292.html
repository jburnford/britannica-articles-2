LONGTOWN

article · 263 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LONGTOWN, a town of Cumberland, on the Scots borders, near the conflux of the Esk and Kirkcudbright, 9 miles from Carlisle, and 313 miles from London; it has a market on Thursday, and a charity school for 60 children. The population in 1801 was estimated at 1313.
