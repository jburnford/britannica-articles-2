MARC GRAVE, or MARGRAVE, a kind of dignity in Germany, answering to our marquis; (see MARQUIS). The word is derived from the German Marche, or Marete, which signifies "a frontier;" and Graf, "count, governor;" Marcgraves being originally governors of cities lying on the frontiers of a country or state.
