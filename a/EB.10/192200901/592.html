MACHIAN

article · 220 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MACHIAN, one of the Molucca islands, in the East Indian ocean, about 20 miles in circumference, populous and fertile, producing cloves and sago; and is in possession of the Dutch, who have three strong forts built on it.
