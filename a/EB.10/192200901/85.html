LIMPURG

article · 270 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LIMPURG, a barony of Germany, in the circle of Franconia, included almost entirely within Suabia, and seated to the south of Hall in Suabia. It is about 15 miles long, and eight broad. Gaildorf and Shonburg, near which is the castle of Limpurg, are the principal places.
