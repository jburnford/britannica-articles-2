MANHEIM

article · 341 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MANHEIM, a town of Germany, in the Lower Palatinate, with a very strong citadel, and a palace, where the elector Palatine often resides. It is seated at the confluence of the rivers Neckar and Rhine, in E. Long. 8. 33. N. Lat. 49. 25. It surrendered to the French in September 1795; but it was retaken by the Austrians in November following.
