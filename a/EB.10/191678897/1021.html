DIGNE

article · 260 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIGNE, an episcopal town in the department of the Lower Alps, formerly Provence, in France, famous for its hot springs. It is seated on a river called Bleonne, 30 miles S. by W. of Embrun, in E. Long. 6. 12. N. Lat. 44. 10. The population is nearly 3000 souls.
