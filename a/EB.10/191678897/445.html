DAUPHINS

DAUPHINS, or Delphins, in literary history, a name given to the commentators on the ancient Latin authors, who were employed by Louis XIV. of France for the benefit of the prince, under the care and direction of M. de Montauſier his governor, and Boſſuet and Huet his preceptors. They were 39 in number.
