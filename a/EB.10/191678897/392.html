DAPPLE-BAY

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DAPPLE-BAY, in the manège: When bay horses have marks of a dark bay, they are called dapple-bays.

DAPPLE-Blacks: When a black horse has got spots or marks more black or shining than the rest of his skin, he is called a dapple-black.
