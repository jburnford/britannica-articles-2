DIGNITARY

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIGNITARY, in the canon law, a person who holds a dignity, that is, a benefice which gives him some pre-eminence over mere priests and canons. Such is a bishop, dean, archdeacon, prebendary, &c.
