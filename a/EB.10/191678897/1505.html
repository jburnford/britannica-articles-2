DRIVERS

article · 330 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DRIVERS, among sportsmen, a machine for driving pheasant powts, consisting of good strong oser wands, such as the basketmakers use; these are to be set in a handle, and twisted or bound with small oser in two or three places. With this instrument the sportsman drives whole eyes of young powts into his nets. See the next article.
