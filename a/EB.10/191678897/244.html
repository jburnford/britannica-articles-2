CYNOSCEPHALÆ

article · 313 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CYNOSCEPHALÆ, in Ancient Geography, a place in Theffaly near Scotussa; where the Romans, under Q. Flaminius, gained a great victory over Philip, son of Demetrius king of Macedon. These Cynoscephalæ

phale are small tops of several equal eminences; named from their resemblance to dogs heads, according to Plutarch.
