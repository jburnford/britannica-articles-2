DIONYSIACA

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIONYSIACA, in antiquity, was a designation

given to plays and all manner of sports acted on the Dionysiac stage; because playhouses were dedicated to Dionysius, i. e. Bacchus, and Venus, as being the deities of sports and pleasure.
