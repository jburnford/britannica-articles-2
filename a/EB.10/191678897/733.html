DEPORTATION

article · 204 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DEPORTATION, a sort of banishment used by
the Romans, whereby some island or other place was
allotted to a criminal for the place of his abode, with
a prohibition not to stir out of the same on pain of
death.
