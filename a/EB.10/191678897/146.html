CURZOLA

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CURZOLA, an island in the gulf of Venice, lying on the coast of Dalmatia. It is about 20 miles long, and has a small town of the same name, with a bishop's see. It belongs to the Venetians. E. Long. 17. 15. N. Lat. 43. 6.
