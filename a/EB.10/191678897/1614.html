DUUPLE

article · 202 chars · page image at NLS ↗

DUUPLE, among mathematicians, denotes the ratio
of 2 to 1. Thus the ratio of 8 to 4 is double, or as 2
to 1.

Sub-DUPLE Ratio, is just the reverse of the former,
or as 1 to 2. Such is 4 to 8, or 6 to 12.
