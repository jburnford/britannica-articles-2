DOCK

article · 379 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DOCK, in the manège, is used for a large case of leather, as long as the dock of a horse's tail, which serves it for a cover. The French call the dock troussequeue. It is made fast by the straps to the crupper, and has leathern thongs that pass between his thighs, and along his flanks to the saddle straps, in order to keep the tail tight, and to hinder it from whistling about.
