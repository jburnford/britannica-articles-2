DIBIO, or DIVIO, in Ancient Geography, the Drivionense Castrum and the Divionum of the lower age: a town of the Lingones, in Gallia Belgica: Dibionenses the people. Now Dijon the capital of Burgundy. E. Long. 5. 5. N. Lat. 47. 15.
