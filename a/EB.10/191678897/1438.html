DRACO, in Astronomy, a constellation of the northern hemisphere; whole stars, according to Ptolemy, are 81: according to Tycho, 32; according to Hevelius, 40; according to Bayer, 33; and according to Mr Flamstead, 80. See ASTRONOMY, No 406.
