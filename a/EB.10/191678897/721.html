DENUNCIATION, a solemn publication or pro-
mulgation of any thing.

All vessels of enemies are lawful prizes, after denun-
ciation or proclamation of war. The design of the de-
nunciation of excommunicated persons is, that the sen-
tence may be the more fully executed by the person's
being more known.
