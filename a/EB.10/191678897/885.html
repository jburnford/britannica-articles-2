DIAL

article · 285 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIAL, an inſtrument ſerving to meaſure time; which, if effected by the aid of the ſun, is called a ſundial. The word is from the Latin dies, "day," becauſe indicating the hour of the day. The ancients alſo called it ſciatherium, from its effect by the ſhadow. See the article DIALLING.
