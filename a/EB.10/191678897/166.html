CUSTOS TEMPORALium

sub_entry · 229 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CUSTOS TEMPORALium, was the person to whom a vacant fee or abbey was given by the king, as supreme lord. His office was, as steward of the goods and profits, to give an account to the exchequer, who did the like to the exchequer.
