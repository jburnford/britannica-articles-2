CUGOGOLO

article · 220 chars · page image at NLS ↗

CUGOGOLO, in Natural History, the name of a stone much used by the Venetians in glass-making, and found in the river Tefino. It is a small stone of an impure white, of a shattery texture, and is of the shape of a pebble.
