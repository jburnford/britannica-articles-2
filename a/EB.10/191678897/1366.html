DORMANT

article · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DORMANT, in Heraldry, is used for the posture of a lion, or any other beast, lying along in a sleeping attitude with the head on the fore paws: by which it is distinguished from the couchant, where, though the beast is lying, yet he holds up his head.
