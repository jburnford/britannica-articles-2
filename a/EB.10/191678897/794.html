DESIDERATUM

article · 382 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DESIDERATUM, is used to signify the desirable perfection in any art or science; thus, it is a desideratum with the blacksmith, to render iron fusible by a gentle heat, and yet preserve it hard enough for ordinary uses; with the glasmaker and looking-glass-maker, to render glass malleable; with the clock-maker, to bring pendulums to be useful where there are irregular motions, &c.
