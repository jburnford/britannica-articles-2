DEMONSTRATIVE

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DEMONSTRATIVE, in Grammar, a term given to such pronouns as serve to indicate or point out a thing. Of this number are hic, hæc, hoc, among the Latins; and this, that, these, those, in English.
