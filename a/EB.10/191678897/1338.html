DONATION

article · 335 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DONATION, (Donatio), an act or contract whereby a man transfers to another either the property or the use of the whole or a part of his effects as a free gift.

A donation, to be valid and complete, supposes a capacity both in the donor and the donee; and requires consent, acceptance, and delivery; and by the French law registry also.
