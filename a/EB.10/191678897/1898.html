ELECT

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ELECT, in some systems of theology, is a term appropriated to the saints, or the predestinated: in which sense the elect are those persons who are said to be predestinated to glory as the end, and to sanctification as the means.
