DETONATION

article · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: CHEMISTRY INDEX

DETONATION, in Chemistry, signifies an explosion with noise made by the sudden inflammation of some combustible body: such are the explosions of gunpowder, fulminating gold, and fulminating powder. See CHEMISTRY INDEX.
