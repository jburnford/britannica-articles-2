CYCLE of Indiction

sub_entry · 285 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CYCLE of Indiction, is a series of 15 years, returning constantly around, like the other cycles, and commenced from the third year before Christ; whence it happens, that if 3 be added to any given year of Christ, and the sum be divided by 15, what remains is the year of the indiction.
