DIGESTIVE

article · 152 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIGESTIVE, in Surgery, denotes a sort of unguent, plaster, or the like, that is supposed to ripen and prepare the matter of wounds, &c. for suppuration.
