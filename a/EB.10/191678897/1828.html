EELS in vinegar

sub_entry · 295 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EELS in vinegar, are similar to those in four paste. The taste of vinegar was formerly thought to be occasioned by the biting of these little animals, but that opinion has been long justly exploded. Mentzelius says, he has observed the actual transformation of these little creatures into flies.
