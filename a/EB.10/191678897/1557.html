DUCKUP

article · 361 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DUCKUP, at sea, is a term used by the steerfman, when the main-sail, fore-sail, or sprit-sail, hinders his steering to steer by a land-mark: upon which he calls out, Duckup the clew-lines of these sails; that is, haul the sails out of the way. Also when a shot is made by a chase piece, if the clew of the sprit-sail hinders the fight they call out, Duckup, &c.
