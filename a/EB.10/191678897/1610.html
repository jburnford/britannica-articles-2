DUO

article · 329 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DUO, in Music, a song or composition, to be performed
on two parts only; one sung, the other played on
an instrument, or by two voices.

DUO is also when two voices sing different parts, as
accompanied with a third, which is a thorough bass.
It is seldom that unisons and octaves are used in duos,
except at the beginning and end.
