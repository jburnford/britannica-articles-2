JACOBITES, a term of reproach bestowed on the
persons who vindicating the doctrines of passive obe-
dience and non-resistance with respect to the arbitrary
proceedings of princes, disavow the revolution in 1688,
and assert the supposed rights, and adhere to the inter-
ests, of the late abdicated King James and his family.
