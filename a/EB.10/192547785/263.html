JEARS

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JEARS, or GEERS, in the sea language, an assemblage of tackles, by which the lower yards of a ship are hoisted along the mast to their usual station, or lowered from thence as occasion requires: the former of which operations is called swaying, and the latter striking.
