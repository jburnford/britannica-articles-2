LAMINUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Carpatani in the Hither Spain; at the distance of seven miles from the head of the Anas or Guadiana: Now Montiel, a citadel of New Castile; and the territory called Ager Laminitanus, is now el Campo de Montiel, (Clusius).
