INTERSCENDENT

article · 174 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INTERSCENDENT, in Algebra, is applied to quantities, when the exponents of their powers are radical quantities. Thus, \sqrt{x}, \sqrt[3]{x}, &c. are interscendent quantities.
