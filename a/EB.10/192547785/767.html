INVERKEITHING

INVERKEITHING, a town of Scotland, in the county of Fife, situated on the northern shore of the

frith of Forth, in W. Long. 3. 15. N. Lat. 56. 5. It was much favoured by William, who granted its first charter. He extended its liberties considerably, and in the time of David I. it became a royal residence. The Franciscans had a convent in this town; and, according to Sir Robert Sibbald, the Dominicans had another. The population in 1801 amounted to 2228; and they are employed in the herring fishery, the coal, and coasting trade. It has a considerable trade in coal and other articles.
