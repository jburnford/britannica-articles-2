HYPALLAGE

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HYPALLAGE, among grammarians, a species of hyperbaton, consisting in a mutual permutation of one case for another. Thus Virgil says, Dare clasficus aufros, for dare clasficus aufros; and again, Nec dum illis labra admovi, for nec dum illa labris admovi.
