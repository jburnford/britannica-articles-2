KAAT'S KILL

article · 210 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KAAT'S KILL, a township of New York, on the west bank of Hudson's river; five miles south of Hudson city, and 125 north of New York. It contained 1645 citizens in 1795, of whom 345 were electors and 305 slaves.
