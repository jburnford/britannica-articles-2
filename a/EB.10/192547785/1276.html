KINGTON

article · 323 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KINGTON, or KYNETON, a pretty large town in Herefordshire, 146 miles from London. It is situated on the river Arrow, and is inhabited chiefly by clothiers, who drive a considerable trade in narrow cloth. It has a considerable market for corn, cattle, leather, home made linen and woollen cloth, and all sorts of provisions.
