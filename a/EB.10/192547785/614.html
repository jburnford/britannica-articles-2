INJECTION

article · 266 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INJECTION, in Surgery, the throwing in some liquor or medicine into a vein opened by incision. This practice, and that of transfusion, or the conveying the arterial blood of one man, or other animal, into another, were once greatly practised, but are now laid aside.
