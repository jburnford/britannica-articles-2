JUNTO

Cross-references: BOARD

JUNTO, in matters of government, denotes a select council for taking cognizance of affairs of great consequence, which require secrecy.

In Spain and Portugal, it signifies much the same with convention, assembly, or board, among us: thus we meet with the junto of the three estates, of commerce, of tobacco, &c. See BOARD, &c.
