HYMETTUS

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HYMETTUS, in Ancient Geography, a mountain of Attica near Athens, famous for its marble quarries, and for its excellent honey. Hymettus the epithet. Pliny says that the orator Crassus was the first who had marble columns from this place.
