ITZECUINTEPOTZOTLI

article · 316 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ITZECUINTEPOTZOLLI, or HUNCH-BACKED DOG, a Mexican quadruped similar to a dog. It is as large as a Mastefan dog, the skin of which is varied with white, tawny, and black. The characteristic mark is a great hunch which it bears from its neck to its rump. This animal abounds most in the kingdom of Michuacan.
