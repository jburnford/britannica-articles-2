INGENUOUS (ingenuus), in Roman antiquity, an appellation given to persons born of free parents, who had never been slaves: for the children of the liberti,

I i or

Ingenious or persons who had obtained their liberty, were called libertini, not ingenui; this appellation of ingenui being reserved for their children, or the third generation.
