INTERVAL

INTERVAL, the distance or space between two extremes, either in time or place. The word comes from the Latin intervallum, which according to Lisdore, signifies the space inter fossam & murum, "between the ditch and the wall;" others note, that the stakes or piles, driven into the ground in the ancient Roman bulwarks, were called valla; and the interstices or vacancy between them, intervalla.
