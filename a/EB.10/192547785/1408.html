LABORATORY

article · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LABORATORY, in military affairs, signifies that place where all sorts of fire-works are prepared, both for actual service and for experiments, viz. quick matches, fuzes, port-fires, grape shot, case shot, carcasses, hand-grenades, cartridges, shells filled, and fuzes fixed, wads, &c. &c.
