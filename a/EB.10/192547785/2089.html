LIEGE-POUSTIE

article · 166 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LIEGE-POUSTIE, in Scots Law, is opposed to death-bed; and signifies a person's enjoying that state of health in which only he can dispose of his property at pleasure.
