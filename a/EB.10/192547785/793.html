Stock-JOBING

sub_entry · 419 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Stock-JOBING, denotes the practice of trafficking in the public funds, or of buying and selling stock with a view to its rise or fall. The term is commonly applied to the illegal practice of buying and selling stock for time, or of accounting for the differences in the rise or fall of any particular stock for a stipulated time, whether the buyer or seller be possessed of any such real stock or not. See Stock-BROKER.
