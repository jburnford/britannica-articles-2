KEISERWERT, a town of Germany, in the circle of Westphalia, the diocese of Cologne, and the duchy of Berg; subject to the elector Palatine. The fortifications are demolished. It is seated on the Rhine, in E. Long. 6. 49. N. Lat. 51. 16.
