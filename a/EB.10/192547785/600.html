INGOLSTADT

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INGOLSTADT, a handsome town of Germany, and the strongest in Bavaria, with a famous university and a handsome church. The houses are built with stone, and the streets large. It is seated on the Danube, in E. Long. 11. 10. N. Lat. 48. 42.
