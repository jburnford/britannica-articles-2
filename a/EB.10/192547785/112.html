JACOBINES, the name assumed by a party or club at
the beginning of the French revolution, composed of
members of the national assembly. This club held its
meetings in the hall belonging to the Jacobin Friars,
from which it derived its name. For an account of the
views and influence of the Jacobin club in the French
revolution, see FRANCE.
