IRON-Sick

IRON-Sick, in the sea-language, is said of a ship or boat,

Iron-fick boat, when her bolts or nails are so eaten with rust, and so worn away, that they occasion hollows in the planks, whereby the vessel is rendered leaky.

Irritability. IRON-Wood, in Botany. See SIDEROXYLUM, BOTANY Index.
