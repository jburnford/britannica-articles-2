JUDENBURG

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JUDENBURG, a considerable town of Germany, in the circle of Austria, and capital of Upper Styria, with a handsome castle; the public buildings are a castle, a college, and two convents. It is seated on the river Meur. E. Long. 14. 25. N. Lat. 47. 10.
