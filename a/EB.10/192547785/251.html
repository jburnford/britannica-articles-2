IDIOM

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IDIOM, among grammarians, properly signifies the peculiar genius of each language, but is often used in a synonymous sense with dialect. The word is Greek, idioma, "propriety;" formed of idios, "proper; own."
