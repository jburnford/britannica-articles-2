KESSELDORF

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KESSELDORF, a village of Germany, in the circle of Upper Saxony, three miles below Dresden, remarkable for the battle gained by the king of Prussia over the Saxons, on the 15th of December 1745.
