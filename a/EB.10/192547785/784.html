INVOLUCRUM

article · 278 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: CALYX

INVOLUCRUM, among botanists, expresses that sort of cup which surrounds a number of flowers together, every one of which has beside this general cup its own particular perianthium. The involucrum consists of a multitude of little leaves disposed in a radiated manner. See CALYX.
