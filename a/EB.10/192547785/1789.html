LEBADEA

LEBADEA, or LEBADIA, an ancient town of Boeotia, on the borders of Phocis, situated between Helicon and Chæronea, near Coronæa. In it stood the oracle of Jupiter Trophonius, which whoever went to consult, descended into a subterraneous gulf.
