LEAR

article · 225 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LEAR, the name of a British king, said in old chronicles to have succeeded his father Bladud, about A. M. 3160. The story of this king and his three daughters is well known, from Shakespeare's excellent tragedy founded on it.
