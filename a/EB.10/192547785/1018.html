IULUS

article · 189 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IULUS, a son of Aescanius, born in Lavinium. In the succession to the kingdom of Alba, Æneas Sylvius, the son of Æneas and Lavinia, was preferred to him. He was, however, made chief priest.
