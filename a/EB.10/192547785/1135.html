KAZY

article · 359 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KAZY, in the East Indies, a Mahometan judge or magistrate; appointed originally by the court of Delhi to administer justice according to their written law; but particularly in matters relative to marriages, the sales of houses, and transgressions of the Koran. He attests or authenticates writings, which under his seal are admitted as the originals in proof.
