LEASH

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LEASH, among sportsmen, denotes three creatures of any kind; but chiefly grehounds, foxes, bucks, and hares.

The term leash also signifies a line to hold in a hunting dog; and a small long thong of leather, by which a falconer holds his hawk.
