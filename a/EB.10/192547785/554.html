INESSE

article · 351 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INESSE is applied to things which are actually existing.

Authors make a difference between a thing in esse, and a thing in posse: a thing that is not, but may be, they say is in posse, or potentia; but a thing apparent and visible, they say is in esse, that is, has a real being co instanti; whereas the other is casual, and at best but a possibility.
