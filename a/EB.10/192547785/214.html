JAZER

article · 524 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JAZER, or JASER, in Ancient Geography, a Levitical city in the territory of the Amorrites beyond Jordan, 10 miles to the west, or rather south-west, of Philadelphia, and 15 miles from Esbeon, and therefore situated between Philadelphia and Hebron, on the east border of the tribe of Gad, supposed to be the Jazorem of Josephus. In Jeremiah xlviii. mention is made of the sea of Jazer, that is, a lake; taken either for an effusion or overflowing of the Arnon, a lake through which it passes, or from which it takes its rise.
