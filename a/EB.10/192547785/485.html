INCOGNITO

article · 271 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INCOGNITO, or INCOG, is applied to a person who is in any place where he would not be known: but is more particularly applied to princes, or great men, who enter towns, or walk the streets, without their ordinary train or the usual marks of their distinction and quality.
