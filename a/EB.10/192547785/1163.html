KEMPTEN

KEMPTEN, a territory in the circle of Suabia, in Germany, between the bishopric of Augsbourg and the barony of Walburg. It is about 17 miles long and broad; and has no considerable place but the towns of Kempten and Kauffbeuren, which are imperial.
