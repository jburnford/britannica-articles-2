INOCULATION

article · 310 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INOCULATION, or BUDDING, in Gardening, is commonly practised upon all sorts of stone fruit; as nectarines, peaches, apricots, plums, cherries, as also upon oranges and jasmines: and indeed this is preferable to any sort of grafting for most sorts of fruit. For the method of performing it, see GARDENING Index.
