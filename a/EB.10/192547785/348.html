ILISSUS

article · 276 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ILISSUS, a river running to the east of Athens; which, with the Eridanus running on the west side, falls below the city into the sea. Sacred to the muses, called Iliassides; on whose bank their altar stood, and where the lustration in the less mysteries was usually performed.
