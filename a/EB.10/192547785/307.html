JETTE

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JETTE, the border made round the slits under a pier, in certain old bridges, being the same with stalling; consisting of a strong framing of timber filled with stones, chalk, &c. to preserve the foundations of the piers from injury.
