KOS

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KOS, in Jewish antiquity, a measure of capacity, containing about four cubic inches: this was the cup of blessing out of which they drank when they gave thanks after solemn meals, like that of the pasover.
