JOINERY

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JOINERY, the art of working in wood, or of fitting various pieces of timber together. It is called by the French menuiserie, "small work," to distinguish it from carpentry, which is employed about large and less curious works.
