LEXICON

article · 161 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LEXICON, the same with dictionary. The word is chiefly used in speaking of Greek dictionaries: it is derived from the Greek λέξ, word, diCTION; of λέγω, I speak.
