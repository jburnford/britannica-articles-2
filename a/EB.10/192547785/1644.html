LARMIER

article · 289 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LARMIER, in Architecture, a flat square member of the cornice below the cymasum, and jets out farthest; being so called from its use, which is to disperse the water, and cause it to fall at a distance from the wall, drop by drop, or as if by tears, the French word larm, signifying a tear.
