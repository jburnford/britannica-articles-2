LIBAW

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LIBAW, a sea-port town of Courland, lying on the Baltic sea, consisting entirely of wooden houses. It belongs to the duke of Courland, and is situated in E. Long. 21. 27. N. Lat. 56. 27.
