HYPERCRITIC

article · 261 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: CRITICISM

HYPERCRITIC, an over-rigid censor or critic: one who will let nothing pass, but animadverts severely on the slightest fault. See CRITICISM. The word is compounded of ὕπερ, super, "over, above, beyond;" and κρίτης, of κρίνος, judex, of κρίμα, judicio, "I judge."
