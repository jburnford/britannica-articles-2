INFAMY

article · 190 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INFAMY, in Law, is a term which extends to forgery, perjury, gross cheats, &c. by which a person is rendered incapable of being a witness or juror, even though he is pardoned for his crimes.
