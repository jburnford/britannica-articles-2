IGNORANCE, the privation or absence of knowledge. The causes of ignorance, according to Locke, are chiefly these three. 1. Want of ideas. 2. Want of a discoverable connexion between the ideas we have. 3. Want of tracing and examining our ideas. See METAPHYSICS.
