ACTÆON

article · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACTÆON, in fabulous history, the son of Aristæus and Autonoe: a great hunter. He was transformed by Diana into a stag, because he looked on her while bathing; and was devoured by his own dogs. The effects of impertinent curiosity and expensive pleasures seem to be the moral of the fable.
