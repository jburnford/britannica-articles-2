ACHICOLUM

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACHICOLUM, is used to express the furnix, the lucus, or sudatorium of the ancient baths: which was a hot room where they used to sweat. It is also called architolus. Achicolum Achillini.
