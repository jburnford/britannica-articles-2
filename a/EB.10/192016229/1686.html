ALIENS-DUTY

article · 297 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALIENS-DUTY is otherwise called petty customs, and navigation duty.—Fish dried or salted, and cod-fish or herring not caught in British vessels and cured by British subjects, pay a double aliens-duty.—On what footing aliens are permitted to import foreign commodities into Great Britain, see DUTY.
