ADAM'S Peak, a high mountain of the East Indies, in the island of Ceylon, on the top of which it is believed the first man was created. It is in the form of a sugar loaf, and terminates in a circular plain about 200 paces in diameter. The summit is covered with trees, and has a deep lake which supplies the principal rivers of the island. The mountain is seen at the distance of twenty leagues from sea. It is situated in N. Lat. 5. 55. E. Long. 80. 39. See ADAM. ADAM or ADOM, a town in the PEREA, or on the other side of the Jordan, over against Jericho, where the Jordan began to be dried up on the passage of the Israelites, (Joshua).
