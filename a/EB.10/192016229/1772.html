ALLOBROGES

ALLOBROGES, (Inscription, Livy, Velleius, Florus); from Allobroxi (Horace): a people of Gallia Narbonensis, situated between the rivers Hara and Rhodanus, and the Lacus Lemanus; commended by Cicero for their fidelity; but reproached by Horace on account of their fondness for novelty.

Novisque rebus infidelis Allobroxi. Epod. 16.
