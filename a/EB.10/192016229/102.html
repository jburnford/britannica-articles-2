ABBOTSBROMLEY, a town in Staffordshire. After the dissolution of the monasteries, it was given to the lord Paget; and has since been called Paget's Bromley. But it retains its old name in the king's books, and with regard to the fairs. W. Long. 1. 2. N. Lat. 52. 45.
