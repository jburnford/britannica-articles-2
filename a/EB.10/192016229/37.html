ABACUS Logisticus

sub_entry · 239 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABACUS Logisticus, is a rectangular triangle, whose sides, forming the right angle, contain the numbers from 1 to 60; and its area, the facta of each two of the numbers perpendicularly opposite. This is also called a canon of sexagesimals.
