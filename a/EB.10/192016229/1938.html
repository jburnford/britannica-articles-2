ALTO BASSO

article · 457 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALTO BASSO, or in ALTO et in BASSO, in Law, signifies the absolute reference of all differences, small and great, high and low, to some arbitrator or indifferent person. Pateat universis per praesentes, quod Wilhelmus Tylar de Yetton et Thomas Gouwer de Almesire, posuerunt se in Alto et in Basso, in arbitrio quatuor hominum; viz. de quadam querela pendente inter eos in curia. Nos et terram nostram altè et balse ipsius domini Regis supposuimus voluntati.
