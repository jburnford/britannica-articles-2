ACANTHOPTERYGIOUS FISHES

article · 127 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACANTHOPTERYGIOUS FISHES, a term used by Linnaeus and others for those fishes whose back fins are hard, ossaceous, and prickly.
