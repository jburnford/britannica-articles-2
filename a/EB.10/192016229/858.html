ADJUNCT

article · 345 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADJUNCT, in Music, a word which is employed to denominate the connexion or relation between the principle mode and the modes of its two-fifths, which, from the intervals that constitute the relation between them and it, are called its adjuncts.

ADJUNCT is also used to signify a colleague, or some person associated with another as an assistant.
