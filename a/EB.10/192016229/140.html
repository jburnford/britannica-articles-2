ABEL-Mizraim

sub_entry · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABEL-Mizraim, (called also the Thrething-floor of Atad), signifying the lamentation of the Egyptians in allusion to the mourning for Jacob, Gen. i. 3, 10, 11. Supposed to be near Hebron.
