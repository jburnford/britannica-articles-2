AMANICÆ PYLÆ

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMANICÆ PYLÆ (Ptolemy); AMANIDES PYLÆ (Strabo); AMANI PORTÆ (Pliny); straits or defiles in Mount Amanus, through which Darius entered Cilicia; at a greater distance from the sea than the Pylæ Ciliciae or Syriæ, through which Alexander passed.
