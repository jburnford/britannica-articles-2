ALBAZIN

article · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALBAZIN, a town of Greater Tartary, with a strong castle. It is situated upon the river Amur, or Yamour, and belongs to the Muscovites. E. Long. 103. 30. N. Lat. 54. 0.
