ABACUS et Palmule

sub_entry · 174 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABACUS et Palmule, in the Ancient Music, denote the machinery, whereby the strings of polyplectra, or instruments of many strings, were struck with a plectrum made of quills.
