ÆGAGROPILA

ÆGAGROPILA, a ball composed of hair, generated in the stomach of the chamois goat, which is similar to those found in cows, hogs, &c. There is another

Agropila || Agilops. another species of ball found in some animals, particularly horses, which is a calculous concretion.
