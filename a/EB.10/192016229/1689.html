ALIENATION in mortmain

sub_entry · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALIENATION in mortmain, is the making over lands, tenements, &c. to a body politic, or to a religious house, for which the king's license must first be obtained, otherwise the lands, &c. alienated will be forfeited.
