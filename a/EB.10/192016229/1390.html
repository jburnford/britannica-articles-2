AIRY

article · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AIRY, or AERY, among Sportsmen, a term expressing the nest of a hawk or eagle.

Army Triplicity, among Astrologers, denotes the three signs, Gemini, Libra, and Aquarius.
