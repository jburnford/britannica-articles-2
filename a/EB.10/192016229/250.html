ABRINCATARUM OFFIDUM

ABRINCATARUM OFFIDUM, in Ancient Geography, the town of the Abrincates or Abrincatui; now Avranches, in France, situated on an eminence in the south-west of Normandy, near the borders of Brittany, on the English channel. W. Long. 1. 10. N. Lat. 48. 40.
