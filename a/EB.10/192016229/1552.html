ALCIAT or ALCIATE, ANDREW, a great lawyer, who flourished in the tenth century, was born at Milan. He mixed much of polite learning in the explication of the laws, and happily drove out the barbarity of language which till then had reigned in the lectures and writings of lawyers; for which Thuanus highly praises
