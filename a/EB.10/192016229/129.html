ABDEST

article · 227 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABDEST, a Persian word, properly signifying the water placed in a basin for washing the hands; but is used to imply the legal purifications practised by the Mahometans before prayer, entering the mosque, or reading the Alcoran.
