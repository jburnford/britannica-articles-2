ALLEGHANY

article · 312 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALLEGHANY, is the most western county in Maryland, and has Pennsylvania on the north. The windings of the Patowmack river separate it from Virginia on the south, and Siding-hill Creek divides it from Washington county on the east. It contains 4809 inhabitants, including 258 slaves. Cumberland is its chief town.
