ABSYRTUS

article · 306 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABSYRTUS, in heathen mythology, the son of Ætes and Hypsea, and the brother of Medea. The latter running away with Jason, after her having assisted him in carrying off the golden fleece, was pursued by her father; when, to stop his progress, she tore Absyrtus in pieces, and scattered his limbs in his way.
