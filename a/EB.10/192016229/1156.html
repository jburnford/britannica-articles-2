AFFA

article · 182 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AFFA, a weight used on the Gold Coast of Guinea. It is equal to an ounce, and the half of it is called eggeba. Most of the blacks on the Gold Coast give these names to these weights.
