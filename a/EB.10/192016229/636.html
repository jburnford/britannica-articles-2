ACRATH, in Ancient Geography, a place in Mauritania Tingitana, now supposed to be Velez de Gomara: A fortified town in the kingdom of Fez, with a citadel and commodious harbour on the Mediterranean, scarce a mile distant from Penon de Velez, a Spanish fort. W. Long. 5. N. Lat. 34. 45.
