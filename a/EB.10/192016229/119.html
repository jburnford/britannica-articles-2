ABCOURT

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABCOURT, a town near St Germain, four leagues from Paris. Here is a brisk chalybeate water, which is also impregnated with carbonic acid and soda; and resembling the waters of Spa and Ilmington.
