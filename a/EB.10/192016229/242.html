ABRANTES

article · 280 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABRANTES, a town of Portugal, in Estremadura, seated on an eminence, in the midst of gardens and olive trees, near the river Tajo, belongs to a marquis of the same name. It contains 35,000 inhabitants, four convents, an alms-house, and an hospital. W. Long. 7. 18. N. Lat. 39. 13.
