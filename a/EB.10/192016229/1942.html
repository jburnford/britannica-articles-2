ALT-RANSTADT

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALT-RANSTADT, a town in Saxony, famous for the treaty between Charles XII. king of Sweden and Augustus elector of Saxony, in 1706, wherein the latter resigned the kingdom of Poland.
