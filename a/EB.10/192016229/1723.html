ALLEGHANY County

sub_entry · 224 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALLEGHANY County, in Pennsylvania, extends from the junction of the river of that name with the Ohio, where its chief town, Pittsburgh, is situated, to the New-York line. It contains 10,309 inhabitants, including 159 slaves.
