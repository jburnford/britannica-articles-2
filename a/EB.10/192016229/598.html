ACOMAC

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACOMAC, the name of a county in Virginia. It is on the eastern side of Chesapeake bay, on a slip of land, by the Virginians called the eastern shore. It contains 13,959 inhabitants.
