AID

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AID, in Law, denotes a petition made in court to call in help from another person who has interest in land, or any thing contested.

Ain-de-Camp, in military affairs, an officer employed to receive and carry the orders of a general.
