ACCELERATION

article · 240 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACCELERATION is also applied in the ancient astronomy, in respect of the fixed stars. This acceleration was the difference between the revolution of the primum mobile and the solar revolution; which was computed at 3 minutes and 56 seconds.
