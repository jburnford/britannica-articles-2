ALCALA DE GUADEIRA

ALCALA DE GUADEIRA, a small town of Spain, in Andalusia, upon the river Guadeira. Here are abundance of springs, from whence they convey water to Seville by an aqueduct. W. Long. 6. 16. N. Lat. 37. 15.
