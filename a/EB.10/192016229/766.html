ADAMI POMUM

article · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADAMI POMUM, in Anatomy, a protuberance in the fore part of the throat, formed by the os hyoides. It is thought to be so called upon a strange conceit, that a piece of the forbidden apple, which Adam ate, stuck by the way, and occasioned it.
