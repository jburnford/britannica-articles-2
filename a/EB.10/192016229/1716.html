ALLANTOIS

ALLANTOIS, or ATLANTOIDES, a thin transparent bag investing the foetus of quadrupeds, as cows, goats, sheep, &c. filled with an urinous liquor conveyed to it from the bladder of the young animals by means of the urachus. See ANATOMY INDEX.
