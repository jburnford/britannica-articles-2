ADROGATION

article · 414 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADROGATION, in Roman antiquities, a species of adoption, whereby a person who was capable of choosing for himself was admitted by another into the relation of a son. The word is compounded of ad, "to," and rogare, "to ask;" on account of a question put in the ceremony of it, Whether the adopter would take such a person for his son? and another to the adoptive, Whether he consented to become such a person's son?
