AGONOTHETA

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AGONOTHETA, or AGONOTHETES, in Grecian antiquity, was the president or superintendent of the sacred games; who not only defrayed the expence attending them, but inspected the manners and discipline of the athlete, and adjudged the prizes to the victors.
