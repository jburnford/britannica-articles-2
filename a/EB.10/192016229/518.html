ACHATES

ACHATES, in Ancient Geography, a river of Sicily, now the Drillo; which runs from north to south, almost parallel with, and at no great distance from, the Gela; and rises in the north of the territory of Noto. It gave name to the achates, or agate, said to be first found there.
