ACEPHALUS

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACEPHALUS, an obsolete term for the tenia or tape-worm, which was long supposed to be accephalous. The first who gave it a head was Tulpius: and after him, Fehr: The former even makes it biceps, or two-headed.
