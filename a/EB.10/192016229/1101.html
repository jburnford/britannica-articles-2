AERIA, or EERIA, in Ancient Geography, the ancient name of Egypt. The scholiast on Apollonius Rhodius, says, that not only Thebæ, but Egypt, was called Hæra by the Greeks, which Eusebius also confirms: and hence Apollinaris, in his translation of the 114th Psalm, uses it for Egypt. Hesychius applies this name to Ethiopia.
