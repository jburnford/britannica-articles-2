ALBA Helviorum

sub_entry · 281 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALBA Helviorum, or Albaugusta, in Ancient Geography, afterwards called Vivarium, now Viviers, in the south-east of Languedoc, on the Rhone. In the lower age the inhabitants were called Albenfes, and their city Civitas Albenfium, in the Notitia Galliae. E. Long. 4. 45. Lat. 44. 50.
