ABBOTS-LANGLEY, a village in Herts, four miles from St Alban's, famous as the birth-place of Pope Adrian IV.

ABBREVIATE of ADJUDICATIONS, in Scotts Law, an abstract or abridgment of a decree of adjudication, which is recorded in a register kept for that purpose.
