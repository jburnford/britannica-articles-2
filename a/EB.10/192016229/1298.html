AGRIONIA

article · 290 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AGRIONIA, in Grecian antiquity, festivals annually celebrated by the Bœotians in honour of Bacchus. At these festivals the women pretended to search after Bacchus as a fugitive; and, after some time, gave over the inquiry, saying that he had fled to the Muses, and was concealed among them.
