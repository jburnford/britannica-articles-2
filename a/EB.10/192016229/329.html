German ACACIA

sub_entry · 359 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

German ACACIA, the juice of unripe floss inspissated nearly to dryness over a gentle fire, care being taken to prevent its burning. It is moderately astringent, similar to the Egyptian acacia, for which it has been commonly substituted in the shops. It is given in fluxes, and other disorders where styptic medicines are indicated, from a scruple to a drachm.
