ALTORF, a town of the circle of Franconia, in Germany.

Altorf Germany. It has a botanical garden, with a great variety of plants, an anatomical theatre, and a handsome library. It is subject to the house of Brandenburg; and is seated on the confines of Bavaria, 15 miles from Nuremberg. E. Long. 11. 7. N. Lat. 49. 25.
