ABAVO

article · 156 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABAVO, in Botany, a synonyme of the ADANSONIA. ABB, a term among clothiers applied to the yarn of a weaver's warp. They say also Abb-wool in the same sense.
