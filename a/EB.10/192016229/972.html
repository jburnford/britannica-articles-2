ADVERSATIVE

article · 214 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADVERSATIVE, in Grammar, a word expressing some difference between what goes before and what follows it. Thus, in the phrase, he is an honest man, but a great enthusiast, the word but is an adversative conjunction.
