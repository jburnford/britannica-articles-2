ALMADIE

article · 196 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALMADIE is also the name of a kind of long boats, fitted out at Calicut, which are eighty feet in length, and six or seven in breadth. They are exceedingly swift, and are otherwise called cathuri.
