ALESHAM

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALESHAM, a small neat town in Norfolk. It is 15 miles north of Norwich, and 121 north-east by north of London. E. Long. 0. 30. N. Lat. 52. 53. The town consists of about 400 houses.
