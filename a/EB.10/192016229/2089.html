AMELIA Isle

sub_entry · 395 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMELIA Isle, on the coast of East Florida, lies about seven leagues north of St Augustine, and very near Talbot island on the south, at the mouth of St John's river. It is 13 miles long and two broad, is very fertile, and has an excellent harbour. Its north end lies opposite Cumberland island, between which and Amelia Isle is the entry into St Mary's river, in N. Lat. 30. 52. W. Long. 67. 23.
