AMERCEMENT

article · 381 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMERCEMENT, or AMERCIAMENT, in Law, a pecuniary punishment imposed on offenders at the mercy of the court. It differs from a fine in being imposed arbitrarily, in proportion to the fault; whereas a fine is a certain punishment settled expressly by some statute.
