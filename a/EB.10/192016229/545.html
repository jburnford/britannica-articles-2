ACHMET GEDUC

article · 373 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACHMET GEDUC, a famous general under Mahomet II. and Bajazet II. in the 15th century. When Mahomet II. died, Bajazet and Zezan both claimed the throne: Achmet sided with the former, and by his bravery

Achmet, bravery and conquest fixed the crown on his head. But Achmin, Bajazet took away his life; shining virtue being always an unpardonable crime in the eyes of a tyrant.
