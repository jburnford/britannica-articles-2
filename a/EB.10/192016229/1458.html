ALBA Pompeia

ALBA Pompeia, in Ancient Geography, on the river Ceba, now Ceva, in Liguria, the birth-place of the emperor Pertinax; a colony either established at first by Pompey, or re-established by him after having been before settled by Scipio. The inhabitants were called Albenfes Pompeiani. At this day the town is simply called Alba, without any epithet.
