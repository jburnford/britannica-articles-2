ACROCORINTHUS

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACROCORINTHUS, in Ancient Geography, a high and steep hill, hanging over the city of Corinth, which was taken within the walls, as an acropolis, or citadel. On its top stood a temple of Venus; and lower down issued the fountain Priene.
