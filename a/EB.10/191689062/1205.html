RECIPROCAL

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RECIPROCAL, in Mathematics, is applied to quantities which multiplied together produce unity. Thus

\frac{1}{x} and x, \frac{1}{y} and y, are reciprocal quantities. Likewise

\frac{1}{x} is said to be the reciprocal of x, which is again the reciprocal of \frac{1}{x}.
