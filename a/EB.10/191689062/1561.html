RHE

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHE, or REE, a little island in the bay of Biscay, near the coast of Aunis in France. It was taken during the war with France which ended in 1763, in the expedition commanded by Hawke and Mordaunt.
