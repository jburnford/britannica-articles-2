POMOERIUM Proferre

sub_entry · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

POMOERIUM Proferre, signifies to extend or enlarge a city, which could not be done by any, but those who had taken away some part of an enemy's country in war. But this qualification was sometimes dispensed with. Pomcerium is quasi pone mæniam, "behind the walls."
