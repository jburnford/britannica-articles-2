PRE-EMPTION

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PRE-EMPTION, a privilege anciently allowed the king's parveyor, of having the choice and first buying of corn and other provisions for the king's house: but taken away by the statute 19 Car. II.
