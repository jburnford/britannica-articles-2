QUINTEN, a town of France, in Bretagne, with a handsome castle. It is seated in a valley near the river Guy, and near a large forest of the same name, eight miles south of St Brieux, and 200 west of Paris. It had

formerly the title of a duchy. W. Long. 2. 40. N. Quinten Lat. 48. 26.
