To RACK Wines, &c

sub_entry · 224 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

To RACK Wines, &c. To draw them off from their lees, after having flood long enough to ebb and fettle. Hence rack-vintage is frequently ufed for the fecond voyage our wine-merchants ufed to make into France for racked wines.
