REHEARSAL

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

REHEARSAL, in Music and the Drama, an essay or experiment of some composition, generally made in private, previous to its representation or performance in public, in order to render the actors and performers more perfect in their parts.
