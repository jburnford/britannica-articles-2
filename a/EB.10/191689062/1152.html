RAVENSBURG

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RAVENSBURG, a free and imperial town of Germany, in Algow, in the circle of Suabia. It is well built, and the public structures are handsome. The inhabitants are partly Protestants and partly Papists. It is seated on the river Chens, in E. Long. 9. 46. N. Lat. 47. 44.
