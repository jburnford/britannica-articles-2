Quadrile QUADREL

sub_entry · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Quadrile QUADREL, in Building, a kind of artificial stone, so called from its being perfectly square. The quadrels are made of a chalky earth, &c. and dried in the shade for two years. These were formerly in great request among the Italian architects.
