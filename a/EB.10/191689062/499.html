PRINOS

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PRINOS, in Botany, a genus of the monogynia order, belonging to the hexandria class of plants; and in the natural method ranking under the 43d order, Dumosa. The calyx is sexfid; the corolla monopetalous, and rotaceous; the belly hexaspermous.
