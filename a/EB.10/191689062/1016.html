RACONI, a populous town of Italy, in Piedmont, feated in a pleafant plain, on the road from Savillan to Turin, on the rivers Grana and Macra. It belongs to the prince of Carignan, who has a handfome caftle here. It is fix miles from Savillan, and fix from Carignan. E. Long. 7. 46. N. Lat. 44. 39.
