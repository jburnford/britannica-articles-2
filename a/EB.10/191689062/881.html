QUART

QUART, a measure of capacity, being the fourth part of some other measure. The English quart is the fourth part of the gallon, and contains two pints. The quart of the Romans was the fourth part of their congius. The French have various quarts, besides their quart or pot consisting of two pints, and are distinguished by the whole of which they are quarters; as quart de maïs, and quart de boisson.
