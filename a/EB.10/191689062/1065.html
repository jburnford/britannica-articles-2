RAMEKINS, a fortress of the United Netherlands, on the south coast of the island of Walcherin, in the province of Zeeland. One of the cautionary towns given to Queen Elizabeth for the repayment of the charges she had been at for the defence of this republic in its infancy. Four miles east of Flushing; in N. Lat. 51. 34. E. Long. 4. 24.
