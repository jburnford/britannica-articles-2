APION

article · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

APION, a famous grammarian, born in Egypt, was a professor at Rome in the reign of Tiberius. He had all the arrogance of a mere pedant, and amused himself with difficult and insignificant inquiries. One of his principal works are his Antiquities of Egypt.
