ARISTOXENUS

article · 245 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARISTOXENUS, one of the most ancient musical writers, was born at Tarentum, a city in Magna Græcia, now Calabria. He was the son of a musician, and it appears that he lived about the time of Alexander the Great and his successors. His Harmonics.
