ARAUCO, a fortress and town of Chili, in South America; situated in a fine valley, on a river of the same name. The bravery of the natives drove the Spaniards out of their country even without fire arms. W. Long. 73. 20. S. Lat. 38. 10. See ARAUCANIA, SUPPLEMENT.
