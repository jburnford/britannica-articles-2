ARBORIBONES

article · 182 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARBORIBONSES, in modern history, priests of Japan, who live an erratic life, and subsist on alms. They dwell in caverns, and cover their heads with bonnets made of the bark of trees.
