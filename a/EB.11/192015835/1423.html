ARNUS

article · 516 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARNUS, now Arno, a very rapid river of Tuscany, which it divides, and in its course washes Florence and Pisa; rising in the Apennines, to the east of Florence, near a village called S. Maria della Gratie, on the borders of Romagna, 15 miles to the west of the sources of the Tiber; and then turning southwards towards Arretium, it is there increased by the lakes of the Clasi; after which it runs eastward, dividing Florence into two parts, and at length washing Pisa, falls eight miles below it into the Tuscan sea.
