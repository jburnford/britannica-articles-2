ARCHIATER, ARCHIATRUS

article · 174 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHIATER, ARCHIATRUS, properly denotes chief physician of a prince who retains several. The word is formed of αρχος, principium, "chief;" and ιατρος, medicus, a "physician."
