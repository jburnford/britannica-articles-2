ASHANTEE

article · 422 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASHANTEE, an extensive territory of western Africa, situated immediately behind the states which occupy the Gold coast. This kingdom, which was but lately known to Europeans, seems to be the most powerful, civilized, and commercial of any on the western coast of Africa. Since 1806 they have had various wars with the tribes on the coast, and from their superior courage and skill, are likely to conquer this whole region.
