APHÆRESIS

article · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

APHÆRESIS, in Grammar, a figure by which a letter or syllable is cut off from the beginning of a word. Thus ciconia, by aphæresis, is written omia; contemnere, temnere; omittere, mittere, &c.

A like retrenchment at the end of a word is called APOCOPÉ.
