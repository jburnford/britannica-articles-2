ANIMAL

article · 195 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANIMAL, used adjectively, denotes any thing belonging to, or partaking of, the nature of animals. Thus, animal actions, those that are peculiar to animals; such are sensation and muscular motion.
