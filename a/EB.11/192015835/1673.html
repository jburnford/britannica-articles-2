ASPERIFOLIÆ PLANTÆ

article · 368 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASPERIFOLIÆ PLANTÆ, rough-leaved plants; the name of a class in Hermannus, Boerhaave, and Ray's methods, consisting of plants which have four naked seeds, and whose leaves are rough to the touch.

In Tournefort's System, these plants constitute the third section or order of the second class; and in Linnaeus's Sexual Method, they make part of the pentandria monogynia.
