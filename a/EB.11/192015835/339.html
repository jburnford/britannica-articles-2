ANDROGYNOUS

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANDROGYNOUS, in Zoology, an appellation given to animals which have both the male and female sex in the same individual.—In Botany, the term is applied to such plants as bear both male and female flowers on the same root.
