ASCALON

article · 421 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASCALON, an ancient city, and one of the five satrapies or principalities of the Philistines; situated on the Mediterranean, 43 miles to the south-west of Jerusalem (Antonine), between Azotus to the north and Gaza to the south. The birthplace of Herod the Great, hence surnamed Ascalonita (Stephanus). Famous for its scallions, which take name from this town (Strabo, Pliny). Now Sealona. E. Long. 34. 30. N. Lat. 31. 30.
