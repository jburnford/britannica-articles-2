ANTELUCAN

article · 239 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTELUCAN, in ecclesiastical writers, is applied to things done in the night or before day. We find frequent mention of the antelucan assemblies (Cactus antelucani) of the ancient Christians, in times of persecution, for religious worship.
