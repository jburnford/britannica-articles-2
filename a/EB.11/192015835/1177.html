ARCHILUTE, ARCILEUTO

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARCHILUTE, ARCILEUTO, a long and large lute, having its bass strings lengthened after the manner of the theorbo, and each row doubled, either with a little octave or an unison. It is used by the Italians for playing a thorough bass.
