ASEKI

article · 203 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASEKI, or ASERAI, the name which the Turks give to the favourite sultanas who have brought forth sons. These are greatly distinguished above others in their apartments, attendants, pensions, and honours.
