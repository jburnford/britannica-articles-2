ARPENT

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARPENT, signifies an acre or furlong of ground; and according to the old French account in Doomsdaybook, 100 perches make an arpent. The most ordinary acre, called Parpent de France, is 100 perches square: but some account it but half an acre.
