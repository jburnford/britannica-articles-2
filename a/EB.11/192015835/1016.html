AQUÆ Quintianæ

sub_entry · 184 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AQUÆ Quintianæ, put by Ptolemy in room of the Aquæ Cilinaæ of Antonine. Now supposed to be Sarria, a town of Galicia, on a rivulet of the same name, three leagues to the south of Lugo.
