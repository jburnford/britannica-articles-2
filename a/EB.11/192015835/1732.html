ASSITHMENT

article · 211 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASSITHMENT, a wiregeld, or composition, by a pecuniary mulct; from the preposition ad, and the Sax. sith, vice; quod vice suppliciis ad expiandum delictum solvitur.
