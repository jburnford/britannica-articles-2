AMETHYSTEA, AMETHYST. See BOTANY Index.

AMETHYSTINE is applied, in antiquity, to a kind of purple garment dyed of the hue of amethyst. In this sense amethystine differed from Tyrian as well as from hyacinthine purple, being a kind of medium between.
