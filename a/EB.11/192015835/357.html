ANDUXAR

article · 436 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANDUXAR, a city in the province of Andalusia, in Spain, situated on the Guadalquivir. It is defended by a good castle, and contains about 14,000 inhabitants. It is adorned with handsome churches and several religious houses, and inhabited by many families of high rank. The land about it abounds in corn, wine, oil, honey, and fruit of all sorts; and the inhabitants carry on a considerable trade in silk. W. Long. 4. 2. N. Lat. 37. 45.
