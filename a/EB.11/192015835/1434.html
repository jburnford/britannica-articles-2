AROPH Paracelsi

sub_entry · 296 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AROPH Paracelsi, a name given to a kind of chemical flowers, probably of the same nature with the Ens Veneris, elegantly prepared by sublimation from equal quantities of lapis hematites and sal ammoniac.

AROPH is also a term frequently used by Paracelsus in a sense synonymous with lithontriptic.
