ARMUYDEN

ARMUYDEN, a sea-port town of the United Provinces, in the island of Walcheren, formerly very flourishing; but now inconsiderable, the sea having stopped up the harbour. The salt works are its chief resource. E. Long. 3. 40. N. Lat. 51. 30.
