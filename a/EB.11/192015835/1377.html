ARMENTIERS

article · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARMENTIERS, a small handsome town in the department of the North in France, containing 7600 inhabitants. It was taken by Lewis XIV. in 1667, who dismantled it; and it now belongs to the French. It is seated on the river Lis. E. Long. 3. 3. N. Lat. 50. 40.
