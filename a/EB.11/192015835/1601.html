ASCHAFFENBURG

article · 332 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASCHAFFENBURG, a town of Germany, seated on the river Maine, in the circle of the Lower Rhine, formerly belonging to the elector of Mentz, but now to the king of Bavaria. It is memorable for being the place where the king of Great Britain took up his quarters the night before the battle of Dettingen. E. Long. 9. 5. N. Lat. 49. 54.
