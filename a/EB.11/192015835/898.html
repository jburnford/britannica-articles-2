APOSTATA CAPIENDO

article · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

APOSTATA CAPIENDO, in the English Law, a writ that formerly lay against a person who, having entered into some order of religious, broke out again, and wandered up and down the country.
