ANNULET

article · 224 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANNULET, a little circle, born as a charge in coats of arms, as also added to them as a difference. Among the Romans it represented liberty and nobility. It also denotes strength and eternity, by reason of its circular form.
