ARISTOTELIA

article · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARISTOTELIA, in antiquity, annual feasts celebrated by the citizens of Stagyra, in honour of Aristotle, who was born there; and in gratitude for his having procured from Alexander the rebuilding and repopling of that city, which had been demolished by King Philip.
