ARTICULATION

article · 296 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARTICULATION, in Botany, is the connexion of parts that consist of joints or knees, such as the pods of French honeysuckles, which when ripe divide into so many parts as there are knees or joints; also those parts of plants which swell into nodes or joints, and which usually send forth branches.
