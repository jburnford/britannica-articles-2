ASSANCALA

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASSANCALA, a strong town in Armenia, near the river Arras, in the road between Erzerum and Erivan, and noted for its hot baths. It stands on a high hill, and has walls round it, strengthened with towers. E. Long. 41. 30. N. Lat. 39. 46.
