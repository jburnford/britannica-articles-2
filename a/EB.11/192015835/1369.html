ARMATURA

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARMATURA is also a denomination given to the soldiers in the emperor's retinue. Of these we find two schools, mentioned in the Notitia Imperii, called the armaturæ seniores and armaturæ juniores. Their commander was entitled tribunus armaturarum.
