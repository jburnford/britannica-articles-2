AQUÆ Convenarum, a hamlet of Gaul, in Aquitaine, (Antonine), and on the borders of the Convenæ, or le Cominge, at the foot of the Pyrenees, near the source of the Garonne. Now Bagnères. W. Long. 3. 39. N. Lat. 42. 20.
