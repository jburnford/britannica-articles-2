ARIA

article · 196 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARIA, one of the ancient names of Thrace, (Stephanus); that is, martial, from the character of the people, whose country Euripides calls the residence of Mars, and Sophocles his place of nativity.
