AMPURIAS

AMPURIAS, the capital of the territory of Ampurdan, in Catalonia, seated at the mouth of the river Flavia, in E. Long. 2. 56. N. Lat. 42. 5. The land about it is barren, full of briars and bulrushes, except in some places which produce flax.
