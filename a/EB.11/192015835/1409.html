ARNAY-LE-DUC

ARNAY-LE-DUC, a town of France, in the department of Cote d'Or, which carries on a pretty good trade. It is seated on the Auxois, in a valley near the river Aroux. E. Long. 4. 26. N. Lat. 47. 7.
