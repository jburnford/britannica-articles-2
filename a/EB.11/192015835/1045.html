AQUINUM, in Ancient Geography, a large municipal town, and a Roman colony on the borders of the Samnites, washed by the river Melpha (Strabo). The inhabitants are called Aquines. Now Aquino, but

almost in ruins, in the territory of Lavoro. E. Long. 17. 11. N. Lat. 41. 35.
