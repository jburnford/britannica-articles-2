ANTIMERIA

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTIMERIA, in Grammar, a figure whereby one part of speech is used for another: e. g. velle suam cuique est, for voluntas sua cuique est; also populus latè rex, for populus latè regnans.
