ANTHERA

article · 300 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANTHERA, in Botany, that part of the stamen which is fixed on the top of the filamentum, within the corolla: it contains the pollen or fine dust, which, when mature, it emits for the impregnation of the plant, according to LINNÆUS. The APEX of RAY, TOURNEF. and RIVIN.; Capsula staminis, of MALPIGHI.
