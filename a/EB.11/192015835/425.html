ANIAN

article · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ANIAN, a name sometimes given to the strait lying between the north-east of Asia and the north-west of America.

ANIAN is also the name of a barren sandy desert lying on the east coast of Africa. It is so excessively hot and otherwise inhospitable, that it contains but very few inhabitants, except some wandering Arabs who live in camps.
