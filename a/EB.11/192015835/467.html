ANNANO, a strong fort of Italy, in the duchy of Milan.

Abj. Milan. It has been twice taken by the French; but
Anne. was restored to the duke of Savoy in 1706. It is seated
on the river Tanaro, in E. Long. 8. 30. N. Lat.
44. 40.
