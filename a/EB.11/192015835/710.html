ANTIRRHINIUM

ANTIRRHINIUM, in Ancient Geography, a promontory at the mouth of the Corinthian bay, where it is scarce a mile broad, and where it separates Ætolia from the Peloponnesus; so called from its opposite situation to Rhium in Peloponnesus (Pliny): both are now called the Dardanelles of Lepanto.
