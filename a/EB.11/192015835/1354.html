ARM

article · 179 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ARM, in Geography, is used for the branch of a sea or river. Italy and Sicily are only parted by an arm of the sea. St George's arm in the Mediterranean is the Thracian Bosphorus.
