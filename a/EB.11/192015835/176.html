ANADROMUS

article · 175 chars · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: SALMO

ANADROMUS, among ichthyologists, a name given to such fishes as go from the sea to the fresh water at stated seasons, and return back again; such as the salmon, &c. See SALMO.
