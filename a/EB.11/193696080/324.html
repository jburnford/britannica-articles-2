AUGUSTINIAN

article · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUGUSTINIAN are also those divines who maintain, on the authority of St Augustine, that GRACE is effectual from its nature, absolutely and morally, and not relatively and gradually. They are divided into rigid and relaxed.
