BERDASH

article · 212 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BERDASH, in antiquity, was a name formerly used in England for a certain kind of neck-dress; and hence a person who made or sold such neckcloths was called a berdasher, from which is derived our word haberdasher.
