BIUMBRES

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BIUMBRES, in Geography, an appellation given to the inhabitants of the torrid zone, by reason, at two different seasons of the year, their shadows are projected two different ways. The biumbres are the same with those otherwise denominated amphiscii.
