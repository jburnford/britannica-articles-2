BEVERAGE

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BEVERAGE, in a general sense, signifies a drink: hence nectar is said to be the beverage of the gods. In writers of the middle age, beverage, beveragium, or bi-beragium, denotes money to give to an artificer, or other person, to drink, over and above his hire or wages.
