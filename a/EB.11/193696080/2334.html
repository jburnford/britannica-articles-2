BLOCZIL

BLOCHZIL, a fortress of Overijssel in the United Provinces, seated on the river A, at the place where it falls into the Zuyder Zee. It has a port sufficient to contain 200 vessels, and serves to defend those ships that cross the sea. Its fortifications, which were considerable, have been allowed to fall to decay. E. Long. 6. 0. N. Lat. 52. 44.
