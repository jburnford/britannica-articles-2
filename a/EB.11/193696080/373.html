AURACH

AURACH, a town of Germany with a good castle, in the kingdom of Wirtemberg. It is the usual residence of the youngest sons of the house of Wirtemberg, and is seated at the foot of a mountain on the rivulet Ermet, nine miles east of Warzach. E. Long. 9. 20. N. Lat. 48. 25.
