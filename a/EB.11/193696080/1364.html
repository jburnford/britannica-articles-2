BEAN-Flour

sub_entry · 154 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BEAN-Flour, called by the Romans lomentum, was
of some repute among the ancient ladies as a cos-
metic, wherewith to smooth the skin, and take away
wrinkles.
