BIZOCHI

article · 183 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BIZOCHI, or BISOCHI, in Church History, certain heretical monks, said to have assumed the religious habit contrary to the canons, rejected the sacraments, and maintained other errors.
