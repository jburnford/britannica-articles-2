AUGUST, (augustus), in a general sense, something majestic, venerable, or sacred. The appellation was first conferred by the Roman senate upon Octavius, after his being confirmed by them in the sovereign power. It was conceived as expressing something divine, or elevated above the pitch of mankind, being derived from the verb augeo, "I increase," tanquam supra humanam sortem auctus. See AUGUSTUS.
