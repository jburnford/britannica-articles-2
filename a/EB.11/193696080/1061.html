BARRE, LOUIS FRANÇOIS JOSEPH DE LA, of Tournay, author of several works printed at Paris. Amongst others, Imper. Orientale, Recueil des Médailles des Empereurs, "Memoirs for the history of France," &c. He died in 1738.
