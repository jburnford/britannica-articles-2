BLACK-Books, a name given to those which treat of necromancy, or, as some call it, negromancy. The black-book of the English monasteries was a detail of the scandalous enormities practised in religious houses, compiled by order of the visitors under King Henry VIII. to blacken, and thus hasten their dissolution.
