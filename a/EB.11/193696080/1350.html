BEALT, BEALTH, or Builth

BEALT, BEALTH, or Builth, a town of Brecknockshire in South Wales, pleasantly seated on the river Wye. It contained 815 inhabitants in 1811, who are chiefly employed in the manufacture of stockings. W. Long. 4. 10. N. Lat. 52. 4.
