BLECHINGLY, a town of Surrey in England, which sends two members to parliament, and the bailiff who returns the members is chosen annually at the lord of the manor's court. The town stands on a hill, and contained 1116 inhabitants in 1811. W. Long. o. 15. N. Lat. 51. 20.
