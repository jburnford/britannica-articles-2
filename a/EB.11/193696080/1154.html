BASINGSTOKE

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BASINGSTOKE, a corporation town of Hampshire in England, and a great thoroughfare on the western road. It is seated on a small brook, and had 2656 inhabitants in 1811. W. Long. 1. 10. N. Lat. 51. 20.
