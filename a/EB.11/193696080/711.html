BALANCER

article · 201 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BALANCER, in the history of insects, a style, or oblong body, ending in a protuberance or head, found under each wing of the two-winged flies; these, it is supposed, serve to poise the body of the fly.
