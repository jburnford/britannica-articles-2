ATOCK, the capital of a province of the same name in the north-western part of Hindostan. It is supposed to be the Taxila of the Greeks, and the place where Alexander, Tamerlane, and Nadir Shah crossed the Indus. Formerly nobody was permitted to enter the fortress without a passport from the Mogul himself. E. Long. 71. 55. N. Lat. 33. 59.
