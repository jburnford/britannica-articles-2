BECK

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BECK, or BEXE, a word which imports a small stream of water issuing from some burn or spring. Hence Hell-becks, little brooks in the rough and wild mountains about Richmond near Lancashire, so called on account of their ghastliness and depth.
