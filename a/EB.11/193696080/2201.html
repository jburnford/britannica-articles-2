BLADDER

article · 238 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BLADDER, in Anatomy, a thin expanded membranous body, found in several parts of an animal, serving as a receptacle of some juice, or of some liquid excrement; from whence it takes various denominations, as urine-bladder, gall-bladder, &c.
