ASTEISM

article · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ASTEISM, in Rhetoric, a genteel irony, or handsome way of deriding another. Such, e. gr. is that of Virgil:

Qui Bævium non odit, amet tua carmina, Mævi, Sc.

Diomed places the characteristic of this figure, or species of irony, in that it is not gross and rustic, but ingenious and polite.
