BLACK-Leather

sub_entry · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: LEATHER

BLACK-Leather, is that which has passed the curriers hands, where, from the russet, as it was left by the tanners, it is become black, by having been scored and rubbed three times on the grain-side with copperas water. See LEATHER.
