BILIOUS

article · 186 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BILIOUS, in general, denotes something belonging to, or partaking of, the nature of bile. Hence,

Bilious Fevers are those occasioned by the over-copiousness or bad qualities of the bile.
