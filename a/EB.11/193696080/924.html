BARBACAN, or BARBICAN

Cross-references: CASTLE

BARBACAN, or BARBICAN, an outer defence or fortification to a city or castle, used especially as a fence to the city or walls; also an aperture made in the wall of a fortress, to fire through upon the enemy. See CASTLE.
