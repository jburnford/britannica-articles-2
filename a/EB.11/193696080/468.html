AXILLA

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AXILLA, in Botany, is the space comprehended between the stems of plants and their leaves. Hence we say those flowers grow in the axillæ of the leaves; i. e. at the base of the leaves, or just within the angle of their pedicles.
