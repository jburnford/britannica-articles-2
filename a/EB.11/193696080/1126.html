BASE, in Law. Base estate, such as base tenants have in their hands. Base tenure, the holding by villeinage, or other customary services; as distinguished from the higher tenures in copite, or by military service. Base fee, is to hold in fee at the will of the lord, as distinguished from soccage tenure. Base court, any court not of record.
