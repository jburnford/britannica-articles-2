BENDER

article · 248 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BENDER, a town of Bessarabia in European Russia, seated on the river Niester, in E. Long. 29. 5. N. Lat. 46. 40. It is remarkable for being the place of retreat of Charles XII. after he was defeated by the Russians at the battle of Pultowa in 1709.
