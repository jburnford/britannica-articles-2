AUBAINE

article · 402 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUBAINE, in the old customs of France, a right vested in the king, by virtue of which he claimed the inheritance of all foreigners that died within his dominions, notwithstanding of any testament the deceased could make. An ambassador was not subject to the right of aubaine; and the Swiss, Savoyards, Scots, and Portuguese were also exempted. This ancient privilege of the crown was abolished in 1819.
