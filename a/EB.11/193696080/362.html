AUNE

article · 180 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUNE, a long measure used in France to measure cloths, stuffs, ribbons, &c. At Rouen, it is equal to one English ell; at Calais, to 1.52; at Lyons, to 1.061; and at Paris, to 0.95.
