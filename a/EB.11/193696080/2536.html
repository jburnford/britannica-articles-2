BOMBARD

article · 282 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOMBARD, a piece of ordnance anciently in use, exceedingly short and thick, and with a very large mouth. There have been bombard which have thrown a ball of 300 pounds weight. They made use of cranes to load them. The bombard is by some called basilisk, and by the Dutch donberbass.
