BALÉ Goods

sub_entry · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BALÉ Goods, among the English merchants, are all such as are imported or exported in bales; but the French give that name to certain hardwares and other sorts of merchandise which come to Paris, and are commonly made by bad workmen of different materials.
