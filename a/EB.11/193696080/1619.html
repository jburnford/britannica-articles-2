BELTZ

article · 179 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BELTZ, or BELZO, a province of Poland, which fell to the share of Austria in 1795. It was afterwards annexed to Bonaparte's kingdom of Poland, but was restored to Austria in 1815.
