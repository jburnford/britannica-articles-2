BALLATOONS

article · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BALLATOONS, large heavy luggage-boats used for carrying wood by the river from Astracan and the Caspian sea to Moscow. These will carry from 100 to 200 tons, and have from 100 to 120 men employed to row and tow them along.
