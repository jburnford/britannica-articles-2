Solemn BAPTISM

sub_entry · 176 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Solemn BAPTISM, that conferred at stated seasons; such, in the ancient church, were the Paschal baptism,

and that at Whitsuntide. This is sometimes also called general baptism.
