BEAUNE

article · 388 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BEAUNE, a handsome town of France, in the department of Coté d'Or, remarkable for its excellent wine, and for an hospital founded here in 1443. Its collegiate church is also one of the finest in France; the great altar is adorned with a table enriched with jewels; and its organs are placed on a piece of architecture which is the admiration of the curious. E. Long. 4. 50. N. Lat. 47. 2.
