BLOODY-Sweat

sub_entry · 419 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BLOODY-Sweat. Many instances of this are recorded, in which it has been owing to bodily disorder, or extreme mental agitation and agony. See particularly Aristotle's Hist. Animal. lib. iii. cap. 19. apud Oper. tom. i. Thuanus Hist. Temp. &c. lib. ii. apud Oper. tom. i. Melanges d'Histoire et de Littérature, &c. par M. V. Marville, tom. iii. p. 149. Acta Physico-Med. Norimbergae, vol. i. p. 84. and vol. viii. p. 428.
