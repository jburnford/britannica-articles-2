BED-Moulding

sub_entry · 207 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BED-Moulding, in Architecture, a term used for those members of a cornice which are placed below the coronet; and now usually consist of an ogee, a list, a large boulaine, and another list under the coronet.
