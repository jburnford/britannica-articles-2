AUSPEX

article · 201 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUSPEX, a name originally given those who were afterwards denominated augurs. In which sense the word is supposed to be formed from avis, "bird," and inspicere, "to inspect;" auspices, q. d. avispices.
