AVERTI

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AVERTI, in Horsemanship, is applied to a regular step or motion enjoined in the lessons. In this sense they say pas averti, sometimes pas ecoute, and pas d'ecole, which all denote the same. The word is mere French, and signifies advised.
