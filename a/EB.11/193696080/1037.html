BARNSLEY

BARNSLEY, or BLACK BARNSLEY, a town of the west riding of Yorkshire, seated on the side of a hill, and five furlongs in length, and containing 5014 inhabitants in 1811. W. Long. 1. 20. N. Lat. 53. 35.
