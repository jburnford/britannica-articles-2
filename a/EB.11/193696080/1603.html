BELLUNESE

article · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BELLUNESE, a territory of Italy, formerly belonging to the Venetians, but now to the Austrians. It lies between Friuli, Cadorino, Feltrino, the bishopric of Trent and Tirol. It has good iron mines, but the only considerable place is Belluno.
