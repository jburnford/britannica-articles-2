BABYLONIAN

article · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BABYLONIAN, BABYLONIUS, is used in some ancient writers for an astrologer, or any thing relating to astrology. Hence Babylonica cura, the art of casting nativities; and numeri Babylonii, the computation of astrologers.
