BOMBAX

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOMBAX, in Zoology, a synonyme of a species of CONUS. Bombax is also used sometimes for silk or cotton; but the true botanic name of cotton is GOSYRUM. It is likewise applied by LINNÆUS to signify such insects as have incumbent wings, and feelers resembling a comb.
