BOIORUM DESERTA

BOIORUM DESERTA, in Ancient Geography, a district of Pannonia, so called from the excision of the Boii by the Getæ. Now the Weinertwald, of Lower Austria, towards Styria, to the east of Mount Cetiæ, or the Hahenberg, and south of Vindobona or Vienna.
