BETELFAGUI

article · 287 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BETELFAGUI, a town of Asia, in Arabia Felix, famous for the vast quantity of coffee bought and sold there; being the mart where the country people bring their coffee to sell; and where the Europeans come, or send their factors or brokers, to purchase it. E. Long. 57. 20. N. Lat. 15. 40.
