ATABULUS

article · 277 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ATABULUS, in Physiology, a provincial wind in Apulia, of a dry pinching quality, and very noxious in its effects. The ancient naturalists speak of the Atabulus in terms of horror, on account of the ravage it made among the fruits of the earth, which it scorched or withered up.
