BARDT

article · 206 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARDT, a strong and rich town of Germany, in the duchy of Pomerania, with a castle and spacious harbour. It is subject to the Swedes; and is situated near the Baltic sea, in E. Long. 13. 20. N. Lat. 54. 23.
