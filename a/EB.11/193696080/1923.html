BIBRACTE

BIBRACTE, in Ancient Geography, a citadel of the Ædui, according to Strabo; but Cæsar describes it as a town well fortified, very large and populous, and of the greatest authority among that nation: Now Beurect, or Bevray; a desolate place four miles to the north-west of Autun.
