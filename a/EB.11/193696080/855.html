BANGOR, a town of Ireland, in the county of Down and province of Ulster. It is seated on the south shore of the bay of Carrick Fergus, opposite to the town of that name; and has a considerable fishery for soles, turbot, &c. W. Long. 6. N. Lat. 54. 42.
