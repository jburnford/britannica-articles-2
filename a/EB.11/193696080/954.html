BARBEZIEUH

article · 168 chars · page image at NLS ↗

BARBEZIEUH, a town of France, in the department of Charente. It had a manufactory of linen cloth; and contained 2740 inhabitants in 1815. W. Long. 0. 5. N. Lat. 45. 23.
