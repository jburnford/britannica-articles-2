BLOSSOM

article · 288 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BLOSSOM, in a more proper sense, is restrained to the flowers of trees which they put forth in the spring,

Blossom as the forerunners of their fruit, otherwise called their blout. The office of the blossom is partly to protect, and partly to draw nourishment to, the embryo fruit or seed.
