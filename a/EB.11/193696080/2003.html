BIMINI

BIMINI, one of the Bahama islands, near the northwest extremity of the Bahama bank. It is about eight miles in length, and as much in breadth, covered with trees, and inhabited by the native Americans. It is very difficult of access on account of the shoals, but is a very pleasant place. W. Long. 79. 30. N. Lat. 25. 0.
