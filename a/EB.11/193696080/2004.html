BIMLIPATAN

article · 248 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BIMLIPATAN, a village in Hindostan, in the northern circars, seated on the west side of the bay of Bengal. The Dutch had formerly a small factory here, designed for buying up the cloth manufactured by the inhabitants. E. Long. 83. 5. N. Lat. 18. 0.
