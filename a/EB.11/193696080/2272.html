BLATTARIÆ

Cross-references: VERBASCUM

BLATTARIÆ, (from Blatta, a moth or little worm), the title of Scopoli's 12th natural class, in his Flora Carniolica. It is taken from the Blattaria, which was Tournefort's generic name for the verbasum of Linnaeus. See VERBASCUM.
