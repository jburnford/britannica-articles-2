BOLUS

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOLUS, in Pharmacy, an extemporaneous form of a medicine, soft, coherent, a little thicker than honey, and the quantity of which is a little morsel or mouthful; for which reason it is by some called buccella.
