AUGUSTA Rauracorum

sub_entry · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AUGUSTA Rauracorum, a town of Gallia Belgica; now a small village called August, at the bend of the Rhine northward; but from the ruins, which are still to be seen, it appears to have been a considerable

colony, at the distance of six miles from Basil to the east.
