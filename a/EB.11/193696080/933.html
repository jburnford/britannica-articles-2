BARBARUS, FRANCIS

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BARBARUS, FRANCIS, a noble Venetian, was a man of great fame in the 15th century, not only for learning, but likewise for a skilful address in the management of public affairs. He is author of a book De Re Uxoria, and some speeches.
