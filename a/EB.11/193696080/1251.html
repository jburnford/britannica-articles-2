BATISTE, in commerce, a fine white kind of linen cloth, manufactured in Flanders and Picardy.

There are three kinds of batiste; the first very thin; the second less thin; and the third much thicker, called Holland Batiste, as coming very near the goodness of Hollands. They are used for neck-cloths, &c.
