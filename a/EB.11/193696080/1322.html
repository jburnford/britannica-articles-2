BAYON, or Bayona, a town of Galicia, in Spain, seated on a small gulf of the Atlantic ocean, about 12 miles from Tuy. It has a very commodious harbour, and the country about it is fertile. W. Long. 9. 30. N. Lat. 43. 3.
