Beatic BENEDICTION

sub_entry · 165 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Beatic BENEDICTION, (benedictio beatica), is the viaticum given to dying persons. The pope begins all his bulls with this form: Salutem et apostolicam benedictionem.
