CASU PROVIPO

article · 207 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CASU PROVIPO, in Law, a writ of entry founded on the statute of Gloucester, where a tenant in dower aliens the lands she so holds in fee or for life; and lies for the party in reversion against the alliance.
