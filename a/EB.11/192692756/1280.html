CASTILE

CASTILE, Old, a province of Spain, with the title of a kingdom. It is about 192 miles in length, and 115 in breadth; bounded on the south by New Castile, on the east by Arragon and Navarre, on the north by Biscay

Castile and Asturias, and on the west by the kingdom of Leon. Burgos is the capital town.

Castile-del-Oro, a fertile and large country in South America, lying to the west of the Oronoko. It comprehends eight governments; viz. Terra Firma, Proper Carthagea, St Martha, Rio de la Hacha, Venezuela, New Andalusia, Popayan, and the kingdom of New Granada.
