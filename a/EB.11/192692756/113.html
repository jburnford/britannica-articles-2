BUVETTE

article · 375 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUVETTE, or BEUETTE, in the French laws, an established place in every court, where the lawyers and counsellors may retire, warm themselves, and take a glass of wine by way of refreshment, at the king's charge. There is one for each court of parliament, but these are only for persons belonging to that body; there are others in the palais, whither other persons also resort.
