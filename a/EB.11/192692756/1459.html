CAVEZON

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAVEZON, in the manège, a sort of nose band, either of iron, leather, or wood, sometimes flat, and at other times hollow or twisted, clapt upon the nose of a horse to wring it, and so forward the suppling and breaking of the horse.
