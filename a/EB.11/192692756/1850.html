CHARENTE

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHARENTE, a department in the south-west of France. It is about 56 miles in length, and on an average 30 in breadth; and contained 327,000 inhabitants in 1815. It covers an extent of 2240 square miles. Angouleme is the chief town.
