CÆSIA SYLVA

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CÆSIA SYLVA, in Ancient Geography, a wood in Germany, part of the great Sylva Hercynia, situated partly in the duchy of Cleves, and partly in Westphalia, between Wesel and Kessfeld.
