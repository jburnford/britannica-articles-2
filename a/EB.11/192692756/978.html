CAREW, a small village or parish of Wales, in the county of Pembrokeshire, with the remains of a fine castle. Here a tournament was held about the beginning of the sixteenth century, or earlier, by the owner Sir Keil of Thomas, which lasted five days. A cross 14 feet high, formed of a single stone, stands by the side of the road. Population 911. Distant five miles from Pembrokeshire.
