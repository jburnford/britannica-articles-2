CARCHEMISH

article · 554 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARCHEMISH, in Ancient Geography, a town lying upon the Euphrates, and belonging to the Assyrians. Necho king of Egypt took it from the king of Assyria, 2 Chron. xxxv. 20. Necho left a garrison in it, which was taken and cut to pieces, in the fourth year of Jechoiachin king of Judah, by Nebuchadnezzar king of Babylon, 2 Kings xxiii. 29. Isaiah (x. 9.) speaks of Carchemish, and seems to say, that Tiglath-Pileser made a conquest of it, perhaps from the Egyptians. This is thought to be the same city with that called Circesium by the Greeks and Latins.
