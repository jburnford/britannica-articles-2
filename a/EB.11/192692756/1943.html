CHAVEZ

article · 326 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHAVEZ, a strong town of Tra-los-Montes in Portugal, seated at the foot of a mountain on the river Tamega. It has two suburbs, and as many forts; one of which looks like a citadel. Between the town and suburb of Magdalena, is an old Roman stone bridge about 92 geometrical paces long. W. Long. 7. 1. N. Lat. 41. 45.
