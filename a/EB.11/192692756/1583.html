CENSUS

article · 410 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CENSUS was also used for a person worth 100,000 sesterces, or who was entered as such in the censual tables, on his own declaration. In which sense, census amounts to the same with classicus, or a man of the first class; though Gellius limits the estate of those of this class to 125,000 asses. By the Voconian law, no census was allowed to give by his will above a fourth part of what he was worth to a woman.
