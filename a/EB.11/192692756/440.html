CALLISTHENES

CALLISTHENES the philosopher, disciple and relation of Aristotle, by whose desire he accompanied Alexander the Great in his expeditions; but proving too severe a censurer of that hero's conduct, he was by him put to the torture (on a suspicion of a treasonable conspiracy), and died under it, 328 years before Christ.
