BUSBY

BUSBY, DR RICHARD, son of a gentleman in Westminster, was born at Luton in Lincolnshire, in 1606. He passed through the classes in Westminster school, as king's scholar; and completed his studies at Christ-church, Oxford. In 1640 he was appointed master of Westminster school; and by his skill and diligence in the discharge of this important and laborious office, for the space of 55 years, bred up the greatest number of eminent men, in church and state, that ever at one time adorned any age or nation. He was extremely severe in his school; though he applauded wit
