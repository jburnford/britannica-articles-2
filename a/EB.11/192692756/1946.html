CHADLE, a town of England in the county of Stafford, situated on the side of a hill. It is surrounded by coal pits; and in the neighbourhood are carried on extensive manufactures in brass, copper, and tin. A weekly market is held here, and there are four annual fairs. Population 3191 in 1811. Distant 15 miles N. E. of Stafford, and 146 N. N. W. of London. W. Long. 2. N. Lat. 53.
