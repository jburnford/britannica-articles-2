CELL

article · 247 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CELL, in Botany, is applied to the hollow place between the partitions in the pods, husks, and other seed-vessels of plants: according as there is one, two, three, &c. of these cells, the vessel is said to be unilocular, bilocular, trilocular, &c.
