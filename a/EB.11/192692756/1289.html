CASTING Net

sub_entry · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CASTING Net, a sort of fishing net, so called, because it is to be cast or thrown out; which when exactly done, nothing escapes it, but weeds and every thing within its extent are brought away.
