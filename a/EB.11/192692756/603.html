CAMPREDON

article · 176 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CAMPREDON, a town of Catalonia in Spain, seated at the foot of the Pyrenean mountains. The fortifications were demolished by the French in 1691. W. Long. 1. 56. N. Lat. 42. 20.
