CHAMPLAIN, SAMUEL DE

CHAMPLAIN, SAMUEL DE, a celebrated French navigator, the founder of the colony of New France, or Canada. He built Quebec; and was the first governor of the colony in 1603. Died after 1649. See QUEBEC.
