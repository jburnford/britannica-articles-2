CALVITIES

CALVITIES, or CALVITUM, in Medicine, baldness, or a want of hair, particularly on the scinaput, occasioned by the moisture of the head, which should feed it, being dried up, by some disease, old age, or the immoderate use of powder, &c. See ALOPECIA.
