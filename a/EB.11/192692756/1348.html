CATALEPSIS

CATALEPSIS, or CATALEPSY, in Medicine, a kind of apoplexy, or drowsy disease, wherein the patient is taken speechless, senseless, and fixed in the same posture wherein the disease first seized him; his eyes open, without seeing or understanding. See MEDICINE Index.
