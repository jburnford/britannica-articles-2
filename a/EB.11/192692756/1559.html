CEMENT

article · 217 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CEMENT, among engravers, jewellers, &c. is the same with the hot cement used in building; and is used for keeping the metals to be engraven firm to the block, foregoing and also for filling up what is to be chiselled.
