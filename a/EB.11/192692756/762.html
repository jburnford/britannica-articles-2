CANTATA

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CANTATA, in Music, a song or composition, intermixed with recitatives, airs, and different movements, chiefly intended for a single voice, with a thorough bass, though sometimes for other instruments. It was first used in Italy, then in France, whence it passed to us.
