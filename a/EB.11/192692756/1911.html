CHATEAU-DU-LOIRE

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHATEAU-DU-LOIRE, a town of France in the department of Indre and Loire, famous for sustaining a siege of seven years against the count of Mans. It is seated on the river Loire, in E. Long. 25. N. Lat. 47. 40.
