CEODES

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CEODES, in Botany, a genus of the dicæa order, belonging to the polygamia class of plants. There is no calyx; the corolla is monopetalous, with a short turbinated tube; the stamens are ten subulated filaments; the anther roundish.
