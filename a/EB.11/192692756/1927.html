CHATILLON SUR SEINE

CHATILLON SUR SEINE, a town of France, in the department of Cote D'Or, divided into two by the river Seine. This town was the scene of the fruitless negotiations between the allies and Bonaparte in 1814. E. Long. 4. 33. N. Lat. 47. 45.
