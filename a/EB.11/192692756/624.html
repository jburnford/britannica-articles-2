CANANORE, a small district of Hindostan, on the coast of Malabar, now subject to the British. The natives are generally Mahometans; and the country produces pepper, cardamoms, ginger, mirobolans, and tamarinds, in which they drive a considerable trade, their vessels sailing to Arabia and Sumatra.
