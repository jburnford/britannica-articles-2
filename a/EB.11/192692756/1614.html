CENTRONIA

article · 283 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CENTRONIA, in Natural History, a name by which the echini marini have been distinguished. Dr Hill makes them a distinct class of animals, living under the defence of shelly coverings formed of one piece, and furnished with a vast number of spines moveable at the creature's pleasure.
