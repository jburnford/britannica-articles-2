CARRARA MARBLE

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARRARA MARBLE, among our artificers, the name of a species of white marble, which is called mar-mor lunense, and ligustrum by the ancients: it is distinguished from the Parian, now called the statuary marble, by being harder and less bright.
