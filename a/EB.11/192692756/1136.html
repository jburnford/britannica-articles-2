CARTRIDGE-BOX

article · 179 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARTRIDGE-BOX, a case of wood or turned iron, covered with leather, holding a dozen musket cartridges. It is worn upon a belt, and hangs a little lower than the right pocket-hole.
