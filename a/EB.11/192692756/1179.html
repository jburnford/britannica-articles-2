CASE

Cross-references: GRAMMAR

CASE, among grammarians, implies the different inflections or terminations of nouns, serving to express the different relations they bear to each other; and to the things they represent. See GRAMMAR.

CASE also denotes a receptacle for various articles; as a case of knives, of lancets, of pistols, &c.
