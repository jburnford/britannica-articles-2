CHEVISANCE

article · 209 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHEVISANCE, in Law, denotes an agreement or composition, as an end or order set down between a creditor and his debtor, &c. In the statutes, this word is most commonly used for an unlawful bargain or contract.
