CALX Nativa, in Natural History, a kind of marly earth, of a dead whitish colour, which, if thrown into water,

Calc II Camæa. water, makes a considerable bubbling and hissing noise, and has, without previous burning, the quality of making a cement like lime or plaster of Paris.
