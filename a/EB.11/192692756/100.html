BUTTER-MILK

article · 282 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUTTER-MILK, the milk which remains after the butter is produced by churning. Butter-milk is esteemed an excellent food, in the spring especially, and is particularly,

larly recommended in hectic fevers. Some make curds of butter-milk, by pouring into it a quantity of new milk hot.
