CHERSON, a town in Europe, in Russia, situated on the Dnieper, about 60 miles from its mouth. It was founded in 1778, and increased rapidly for some time; but owing to the unhealthiness of the situation, and the difficulty of navigating the river, it has since declined. E. Long. 33. 5. N. Lat. 46. 20.
