CHIEF

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHIEF, in Heraldry, is that which takes up all the upper part of the escutcheon from side to side, and represents a man's head. In chief, imports something borne in the chief part or top of the escutcheon.
