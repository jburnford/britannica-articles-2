BURSA, or PRUSA, in Geography, the capital of Bithynia in Asia Minor, situated in a fine fruitful plain, at the foot of Mount Olympus, about 100 miles south of Constantinople. It is still a large town. E. Long. 29. o. N. Lat. 40. 30.
