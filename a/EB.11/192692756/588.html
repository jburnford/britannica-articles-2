CAMPEN, a strong town in Overijssel in the United Provinces. It hath a citadel and a harbour; but the latter is almost choked up with sand. It was taken by the Dutch in 1578, and by the French in 1672. E. Long. 5. 35. N. Lat. 52. 38.
