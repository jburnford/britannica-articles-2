CARAVEL

article · 295 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CARAVEL; thus they call a small vessel on the coast of France, which goes to fish for herring on the banks. They are commonly from 25 to 30 tons burden. Those which are designed for the same fishery in the British channel are called by the French trinquarts; these are from 12 to 15 tons burden.
