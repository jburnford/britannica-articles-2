CANDELARES

article · 260 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CANDELALES, (from candela, a candle), the name of an order in the former editions of Linnæus's Fragments of a Natural Method, consisting of these three genera, rhizophora, nyssa, and mimusops. They are removed, in the later editions, into the order HOLLORACEÆ.
