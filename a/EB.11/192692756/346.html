CALCULUS

article · 214 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CALCULUS is also used in ancient geometric writers for a kind of weight equal to two grains of cicer. Some make it equivalent to the siliqua, which is equal to three grains of barley. Two calculi made the ceratium.
