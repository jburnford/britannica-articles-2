CENSITUS, a person censed, or entered in the censual tables. See CENSUS.

In an ancient monument found at Ancyra, containing the actions of the emperor Octavius, we read,

Quo lustro civium Romanorum
Censita sunt capita quadragies
Centum millia et sexaginta tria.
