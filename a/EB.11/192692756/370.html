CALEDONIAN CANAL, a canal extending across the Highlands of Scotland, in a north-east and south-west direction, from Fort William to Inverness. It was begun in 1803, and is expected to be completed in 1821. See CALEDONIAN CANAL, SUPPLEMENT.
