CANCELLI

article · 254 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CANCELLI, a term used to denote lattice windows, or those made of cross bars disposed lattice-wise; it is also used for rails or ballusters inclosing the communion-table, a court of justice, or the like, and for the network in the inside of hollow bones.
