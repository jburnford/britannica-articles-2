BREACH

article · 392 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BREACH, in a legal sense, is where a person breaks through the condition of a bond or covenant; on an action upon which, the breach must be assigned: And this assignment must not be general, but particular, as, in an action of covenant for not repairing houses, it ought to be assigned particularly what is the want of repair: and in such certain manner, that the defendant may take an issue.
