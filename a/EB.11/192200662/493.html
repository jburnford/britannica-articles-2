BRIDGEWATER

article · 410 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRIDGEWATER, a town of Somersetshire in England, seated on the river Parret, over which there is a stone-bridge, near which ships of 100 tons burden may ride with ease. It is a large well frequented place, with the title of a duchy, and sends two members to parliament. There are in it several large inns, and the market is well supplied with provisions. Population 4911 in 1811. W. Long. 3. 0. N. Lat. 51. 15.
