BULLIALDUS

article · 680 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BULLIALDUS, ISMAEL, an eminent astronomer, was born at Laon in the Isle of France in 1605. He travelled in his youth for the sake of improvement; and afterwards published several works, among which are, 1. De natura lucis. 2. Philolaus. 3. Astronomia philolaica, opus novum, in quo motus planetarum per novam et veram hypothesis demonstrantur. 4. Astronomice philolaicæ fundamenta clarius explicata et asserta adversus Zethi Wardi impugnationem. He also wrote a piece or two upon geometry and arithmetic. In 1661, he paid Hevelius a visit at Dantzic, for the sake of seeing his optical and astronomical apparatus. Afterwards he became a presbyter at Paris, and died there in 1694.
