BREMEGARTON

article · 361 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BREMEGARTON, a handsome and pretty considerable town of Switzerland, which, since 1798, forms the head town of a district in the new canton of Argau. The inhabitants deal chiefly in paper; and their religion is the Roman-catholic. It is divided into the upper and lower towns, and is very advantageously seated on the river Russ. E. Long. 8. 25. N. Lat. 47. 20.
