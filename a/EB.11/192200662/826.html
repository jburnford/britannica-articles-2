BULL. See Bos, MAMMALIA Index.

Wild Bulls. The wild bulls, now so numerous on the continent of America, are said to have sprung from one bull and seven cows, which were carried thither by some of the first conquerors. For the manner of hunting these, see BUCANEERS.
