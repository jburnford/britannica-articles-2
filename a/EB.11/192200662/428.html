BRESCIA

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRESCIA, a strong and handsome town of Italy, with a bishop's see and good citadel. It is the capital of Bresciano, contains 48,000 inhabitants, and is seated in an agreeable plain on the river Garza, in E. Long. 10. 5. N. Lat. 45. 31.
