BRISACH, a town of Germany, and capital of Brisgau, now in the territories of Baden. It was twice in the possession of the French; but restored to the house of Austria, by treaties of peace. It was a very strong place, but the fortifications have been demolished. It is seated on the Rhine, where there is a bridge of boats. E. Long. 7. 49. N. Lat. 48. 5.
