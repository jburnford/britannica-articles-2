BUBONOCLE

BUBONOCLE, or HERNIA INGUINALIS, in Surgery, a tumour in the inguen, formed by a prolapsus of the intestines, omentum, or both, through the processus of the peritoneum and rings of the abdominal muscles. See SURGERY Index.
