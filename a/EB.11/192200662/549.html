BRISGAW

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRISGAW, a territory of Germany, in the circle of Suabia, on the eastern bank of the Rhine, about 50 miles in length, and 30 in breadth, now subject to the grand duke of Baden. The principal places are Old Brisach, New Brisach, Freyburgh, and Rhinmarck.
