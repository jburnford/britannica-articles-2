BRAIN, in Anatomy, is that large, soft, whitish mass, enclosed in the cranium or skull; wherein all the organs of sense terminate, and the soul is supposed principally to reside. See ANATOMY INDEX.

BRAIN le Comte, a town of the Austrian Netherlands, in the province of Hainault. E. Long. 4. 11. N. Lat. 50. 35.
