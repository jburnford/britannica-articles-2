BRISÉIS

article · 299 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRISÉIS, or HIPPODAMIA, in fabulous history, the wife of Mynes king of Lyrnessa. After Achilles had taken that city, and killed her husband, she became his captive. That hero loved her tenderly; but Agamemnon taking her from him, she became the accidental cause of sad disorders in the Grecian army.
