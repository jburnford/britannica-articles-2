BRETTIGAW

article · 173 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRETTIGAW, a territory or valley of the Grisons, lying between the Rhine and the county of Tyrol, and along the river Lanquet. The fortress of Castels is the principal town.
