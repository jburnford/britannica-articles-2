BREAKERS

article · 428 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BREAKERS, a name given by sailors to those bilows that break violently over rocks lying under the surface of the sea. They are distinguished both by their appearance and sound, as they cover that part of the sea with a perpetual foam, and produce a hoarse and terrible roaring, very different from what the waves usually have in a deeper bottom. When a ship is unhappily driven among breakers, it is hardly possible to save her.
