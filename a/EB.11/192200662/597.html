BRONTIUM

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRONTIUM, in Grecian antiquity, a place underneath the floor of the theatres, in which were kept brazen vessels full of stones and other materials, with which they imitated the noise of thunder.
