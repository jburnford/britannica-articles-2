BRASIL-WOOD

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BRASIL-WOOD, or Brasil-wood, an American wood of a red colour, and very heavy. It is denominated variously, according to the places from whence it is brought: Thus we have brasil of Fernambucca, Sappan, Lamon, &c. For its description, &c. see CÆSALPINIA, BOTANY INDEX.
