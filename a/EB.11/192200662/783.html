BUFALMACO

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUFALMACO, BORAMICO, an Italian painter; the first who put labels to the mouths of his figures, with sentences; since followed by bad masters, but more frequently in caricature engravings. He died in 1340.
