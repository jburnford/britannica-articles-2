BUCENTAUR

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUCENTAUR is also the name of a ship, as great and as magnificent as that of the Venetians, built by order of the elector of Bavaria, and launched on a lake which is six leagues in length.
