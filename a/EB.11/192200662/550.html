BRISACUS MONS

BRISACUS MONS, in Ancient Geography, a town on the right or east side of the Rhine. Now Brisach, situated on a round hill; a fortified town of Suabia, and distinguished by the name of Old Brisach. E. Long. 7. 15. N. Lat. 48. 10.
