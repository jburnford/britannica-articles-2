BUCKENHAM

article · 184 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUCKENHAM, NEW, a town of Norfolk in England, which formerly had a strong castle, but now demolished. It is seated in a flat, in E. Long. 1. 10. N. Lat. 52. 30. Population 656 in 1811.
