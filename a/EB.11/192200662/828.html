BULLS Eye

sub_entry · 171 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BULLS Eye, among seamen, a small, obscure, sublime cloud, ruddy in the middle, that sometimes appears to mariners, and is the immediate forerunner of a great storm at sea.
