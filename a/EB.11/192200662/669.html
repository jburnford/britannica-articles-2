BRUNDISIUM, or BRUNDUSIUM

BRUNDISIUM, or BRUNDUSIUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of Calabria, with the best harbour in Italy. It was a very ancient town, and belonged originally to the Salentines; but was taken by the Romans about 256 years before Christ. Now Brindisi; which see.
