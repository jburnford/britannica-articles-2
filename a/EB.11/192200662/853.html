BUNT-Lines

sub_entry · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BUNT-Lines, are small lines made fast to the bottom of the sails, in the middle part of the bolt-ropes, to a cringle, and are so reeved through a small block, seized to the yard. Their use is to trice up the bunt of the sail, for the better furling it up.
