BOSA

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

BOSA, a maritime town in the western part of the island of Sardinia, with a castle, a good port, and a bishop's see. It is seated on the river Bosa, to the north-east of an island of the same name; and has good salt pits. E. Long. 8. 30. N. Lat. 40. 19.
