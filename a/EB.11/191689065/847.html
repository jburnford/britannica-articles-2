NAUCRARI

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NAUCRARI, among the Athenians, was the name given to the chief magistrates of the Δμοι, "boroughs or townships," called Ναυρχηται; because each was obliged, besides two horsemen, to furnish out one ship for the public service.
