MODERATION

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MODERATION, in Ethics, is a virtue consisting in the proper government of our appetites, passions, and pursuits, with respect to honours, riches, and pleasures; and in this sense it is synonymous with temperance: it is also often used to denote candour.
