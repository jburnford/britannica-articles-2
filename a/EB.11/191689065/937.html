NEHOW

NEHOW, one of the Sandwich islands, discovered by Captain Cook in his last voyage to the Pacific ocean: these islands are eleven in number, and are situated from 18° 44' to 22° 15' N. Lat. and from 154° 56' to 160° 24' W. Long.
