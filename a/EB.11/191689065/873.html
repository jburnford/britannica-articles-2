NAXOS

NAXOS, or NAXIA, a considerable town, and capital of the island of Naxos, over against the isle of Paros, with a castle and two archbishops sees, the one Greek and the other Latin. The greatest part of the inhabitants are Greeks. E. Long. 25. 51. N. Lat. 37. 8.
