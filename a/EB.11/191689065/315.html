MONONGAHELA

article · 258 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONONGAHELA, a river of North America, which rises in the Laurel mountains in Virginia, and after a course of about 200 miles, joins the Alleghany at Pittsburg. The two rivers united then form the Ohio. The Monongahela is navigable for boats about 100 miles.
