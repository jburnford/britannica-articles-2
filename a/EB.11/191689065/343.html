MONTARGIS

MONTARGIS, a considerable town of France, in the department of Loir-et-Cher, situated on the river Long, at the junction of the two canals which connect the Seine and the Loire. It is 62 miles south of Paris, has some manufactories of paper and woollen, and is a place of considerable trade. E. Long. 2. 36. N. Lat. 48. 1.
