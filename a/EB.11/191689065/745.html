NABLOUS

NABLOUS, a province of Syria, anciently celebrated under the name of the kingdom of Samaria. Its capital, likewise called Nablos, is situated near to Sichem on the ruins of the Nicopolis of the Greeks, and is the residence of a sheik, who is subordinate to the pacha of Damascus, from whom he farms the tribute of the province.
