MOULDS

article · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MOULDS for leaden bullets are little iron pincers, each of whose branches terminates in a hemispherical concave, which when shut from an entire sphere. In the lips or sides where the branches meet is a little jet or hole, through which the melted lead is conveyed.
