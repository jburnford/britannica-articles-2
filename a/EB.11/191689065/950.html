NEMOURS

article · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NEMOURS, a town in France, in the department of Seine and Marne, containing 3469 inhabitants in 1800. It is seated on the river Loing, in E. Long 2. 45. N. Lat. 48. 15.
