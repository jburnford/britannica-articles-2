MONTMEDI

article · 277 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONTMEDI, a small but strong town of France, in the department of the Meuse, seated on the river Chire, which divides it into the upper and lower towns. It is 22 miles south-east of Sedan, 27 south-west of Luxemburg, and 135 north-east of Paris. E. Long. 5. 23. N. Lat. 49. 32.
