MONETARIUS

MONETARIUS, or MONEYER, a name which antiquaries and medallists give to those who struck the ancient coins or moneys.

Many of the old Roman, &c. coins have the name of the monetarius, either written at length, or at least the initial letters of it. See MEDAL.
