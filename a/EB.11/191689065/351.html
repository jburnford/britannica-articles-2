MONTÉ-FALCONE

MONTÉ-FALCONE, a town of Italy, in Friuli, with a castle, near the river Ponzano, 12 miles north-west of Trieste. It is in the Lombardo-Venetian territories of Austria. E. Long. 13. 20. N. Lat. 45. 50.
