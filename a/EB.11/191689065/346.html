MONTBELLIARD

article · 198 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONTBELLIARD, a strong town of France, in the department of Haut Rhine, seated near the river Doubs, 45 miles north-east of Besançon. Its population in 1800 was 4000. E. Long. 6. 50. N. Lat. 47. 31.
