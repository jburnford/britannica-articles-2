MYSON

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MYSON, a native of Sparta, one of the seven wise men of Greece. When Anacharsis consulted the oracle of Apollo, to know which was the wisest man in

Greece, he received for answer, he who is now ploughing his fields. This was Myson.
