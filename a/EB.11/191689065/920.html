NEEDWOOD FOREST

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NEEDWOOD FOREST, in Staffordshire, between the Trent, Dove, and Blythe, and near Uxeter, is said to exceed all the forests in England in the excellence of its soil and the fineness of its turf.
