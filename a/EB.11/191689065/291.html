MONDA

article · 275 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONDA, or MUNDA, in Ancient Geography, a river of Lusitania, running mid-way from east to west into the Atlantic, between the Durius and Tagus, and washing Conimbrica. Now the Mondego, a river of Portugal, which running by Coimbra, falls into the Atlantic, 30 miles below it.
