MINORS, or FRIARS MINOR

sub_entry · 297 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MINORS, or FRIARS MINOR, an appellation which the Franciscans assume, out of show of humility; calling themselves fratres minores, i. e. lesser brothers, and sometimes minorites. There is also an order of regular minors at Naples, which was established in the year 1388, and confirmed by Sixtus V.
