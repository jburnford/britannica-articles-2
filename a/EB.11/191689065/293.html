MONDOVI

article · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MONDOVI, a considerable town of Italy, in Piedmont; with a citadel, university, and bishop's see. It belongs to the king of Sardinia, is large and populous, and is seated in E. Long. 7. 40. N. Lat. 44. 33.
