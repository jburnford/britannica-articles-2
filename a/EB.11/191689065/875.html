NAXUS, in Ancient Geography, a town of Crete, famous for its bones, called lapis Naxius. Another of Sicily, built by the Chalcidians; situated on the south side of Mount Taurus, destroyed by Dionysius the

tyrant: from its ruins Tauromenium, built by Timoleon, either arose or was increased, (Plutarch).
