ALLENDORF

article · 244 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALLENDOFF, a small town in the circle of the Upper Rhine, and in the landgraviate of Hesse Cassel, remarkable for its salt works, and three stone bridges. It is seated on the river Weser, 15 miles east of Cassel. E. Long. 10. 5. N. Lat. 51. 26.
