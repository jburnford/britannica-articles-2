ACTUARIUS, a celebrated Greek physician of the 13th century, and the first Greek author who has treated of mild purgatives, such as cassia, manna, sena, &c. He is the first also who mentions distilled waters.

His works were printed in one volume folio, by Henry Stephens in 1567. Actuarius || Acuna.
