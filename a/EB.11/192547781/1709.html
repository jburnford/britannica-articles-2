ALKADARIJ

article · 279 chars · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: ALGIABARIJ

ALKADARIJ, a sect among the Mahometans who deny any eternal, fixed, divine decrees, and are assertors of free-will. The word is formed from the Arabic alkadar, which signifies "decree." The Alkadari are a branch of Motazalites, and stand opposed to the Algiabari. See ALGIABARIJ.
