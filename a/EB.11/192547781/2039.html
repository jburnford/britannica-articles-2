AMBERG

AMBERG, a city of Germany, the capital of the upper palatinate of Bavaria, with a good castle, ramparts, and deep ditches. It is seated near the confines of Franconia, on the river Wils. It has a great trade in iron and other metals, which are found in the neighbouring mountains. E. Long. 12. o. N. Lat. 49. 25.
