ABADIR

article · 275 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABADIR, a title which the Carthaginians gave to gods of the first order. In the Roman mythology, it is the name of a stone which Saturn swallowed, by the contrivance of his wife Ops, believing it to be his newborn son Jupiter: hence it became the object of religious worship.
