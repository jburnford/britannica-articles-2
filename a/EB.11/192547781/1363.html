AIGUISCE

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AIGUISCE, in Heraldry, denotes a cross with its four ends sharpened, but so as to terminate in obtuse angles. It differs from the cross fitchee, in as much as the latter tapers by degrees to a point, and the former only at the ends.
