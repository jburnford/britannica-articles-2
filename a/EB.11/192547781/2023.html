AMATTA FOA

article · 286 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMATTA FOA, an island in the Southern Pacific ocean, which was discovered by Captain Cook in 1774. It is about five leagues in circumference, and considerably elevated; it is inhabited, but not very fertile; and it lies about twelve leagues distant, and north north-west, from Anamooka.
