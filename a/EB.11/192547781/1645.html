ALEXANDRIAN

article · 416 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALEXANDRIAN, in a particular sense, is applied to all those who professed or taught the sciences in the school of Alexandria. In this sense, Clemens is denominated Alexandrinus, though born at Athens. The same may be said of Apion, who was born at Oasis; and Arostarchus, by birth a Samothracian. The chief Alexandrian philosophers were, Amomus, Plotinus, Origen, Porphyry, Iamblicus, Sopater, Maximus, and Dexippus.
