AMARYNTHUS

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMARYNTHUS, in Ancient Geography, a hamlet of Eretria, in the island of Eubœa, about seven stadia distant from its walls. Here Diana was worshipped in an annual solemnity, at which those of Carystus assisted; hence the title of the goddess was Amarynthis and Amarysia.
