ALAUTA

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALAUTA, a considerable river of Turkey in Europe, which, after watering the north-east part of Transylvania and part of Walachia, falls into the Danube almost opposite to Nicopolis.
