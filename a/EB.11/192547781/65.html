ABASSI

article · 202 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABASSI, or ABASSIS, a silver coin current in Persia, equivalent in value to a French livre, or tenpence halfpenny sterling. It took its name from Shah Abbas II. king of Persia, under whom it was struck.
