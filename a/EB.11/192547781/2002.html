AMAND, Saint, a city of France, in the department of the North, seated on the river Scarpe. It contained by the last enumeration 8039 inhabitants. The abbot of the place is the temporal lord, and disposes of the magistracy. It was given to France by the treaty of Utrecht. E. Long. 2. 25. N. Lat. 50. 27.
