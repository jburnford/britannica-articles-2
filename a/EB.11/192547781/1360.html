AIGLE, a small town of France, in Upper Normandy, 23 miles from D'Evereux, and 38 from Rouen, in the department of Orne. It is surrounded with walls and ditches, and has six gates, three suburbs and three parishes. It trades in corn, toys, and more particularly in needles and pins. E. Long. 0. 46. N. Lat. 48. 46.
