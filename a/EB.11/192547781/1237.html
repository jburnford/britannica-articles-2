AGGLUTINATION

article · 227 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AGGLUTINATION, among physicians, implies the action of reuniting the parts of a body, separated by a wound, cut, &c. It is also applied to the action of such internal medicines as are supposed to be of an agglutinating quality.
