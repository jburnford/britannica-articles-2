ACQUARIA

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACQUARIA, a small town of Italy, in Frigana, a district of Modena, which is remarkable for its medicinal waters. It is 18 miles south-west of the city of Modena. E. Long. 10. 45. N. Lat. 44. 24.
