ADOWA

article · 518 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADOWA, the capital of Tigre in Abyssinia, is situated on the declivity of a hill on the west side of a small plain, which is surrounded on every side by mountains. The name, signifying pass or passage, is characteristic of its situation; for the only road from the Red sea to Gondar passes by Adowa. The town consists of 300 houses, is the residence of the governor, and has a manufactory of coarse cotton cloth which circulates in Abyssinia as the medium of exchange in place of money. N. Lat. 14. 7. E. Long. 38. 50.
