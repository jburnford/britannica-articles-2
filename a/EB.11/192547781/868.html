ADERNO, a small place in the Val di Demona in the

the kingdom of Sicily. E. Long. 15. 25. N. Lat. 38. 5. Anciently ADRANUM, at the foot of Mount Gibel. The ruined walls of this ancient city still exhibit an air of its former grandeur.
