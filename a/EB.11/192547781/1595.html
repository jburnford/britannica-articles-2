Gill ALE

sub_entry · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Gill ALE, is that in which the dried leaves of gill or ground-ivy have been infused. It is esteemed absterse and vulnerary, and consequently good in disorders of the breast and obstructions of the viscera.
