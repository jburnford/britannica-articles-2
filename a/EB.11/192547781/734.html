ACTANIA

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACTANIA, an island, according to Pliny, in the North sea. It lies to the west of Holstein and Dithmarsch, not far from the mouth of the Eyder and Elbe, and is now called Heyligland.
