ÆLII PONS

article · 263 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ÆLII PONS, in Ancient Geography, one of the fortresses near the wall or rampart, or, in the words of the Notitia, through the line of the hither wall; built, as is thought, by Adrian: now named Portland, in Northumberland, between Newcastle and Morpeth, (Camden.)
