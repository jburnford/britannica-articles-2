ALMEDIA

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALMEDIA, a frontier town of Portugal, in the province of Tralos Montes, on the confines of Leon, where there was a very brisk action between the French and Portuguese in 1663; 17 miles north-west of Ciudad Rodrigo. W. Long. 7. 20. N. Lat. 40. 31.
