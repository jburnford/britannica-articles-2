ALIENS-Duty

sub_entry · 298 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALIENS-Duty is otherwise called petty customs, and
navigation-duty.—Fish dried or salted, and cod-fish or
herrings not caught in British vessels and cured by Bri-
tish subjects, pay a double aliens-duty.—On what foot-
ing aliens are permitted to import foreign commodities
into Great Britain, see DUTY.
