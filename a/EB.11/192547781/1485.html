ALBEMARLE, or AUMALE, a town of France, in Upper Normandy, now in the department of Seine Inferior, from whence the noble family of Keppel takes the title of earl. It is seated on the declivity of a hill, on the confines of Picardy, 35 miles north-east of Rouen, and 70 north-west of Paris. E. Long. 1. 42. N. Lat. 49. 50.
