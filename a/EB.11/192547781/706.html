ACROBATES

article · 271 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACROBATES, in antiquity, were rope-dancers who performed various feats by vaulting or tumbling on a rope; sliding down on a rope from a lofty station

with arms and legs extended, in imitation of flying; and running, dancing, and leaping, on a rope stretched horizontally.
