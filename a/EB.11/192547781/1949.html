ALTORF, a town of the circle of Franconia, in Germany.

Germany. It has a botanical garden, with a great variety of plants, an anatomical theatre, a handsome library, and contained 2070 inhabitants in 1803. It is subject to the house of Bavaria, and is 15 miles from Nuremberg. E. Long. 11. 7. N. Lat. 49. 25.
