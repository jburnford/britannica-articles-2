ALIENATION-OFFICE

article · 192 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALIENATION-OFFICE, is an office to which all writs of
covenants and entry, upon which fines are levied, and
recoveries suffered, are carried, to have fines for aliena-
tion set and paid thereon.
