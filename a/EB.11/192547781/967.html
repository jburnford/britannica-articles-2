ADRASTEIA

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADRASTEIA, in mythology, was the daughter of Jupiter and Necessity, and, according to Plutarch, the only fury who executed the vengeance of the gods. The name is derived from King Adrastus, who first erected a temple to that deity.
