ÆRARIUS

article · 247 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ÆRARIUS was also used for a person employed in coining or working brass.

These are sometimes called ærorii fusores; at other times, ærarius is distinguished from fusor; the former answering to what we now call coppersmiths, the latter to founders.
