ALBUM

article · 249 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALBUM, in Antiquity, a kind of white table or register, wherein the names of certain magistrates, public transactions, &c. were entered. Of these there were various sorts; as the album decurionum, album senatorum, album judicium, album pratoris, &c.
