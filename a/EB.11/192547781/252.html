ABLACQUEATION

article · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABLACQUEATION, an old term in Gardening, signifies the operation of removing the earth, and barring the roots of trees in winter, to expose them more freely to the air, rain, snows, &c.
