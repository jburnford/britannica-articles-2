ABACUS Logisticus

sub_entry · 412 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABACUS Logisticus, is a rectangled triangle, whose sides, forming the right angle, contain the numbers from 1 to 60; and its area, the facta of each two of the numbers perpendicularly opposite. This is also called a canon of sexagintals.

ABACUS et Palmula, in the Ancient Music, denote the machinery, whereby the strings of polyplectra, or instruments of many strings, were struck with a plectrum made of quills.
