AGITATORS

article · 251 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AGITATORS, in the English history, certain officers set up in the army in 1647, to take care of its interests. —Cromwell joined the agitators, only with a view to serve his own ends; which being once accomplished, he found means to get them abolished.
