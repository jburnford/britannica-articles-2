ADENBURG

ADENBURG, or ALDENBURG, a town of Westphalia, and in the duchy of Berg, subject to the Elector Palatine. It is 12 miles north-east of Cologne, and 17 west of Bonn. E. Long. 7. 25. N. Lat. 51. 2.
