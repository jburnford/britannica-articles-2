AHEAD

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AHEAD, a sea term, signifying further onward than the ship, or at any distance before her, lying immediately on that point of the compass to which her stem is directed. It is used in opposition to astern, which expresses the situation of any object behind the ship.
