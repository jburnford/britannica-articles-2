AMAIN

article · 204 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMAIN, in the sea language, a term importing to lower something at once. Thus, to strike amain, is to make a signal, by waving a drawn sword, or the like, as a demand that the enemy strike their topsails.
