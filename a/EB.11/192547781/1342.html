AHUN, a town of France, in the Upper Marche and generality of Moulins, in the department of Creuse. It is seated on the river Creuse, eight miles south-east of Gueret, 30 north-east of Lomages, and 55 south-east of Moulins. E. Long. 1. 52. N. Lat. 49. 5.
