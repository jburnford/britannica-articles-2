AIRE, in Geography, an ancient town of France, in the department of Landes, formerly Gascony, containing 3000 inhabitants. It is seated on the river Adour, on the declivity of a mountain, 155 leagues from Paris. W. Long. 0. 12. N. Lat. 43. 42.
