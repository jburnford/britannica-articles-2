ADDEXTRATOR

article · 172 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADDEXTRATOR, in the court of Rome, the pope's mitre-bearers, so called according to Ducange, because they walk at the pope's right hand when he rides to visit the churches.
