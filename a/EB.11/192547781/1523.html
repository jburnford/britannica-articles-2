ALBUM Græcum

sub_entry · 201 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALBUM Græcum, the white dung of dogs, formerly prescribed for inflammations of the throat, &c. but now disused, and chiefly employed by leather-dressers to soften leather after the application of lime.
