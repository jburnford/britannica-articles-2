AETIUS

AETIUS, a famous physician, born at Amida in Mesopotamia, and the author of a work entitled Tetrabiblos, which is a collection from the writings of those physicians who went before him. He lived, according to Dr Freind, at the end of the fifth or the beginning of the sixth century.
