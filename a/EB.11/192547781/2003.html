AMANICÆ PYLE

article · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMANICÆ PYLE (Ptolemy); AMANIDES PYLE (Strabo), AMANI PORTÆ (Pliny); straits or defiles in Mount Amanus, through which Darius entered Cilicia; at a greater distance from the sea than the Pyle Cilicia or Syriæ, through which Alexander passed.
