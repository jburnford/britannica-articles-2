ADRASTEIA Certamina

sub_entry · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ADRASTEIA Certamina, in antiquity, a kind of Pythian games, instituted by Adrastus king of Argos, in the year of the world 2700, in honour of Apollo, at Sicyon. These are to be distinguished from the Pythian games celebrated at Delphi.
