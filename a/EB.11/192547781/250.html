ABLACTATION

article · 275 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ABLACTATION, among the ancient gardeners, the same with what is now called GRAFTING by approach, is a method of engrafting, by which the scion of one tree being for some time united to the stock of another, is afterwards cut off, and, as it were, weaned from the parent tree.
