AMATORII MUSCULI

article · 183 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AMATORII MUSCULI, in Anatomy, a term sometimes used for the obliquus superior and obliquus inferior muscles of the eye, as these muscles assist in oggling or drawing the eye sideways.
