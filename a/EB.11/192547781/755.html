ACTIVE

article · 239 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ACTIVE, in Grammar, is applied to such words as express action; and is therefore opposed to passive. The active performs the action, as the passive receives it.

Thus we say, a verb active, a conjugation active, &c. or an active participle.
