ALOPECIA, ALOPECIA, in Ancient Geography, an island placed by Ptolemy at the mouth of the Tanais, and called the island Tanais, now l'Isle des Renards. (Baudrand). Also an island of the Bosphorus Cimmerius (Pliny); and another in the Ægean sea, over against Smyrna.
