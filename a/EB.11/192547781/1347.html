AJAX

article · 527 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AJAX, the son of Telamon, was, next to Achilles, the most valiant general among the Greeks at the siege of Troy. He commanded the troops of Salamis, and performed many great actions, of which we have an account in the Iliad, in Dictys Cretensis, and in the 23d book of Ovid's Metamorphoses. He was so enraged, that the arms of Achilles were adjudged to Ulysses, that he immediately became mad. The Greeks paid great honour to him after his death, and erected a magnificent monument to his memory upon the promontory of Rhetium.
