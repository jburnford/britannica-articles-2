ALLOTTING

article · 391 chars · page image at NLS ↗

ALLOTTING, or ALLOTMENT of Goods, in matters of commerce, is when a ship's cargo is divided into several parts, bought by divers persons, whose names are written on as many pieces of paper, which are applied by an indifferent person to the several lots or parcels; by which means the goods are divided without partiality, every man having the parcel which the lot with his name appropriates.
