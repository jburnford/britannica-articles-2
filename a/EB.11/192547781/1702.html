ALIPILARIUS

article · 272 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ALIPILARIUS, or ALIPILUS, in Roman antiquity, a servant belonging to the baths, whose business it was, by means of waxen plasters, and an instrument called volcella, to take off the hair from the arm-pits, and even arms, legs, &c. this being deemed a point of cleanliness.
