AFFECTION, among physicians, signifies the same as disease. Thus the hysteric affection is the same with the hysteric disease.

AFFERERS or AFFERORS, in Law, persons appointed in courts-leet, courts-baron, &c. to settle, upon oath, the fines to be imposed upon those who have been guilty of faults arbitrarily punishable.
