AD PONDUS OMNIUM

article · 372 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

AD PONDUS OMNIUM, among physicians, an abbreviation in their prescriptions, signifying that the last-mentioned ingredient is to weigh as much as all the rest together.

An Quod Damnum, in the English Law, a writ directed to the sheriff, commanding him to inquire into the damage which may arise from granting certain privileges to a place, as a fair, a market, or the like.
