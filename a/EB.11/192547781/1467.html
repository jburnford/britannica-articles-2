ALBANA

ALBANA, in Ancient Geography, a sea-port town of Albania, on the Caspian sea, between the rivers Casius and Albanus; now called Bachu, or Bachy, giving name to the Caspian sea, viz. Mer de Bachu. E. Long. 49. 0. N. Lat. 40. 0.
