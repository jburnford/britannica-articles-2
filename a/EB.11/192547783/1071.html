PLOCKSKO

article · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PLOCKSKO, formerly a palatinate of Poland, bounded on the north by Regal Prussia, on the east by the palatinate of Mazovia, on the south by the Vistula, and on the west by the palatinate of Inovladislav. The capital town is of the same name.
