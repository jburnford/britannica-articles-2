PERIANTHIUM

Cross-references: BOTANY

PERIANTHIUM, (from περι, "round," and ανθος, "the flower,") the flower cup properly so called, the most common species of calyx, placed immediately under the flower, which is contained in it as in a cup. See BOTANY Index.
