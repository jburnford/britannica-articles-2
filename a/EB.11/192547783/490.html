PESCHIERA

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PESCHIERA, a small but strong town of Italy, in the Veronese, with a castle and a strong fort; seated on the river Mincio or Menzo, which proceeds from the lake Garda. E. Long. 11. 4. N. Lat. 42. 27.
