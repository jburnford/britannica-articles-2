PATOMACK

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PATOMACK, a large river of Virginia, in North America, which rises in the Alleghany mountains, separates Virginia from Maryland, and falls into Chesapeake bay. It is navigable for near 300 miles. Washington is situated on its north bank.
