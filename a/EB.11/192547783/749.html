PHYLACTERY

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PHYLACTERY, in the general, was a name given by the ancients to all kinds of charms, spells, or cha-

acters, which they wore about them, as amulets, to preserve them from danger or diseases.
