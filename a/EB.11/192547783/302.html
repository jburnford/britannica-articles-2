PENMAN-MAWR

article · 326 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PENMAN-MAWR, a mountain in Caernarvonshire, 1400 feet high. It hangs perpendicularly over the sea, at so vast a height, that few spectators are able to look down the dreadful steep. On the side which is next the sea, there is a road cut out of the side of the rock, about six or seven feet wide, which winds up a steep ascent.
