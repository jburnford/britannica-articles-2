PETRA Jeckael

PETRA Jeckael (2 Kings xiv.), a town of the Amalekites; near the Adscensus Scorpionis (Judges i.) and the valley of Salt in the south of Judea; afterwards in the possession of the Edomites, after destroying the Amalekites.
