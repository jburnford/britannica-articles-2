PHARMACOPOLA, or PHARMACOPEIUS, an apothecary, or a person who prepares and sells medicines; but this word is rarely used but in the way of ridicule. It is composed of φάρμακον, medicine, and πώλιν, to sell. Hor. Sat. ii. lib. i. ver. 1.
