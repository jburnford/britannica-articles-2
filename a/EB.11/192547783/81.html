PATIENCE

article · 184 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PATIENCE. PATHOGNOMONIC, among Physicians, an appellation for a symptom, or concurrence of symptoms, that are inseparable from a distemper, and are found in that only, and in no other.
