PENTACHORD

article · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PENTACHORD (compounded of πέντε, five, and χορδα, string), an ancient musical instrument with five strings. The invention of the pentachord is referred to the Scythians; the strings were of bullocks leather, and they were struck with a plectrum made of goats horn.
