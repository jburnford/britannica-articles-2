PERICARDIUM

PERICARDIUM, in Anatomy, a membranous bag filled with water, which contains the heart in man and many other animals. It is formed by a duplicature of the mediastinum, or membrane which divides the thorax into two unequal parts. See ANATOMY, No 121.
