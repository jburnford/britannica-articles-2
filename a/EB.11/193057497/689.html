SLOOP

article · 342 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: SHIP

SLOOP, a small vessel furnished with one mast, the mainsail of which is attached to a gaff above, or to the mast on its foremost edge, and to a long boom below, by which it is occasionally shifted to either quarter. See SHIP.

Sloop of War, a name given to the smallest vessels of war except cutters. They are either rigged as ships or sloops.
