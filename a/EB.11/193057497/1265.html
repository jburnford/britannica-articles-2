STOCKS

article · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STOCKS, a frame erected on the shore of a river or harbour, whereon to build shipping. It generally consists of a number of wooden blocks, ranged parallel to each other at convenient distances, and with a gradual declivity towards the water.
