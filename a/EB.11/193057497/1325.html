STRAPADO

article · 339 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

STRAPADO, or STRAPPADO, a kind of military punishment, wherein the criminal's hands being tied behind him, he is hoisted up with a rope to the top of a long piece of wood, and let fall again almost to the ground; so that, by the weight of his body in the shock, his arms are dislocated. Sometimes he is to undergo three strapadoes or more.
