SEVERNDROOG

article · 169 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEVERNDRÖOG, a sea-port town and fortress of Hindostan, which was taken by the English in 1756. It is 68 miles south from Bombay, and in N. Lat. 17. 55. E. Long. 72. 50.
