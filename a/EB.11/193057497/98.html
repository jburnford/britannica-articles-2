SEDAN-CHAIR

article · 313 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SEDAN-CHAIR is a covered vehicle for carrying a single person, suspended by two poles, and borne by two men, hence denominated chairmen. They were first introduced in London in 1634, when Sir Sanders Duncomb obtained the sole privilege to use, let, and hire a number of the said covered chairs for fourteen years.
