SUGILLATION

article · 421 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SUGILLATION, in Medicine, an extravasation of blood in the coats of the eye, which at first appears of a reddish colour, and afterwards livid or black. If the disorder is great, bleeding and purging are proper, as are also discutients.
