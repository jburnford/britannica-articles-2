STIMULI

STIMULI, in Botany, a species of armature or offensive weapon, with which some plants, as nettle, cassava,

da, acalypha, and tragis, are furnished. Their use, says Linnaeus, is by their venomous punctures to keep off naked animals that would approach to hurt them.
