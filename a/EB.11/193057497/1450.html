SUET, SEVUM, or SEBUM

sub_entry · 185 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SUET, SEVUM, or SEBUM, in Anatomy, the solid fat found in several animals, as sheep, oxen, &c. but not in the human species. See the article FAT.—It is of the sevum that tallow is made.
