TO SHOE an Anchor

TO SHOE an Anchor, is to cover the flukes with a broad

broad triangular piece of plank, whose area or superficies is much larger than that of the flukes. It is intended to give the anchor a stronger and surer hold of the bottom in very soft and oozy ground.
