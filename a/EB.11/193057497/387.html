SHAMBLES

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SHAMBLES, among miners, a sort of niches or landing places, left at such distances in the adits of the mines, that the shovel-men may conveniently throw up the ore from shamble to shamble, till it comes to the top of the mine.
