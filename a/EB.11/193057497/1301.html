STOWMARKET, a town of Suffolk, in England,

situated in E. Long. 1. 6. N. Lat. 52. 16. It is a large handsome place, situated between the rivers Gypping and Orwell, and has the best cherries in England. It contained 2006 inhabitants in 1811.
