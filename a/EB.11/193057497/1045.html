Elater SPRING

sub_entry · 274 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Elater SPRING, in Physics, denotes a natural faculty or endeavour of certain bodies, to return to their first state, after having been violently put out of it by compressing or bending them. This faculty is, by philosophers, usually denominated elastic force, or elasticity.
