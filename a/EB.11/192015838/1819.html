EIDER-Down

sub_entry · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EIDER-Down, the down of the eider-duck. The eider-duck plucks off the down from its breast for the purpose of making its nest, which, after being robbed by those who collect the down, is renewed by the bird till its breast is quite bare.
