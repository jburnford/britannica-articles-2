DEDHAM

DEDHAM, a town of Essex in England, containing 1432 inhabitants in 1811. The streets are not paved, but very clean, occasioned by their lying pretty high. It has one large old church, remarkable for a fine Gothic steeple, with a great deal of carved work about it, but much injured by time. E. Long. 1. 10. N. Lat. 52. 5.
