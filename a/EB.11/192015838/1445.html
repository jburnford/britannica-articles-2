DRAPERY

Cross-references: PAINTING

DRAPERY, in Sculpture and Painting, signifies
the representation of the clothing of human figures, and
also hangings, tapestry, curtains, and most other things
that are not carvings or landscapes. See PAINTING,
CRAYON, DRAWING, and MINIATURE.
