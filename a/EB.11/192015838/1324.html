DONCASTER

article · 257 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DONCASTER, a market town of Yorkshire, 37 miles south of York. It has been long noted for the manufacture of stockings, knitted waistcoats and gloves. A theatre was erected here in 1795. It contained 6935 inhabitants in 1811. W. Long. 1. 12. N. Lat. 53. 33.
