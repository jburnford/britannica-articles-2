DAN

DAN, in Ancient Geography, a town to the west of the source of the Jordan; formerly called Lais (Joshua, Judges, Josephus). This was the north, as Beersheba was the south, boundary of the Israelites; as appears from the common expression in Scripture from Dan to Beersheba. At Dan Jeroboam erected one of the golden calves (1 Kings xii.).
