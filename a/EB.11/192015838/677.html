DENDERMOND

article · 353 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DENDERMOND, a handsome and strong town in the kingdom of the Netherlands, with a strong citadel. It was taken by the allies in 1706, and by the French in 1745. It is surrounded by marshes and fine meadows, which the inhabitants can lay under water when they please. It is seated at the confluence of the Dender and Scheldt. E. Long. 4. 3. N. Lat. 51. 3.
