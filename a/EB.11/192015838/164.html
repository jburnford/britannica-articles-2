CUSTOS SPIRITUALIS

article · 245 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CUSTOS SPIRITUALIS, he that exercises the spiritual jurisdiction of a diocese, during the vacancy of any see, which by the canon law, belongs to the dean and chapter; but at present, in England, to the archbishop of the province by prescription.
