CYTHERA, -orum, in Ancient Geography, an island opposite to Mallea, a promontory, and to Boize a town of Laconia. The island was sacred to Venus, with a very ancient temple of that goddess exhibited in armour at Cythera, as in Cyprus. It is now called Cerigo, and forms one of the seven Ionian islands.
