DONAWERT

article · 210 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DONAWERT, a strong town of Germany. It is situated on the left bank of the Danube, at the influx of the Wernitz. It was formerly an imperial city, but is now subject to Bavaria. E. Long. 10. 32. N. Lat. 48. 32.
