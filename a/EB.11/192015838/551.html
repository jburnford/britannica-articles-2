DEFAMATION

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DEFAMATION, the speaking slanderous words of another; for which the slanderer is punishable, according to the nature of his offence, either by action upon the case at common law, or by statute in the ecclesiastical court.
