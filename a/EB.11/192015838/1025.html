DILLENGEN

article · 229 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DILLENGEN, a town of Germany, in the circle of Suabia, formerly with a university, which was converted into a lyceum when the town was transferred to Bavaria in 1802. It is seated near the Danube. E. Long. 10. 30. N. Lat. 48. 34.
