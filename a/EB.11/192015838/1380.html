DOVE. See COLUMBA, ORNITHOLOGY Index.

Dove-Tailing, in carpentry, is the manner of fastening boards together by letting one piece into another

ther in the form of the tail of a dove. The dove-tail is the strongest of the assemblages or joinings; because the tenon, or piece of wood which is put into the other, goes widening to the extreme, so that it cannot be drawn out again, by reason the extreme or tip is bigger than the hole.
