DORTMUND

article · 284 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DORTMUND, a city of Germany, in the circle of Westphalia. It is pretty large, but not well built. Formerly it was one of the Hanse towns. Its territory also was formerly a county, and had lords of its own. It was afterwards an imperial city, but in 1815 was delivered over to Prussia.
