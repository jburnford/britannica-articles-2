ECLIPTA

article · 191 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ECLIPTA, in Botany, a genus of the polygamia superflua order, belonging to the syngenesia class of plants. The receptacle is chaffy; there is no pappus, and the corollæ of the disk quadrifid.
