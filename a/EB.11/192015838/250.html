CYNOSURUS, in Botany, a genus of plants belonging to the triandria class, and in the natural method ranking under the 4th order, Gramineæ. See BOTANY Index.

CYNTHIUS and CYNTHIA, in Mythology, sur-names of Apollo and Diana, derived from Cynthia, the name of a mountain in the middle of the island of Delos.
