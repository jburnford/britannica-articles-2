DOUGLAS, the principal town of the isle of Man, and which has lately increased both in trade and buildings. The harbour, for ships of a tolerable burden, is the safest in the island, and is much improved by a fine mole that has been built. Population 2631. W. Long. 3. 25. N. Lat. 54. 7.
