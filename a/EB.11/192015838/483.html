DECAN

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DECAN, a kingdom of Asia, in the peninsula on this side the Ganges, bounded on the south by the kingdom of Bisnagar, on the west by the ocean, on the north by Mogulistan, and on the east by the mountains which separate it from Golconda.
