ELASMIS

article · 379 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ELASMIS, in Natural History, an old term applied to tales, composed of small plates in form of spangles; and either single, and not farther fissile; or, if complex, only fissile to a certain degree, and that in somewhat thick laminae.—Of these tales there are several varie-

ties, some with large and others with small spangles, which differ also in colour and other peculiarities.
