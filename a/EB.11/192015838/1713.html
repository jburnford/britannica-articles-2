ECATEA

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ECATEA, Εκάτα, in antiquity, statues erected to the goddess Hecate, for whom the Athenians had a great veneration, believing that she was the overseer of their families, and that she protected their children.
