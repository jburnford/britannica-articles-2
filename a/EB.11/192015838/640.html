DEMETRIA

article · 239 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DEMETRIA, a festival in honour of Ceres, called by the Greeks Demeter. The votaries of the goddess lashed themselves with whips made with the bark of trees. The Athenians had a solemnity of the same name in honour of Demetrius Poliorcetes.
