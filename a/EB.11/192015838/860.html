DIERESIS

DIERESIS, in Medicine, is the consuming of the vessels of an animal body, when, from some corroding cause, certain passages are made which naturally ought not

Diacrisis to have been; or certain natural passages are dilated beyond their ordinary dimensions, so that the humours which ought to have been contained in the vessels extravasate or run out.
