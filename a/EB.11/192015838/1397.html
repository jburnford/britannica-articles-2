DOWNHAM

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DOWNHAM, a market town of Norfolk, 10 miles south of Lynn, famous for its good butter; there being 1000, and sometimes 2000, firkins bought here every Monday, and sent up the river Ouse to Cambridge, and thence to London. Population 1771 in 1811.
