DITTO

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DITTO, in books of accounts, usually written Do, signifies the afore-mentioned. The word is corrupted from the Italian detto, "the said: as in our law-phrase, "the said premises," meaning the same as were afore-mentioned.
