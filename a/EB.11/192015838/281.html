CZASLAU

article · 281 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CZASLAU, a town of Bohemia, and capital of a circle of the same name. The principal church has the highest tower in Bohemia; and near this place the king of Prussia gained a victory over the Austrians in 1742. It is seated on the river Crudenka, in E. Long. 15. 33. N. Lat. 49. 50.
