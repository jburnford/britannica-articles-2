CURATOR

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CURATOR, among the Romans, an officer under the emperors, who regulated the price of all kinds of merchandise and vendible commodities in the cities of the empire. They had likewise the superintendence of the customs and tributes; whence also they were called logistæ.
