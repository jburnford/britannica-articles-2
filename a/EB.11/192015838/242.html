CYNOPHONTIS

article · 202 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CYNOPHONTIS, in antiquity, a festival observed in the dog days at Argos, and so called ἀπὸ τῶν κυνῶν; φόνος, i. e. from killing dogs: because it was usual on this day to kill all the dogs they met with.
