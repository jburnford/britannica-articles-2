DRANK

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DRANK, among farmers, a term used to denote
wild oats, which never fail to infest worn-out lands; so
that, when ploughed lands run to these weeds and
thistles, the farmer knows it is high time to fallow
them, or else to sow them with hay seed, and make
pasture of them.
