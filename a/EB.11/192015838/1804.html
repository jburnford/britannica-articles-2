EFFUSION

article · 181 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EFFUSION, or FUSION, in Astronomy, denotes that part of the sign Aquarius, represented on celestial globes and planispheres, by the water issuing out of the urn of the water-bearer.
