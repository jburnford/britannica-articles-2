DERTOSA, in Ancient Geography, the capital of the Hercaones, in Tarraconensis, or the Hither Spain: a municipium and colony; surnamed Julia Ilergavonia (Coins). Dertosani, the people. Now Tortosa, in Catalonia, on the Ebro. E. Long. 0. 15. N. Lat. 40. 45.
