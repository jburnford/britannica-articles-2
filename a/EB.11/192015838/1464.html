DREPANUM, -i; or Drepana, -orum; a town and port on the west side of Sicily, and to the west of Mount Eryx; Drepanitani the people. Now Trepano, a city and port town on the westmost point of Sicily. E. Long. 12. 8. N. Lat. 38. c.
