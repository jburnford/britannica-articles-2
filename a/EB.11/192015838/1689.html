EASING

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EASING, in the sea language, signifies the slackening a rope or the like. Thus, to ease the bow line, or sheet, is to let them go slack; to ease the helm, is to let the ship go more large, more before the wind, or more larboard.
