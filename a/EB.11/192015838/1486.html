DROME

article · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DROME, a department of France, on the east side of the Rhone. It contained 253,000 inhabitants in 1815, including 34,000 Protestants. It is hilly, and abounds in wood. Wine is a staple produce, and it imports corn. Valence is the chief town.
