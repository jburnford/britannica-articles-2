DIFFUSE

article · 244 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DIFFUSE, an epithet applied to such writings as are wrote in a prolix manner. Among historians, Salust is reckoned sententious, and Livy diffuse. Thus also among the orators, Demosthenes is close and concise; Cicero, on the other hand, diffuse.
