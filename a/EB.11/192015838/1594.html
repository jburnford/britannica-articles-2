DUPLICATE

Cross-references: RATIO

DUPLICATE, among lawyers, denotes a copy of any deed, writing, or account. It is also used for the second letters-patent, granted by the lord chancellor in a case wherein he had before done the same. Also a second letter written and sent to the same party and purpose as a former, for fear of the first's miscarrying, is called a Duplicate.
