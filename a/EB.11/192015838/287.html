DABUL

DABUL, a town of Asia, in the East Indies, on the coast of Malabar, and to the south of the gulf of Cambaye, on a navigable river. It was formerly very flourishing, but is now much decayed. It belongs to the Mahrattas, and its trade consists principally in pepper and salt. E. Long. 72. 50. N. Lat. 17. 30.
