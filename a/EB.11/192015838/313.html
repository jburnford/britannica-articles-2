DAGO

DAGO, or DAGHO, an island in the Baltic sea, on the coast of Livonia, between the gulf of Finland and Riga. It is of a triangular figure, and is about 20 miles in circumference. It has nothing considerable but two castles, called Dagger-wort and Paden. E. Long. 22. 30. N. Lat. 58. 48.
