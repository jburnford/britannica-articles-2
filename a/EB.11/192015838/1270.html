DOGMATICAL

article · 175 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

DOGMATICAL, something belonging to a doctrine or opinion. A dogmatical philosopher is one who asserts things positively; in opposition to a sceptic, who doubts of every thing.
