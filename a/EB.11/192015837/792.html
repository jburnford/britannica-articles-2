OVARIUM

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

OVARIUM, in Botany, a name by which botanists who are fond of assimilating the animal and vegetable kingdoms have distinguished the germin or seed bud, as containing the rudiments of the future seed.
