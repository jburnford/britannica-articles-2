NIEMEN

article · 329 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NIEMEN, a large river of Poland, which rises in Lithuania, where it passes by Bielica, Grodno, and Konno: it afterwards runs through part of Samogitia and Ducal Prussia, where it falls into the lake called the Curisch-kaff, by several mouths, of which the most northern is called the Russ. This lake communicates with the Baltic.
