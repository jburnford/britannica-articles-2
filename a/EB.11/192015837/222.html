NOTTEBURG

article · 281 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NOTTEBURG, a town of Russia, in the province of Ingria, seated on an island in the lake Ladoga, at the place where the river Neva proceeds from this lake. It is strong, has a good citadel, and was capital of the province before Petersburg was built. E. Long. 31. 40. N. Lat. 60. o.
