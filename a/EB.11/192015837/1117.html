PAIREIRA FLAVA

article · 230 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PAIREIRA FLAVA, in the Materia Medica, a kind of oblong and larger root brought from the Brasils.—It is certainly a diuretic of no mean character, and is said to have done great service in nephritic cases, pleurises, and quinsies.
