OETING, a town of Germany, in Upper Bavaria, under the jurisdiction of Buckhausen. It is divided

into the upper and the lower town, and seated on the river Inn, eight miles west of Buckhausen. E. Long. 12. 47. N. Lat. 48. 0. There is a great resort of pilgrims to the old chapel.
