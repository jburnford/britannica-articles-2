ORMSIDE

article · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ORMSIDE, a small town of England, near Appleby, in Westmoreland. A great number of vessels of brass, some of which seemed to have been guilt, were discovered near the manor-house, by the water washing away the soil.
