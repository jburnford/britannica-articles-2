OCTAGON

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

OCTAGON, or OCTOGON, in Geometry, is a figure of eight sides and angles; and this, when all the sides and angles are equal, is called a regular octagon, or one that may be inscribed in a circle.
