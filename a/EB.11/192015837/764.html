OSTUNI

article · 259 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

OSTUNI, a town of Italy, in the kingdom of Naples, and in the Terra di Otranto, with a bishop's see. Its territory is well cultivated, and abounds with olives and almonds. It is seated on a mountain near the gulf of Venice, in E. Long. 17. 49. N. Lat. 49. 59.
