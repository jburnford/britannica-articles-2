ONTARIO, a lake of North America, in the country of the Iroquois, 160 miles in length, and from 60 to 70 in breadth. Many rivers run into this lake: and the

Gatario
Oonella. the great river St Lawrence passes through it. It is the lowest of the great chain of American lakes, and contains 19 islands, most of them small.
