PAO-TING-FOU

article · 363 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PAO-TING-FOU, in China, where the viceroy resides, is the most considerable city in the province next to Pekin. It has 20 others under its jurisdiction, three of the second and 17 of the third class. The country around it is pleasant, and inferior in fertility to no part of China. It is necessary to pass this city in going from Pekin to the province of Chan-si.
