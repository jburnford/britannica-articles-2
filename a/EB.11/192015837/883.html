PÆSTUM, called Posidonia by the Greeks, a town of Lucania, on the Sinus Pæstinus; an ancient colony prior to the first Punic war, according to Livy; but later, according to Velleius. Pæstane rosæ were in great esteem, and produced twice a year (Virgil, Ovid.)
