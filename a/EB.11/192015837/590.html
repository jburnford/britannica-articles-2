OPIUM

article · 223 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: MATERIA MEDICA

OPIUM, in the Materia Medica, is an inspissated juice, obtained from the capsule of the white poppy, partly of the resinous and partly of the gummy kind, and possessing also a narcotic principle. See MATERIA MEDICA, No 612.
