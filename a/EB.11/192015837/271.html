NUN

article · 198 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

NUN, the son of Elishamah, and father of Joshua, of the tribe of Ephraim. The Greeks gave him the name of Nane instead of Nun. This man is known in sacred history only by being the father of Joshua.
