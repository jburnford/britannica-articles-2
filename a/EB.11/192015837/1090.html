PARALOGISM

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PARALOGISM, in Logic, a false reasoning, or a fault committed in demonstration, when a consequence is drawn from principles that are false, or, though true, are not proved; or when a proposition is passed over that should have been proved by the way.
