PAPPENHEIM

article · 421 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PAPPENHEIM, a town of Germany, in the kingdom of Bavaria, and capital of a county of the same name, with a castle, where the counts reside. It is seated near the river Altmal, 17 miles north-west of Neuburg, and 32 south of Nuremberg; and is subject to its own count. E. Long. 10. 51. N. Lat. 48. 58. The count of Pappenheim was hereditary marshal of the empire, and performed his office at the coronation of the emperor.
