OCTAETERIS

article · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

OCTAETERIS, a cycle or term of eight years, in the Grecian chronology, at the conclusion of which three entire lunar months were added. The cycle was in use till Meton's invention of the golden number or cycle of 19 years.
