PARISII

article · 399 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PARISI, in Ancient Geography, a people of Gallia Celtica, inhabiting the country about the Sequana and Matrona. Now a great part of the Isle of France.—Parisi (Ptolemy), a people of Britain, having the Brigantes to the north and west, the German sea to the west, and the Coritani to the south, from whom they were separated by the Humber. Now Holderness, a peninsula of the east riding of Yorkshire.
