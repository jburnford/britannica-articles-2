PARIUM

article · 374 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PARIUM, in Ancient Geography, a noble city of Mysia Minor, with a port on the Propontis; called Adrastia by Homer, according to Pliny; but Strabo distinguishes them: according to others, the Paeustos of Homer. Pariani, the people (Strabo). The birthplace of Neoptolemus surnamed Glossographus (Strabo). Here stood a Cupid equal in exquisite workmanship to the Coidian Venus.
