PALEMBANG

article · 295 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PALEMBANG, a town and district on the north-east coast of the island of Sumatra, where the Dutch had a factory. The town is situated on a river of the same name, about 60 miles from the sea. The religion is Mahometan. The town and district were taken possession of by the British forces in 1812.
