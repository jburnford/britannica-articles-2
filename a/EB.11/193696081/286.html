TAMBOURIN

article · 397 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TAMBOURIN, is the name of a dance performed on the French stage. The air is lively, and the movements are quick.

The same name is applied to a musical instrument, formed of a hoop, over which is stretched a piece of parchment or vellum, while bells and hollow hemi-

spheres of brass are loosely hung in holes cut in the Tambourin. The tambourin is used only as an accompani-
ment to other instruments.
