TRICOCCEÆ

TRICOCCEÆ, (τρίς, "three," and κόκκος, "a grain"), the name of the 38th order in Linnæus's Fragments of a Natural Method, consisting of plants with a single three-cornered capsule, having three cells, or internal divisions, each containing a single seed. See BOTANY.
