VERMILION, a bright and beautiful red colour, composed of quicksilver and sulphur, in great esteem among the ancients under the name of minium. See CHEMISTRY, No 1701, and 1713; but what goes by the name of minium amongst us, is a preparation of lead, known also by the name of red-lead. See CHEMISTRY, No 1832.
