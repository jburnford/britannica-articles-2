TARPA, SPURIUS MECIUS

article · 257 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TARPA, SPURIUS MECIUS, a Latin critic in the time of Julius Cæsar and Augustus. He had his tribunal in the temple of Apollo, where, with four assistants, he passed sentence on the works of the poets. Cicero and Horace make honourable mention of this critic.
