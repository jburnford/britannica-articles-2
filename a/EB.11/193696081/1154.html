VERONESE

VERONESE, a district of Italy, in the Austro-Venetian territories; bounded on the north by the Trentino, on the east by the Vicentino and Paduano, on the south by the Mantuano, and on the west by the Bresciano. It is about 35 miles in length, and 27 in breadth; and fertile in corn, wine, fruits, and cattle.
