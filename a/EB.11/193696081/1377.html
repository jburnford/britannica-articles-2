URINE

URINE, a fluid, separated from the blood, and carried by the emulgent arteries to the kidneys, from whence it descends to the bladder by the ureters, and is from time to time emitted thence by the canal of the urethra. See ANATOMY, No 107. For the properties of urine, see CHEMISTRY Index.
