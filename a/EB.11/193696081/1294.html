UNCIA

article · 189 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

UNCIA, in general, a Latin term, denoting the twelfth part of any thing; particularly the twelfth part of a pound, called in English an ounce; or the twelfth part of a foot, called an inch.
