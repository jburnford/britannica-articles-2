WURTSBURG

article · 156 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WURTSBURG, a large bishopric in Germany, situated on the Maine, erected into a dukedom in 1806, and given to Bavaria in 1814 as a compensation for Salzburg.
