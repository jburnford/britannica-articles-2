YARE

article · 238 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

YARE, among sailors, implies ready or quick: as he yare at the helm; that is, he quick, ready, and expeditious at the helm. It is sometimes also used for bright by seamen: as, to keep his arms yare; that is, to keep them clean and bright.
