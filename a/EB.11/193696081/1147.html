VERMIN

article · 202 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VERMIN is a general term, denoting those animals which are either directly or indirectly injurious to mankind, the inferior animals, or the fruits of the earth; as fleas, caterpillars, flies, worms, &c.
