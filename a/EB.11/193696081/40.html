SUPERFETATION

article · 391 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SUPERFETATION, in Physiology, a second or after-conception, happening when the mother, already pregnant, conceives of a latter coition; so that she bears at once two fetuses of unequal age and bulk, and is delivered of them at different times. We meet with instances of superfetations in Hippocrates, Aristotle, Du Laurens, &c.: but they are said to be much more frequent in hares and swine.
