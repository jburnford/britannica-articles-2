WOLGAST

article · 313 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WOLGAST, a considerable town of Germany, in the circle of Upper Saxony, and in Pomerania, capital of a territory of the same name, with a castle, and one of the best and largest harbours on the Baltic sea. It is a well built place, subject to Prussia, and seated on the river Psin. E. Long. 13. 42. N. Lat. 54. 4.
