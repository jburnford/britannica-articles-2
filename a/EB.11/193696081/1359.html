VOWEL

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VOWEL, in Grammar, a letter which affords a complete sound of itself, or a letter so simple as only to need a bare opening of the mouth to make it heard, and to form a distinct voice. The vowels are six in number, viz. A, E, I, O, U, Y.
