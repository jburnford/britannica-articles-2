TAIL

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TAIL, the train of a beast, bird, or fish; which in land animals, it is said, serves to drive away flies, &c. and in birds and fishes to direct their course, and assist them in ascending or descending in the air or water. But the tail in all animals is of great use in directing their motions.
