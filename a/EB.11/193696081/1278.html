ULM

article · 612 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ULM, formerly a free and imperial city of Germany, but now belonging to Bavaria. It is a pretty large place, defended by fortifications; and the inhabitants, who are chiefly Protestants, amounted to 14,000 in 1800. The town is seated on the Danube, a little above the confluence of that river and the Iller, and carries on a considerable trade. It has many handsome buildings. The principal church is a magnificent Gothic edifice, and is said to surpass all the cathedrals in Germany in height. The Roman Catholics have but two churches, all the rest belonging to the Protestants. E. Long. 9. 59. N. Lat. 48. 25.
