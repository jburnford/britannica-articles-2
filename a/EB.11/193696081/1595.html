WHITE Sea

sub_entry · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

WHITE Sea, is a bay of the Frozen ocean, so called in the north part of Muscovy, lying between Russian Lapland and Sainoidea; at the bottom of which stands the city of Archangel. This was the chief port the Russians had before their conquest of Livonia.
