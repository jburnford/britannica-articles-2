TROGLODYTES

article · 245 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TROGLODYTES, in the Ancient Geography, a people of Ethiopia, said to have lived in caves under ground. Pomponius Melo gives a strange account of the Trogloodytes: he says they did not so properly speak as shriek; and that they lived on serpents.
