TERNATE

article · 315 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TERNATE, the most northerly of the Molucca or Clove islands in the East Indies. It abounds in coconuts, bananas, citrons, oranges, and other fruits peculiar to the torrid zone; but cloves are the most valuable produce. It is in the possession of the Dutch. Malaya is the capital town. E. Long. 129. 0. N. Lat. 1. 0.
