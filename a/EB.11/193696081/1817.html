ZAPATA, a kind of feast or ceremony held in Italy in the courts of certain princes, on St Nicholas's day; wherein people hide presents in the shoes or slip-

pers of those they would do honour to, in such a manner as may surprise them on the morrow when they come to dress; being done in imitation of the practice of St Nicholas, who used in the night-time to throw purses of money in at the windows to marry poor maids withal.
