TRAJAN, MARCUS ULPIUS

article · 275 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TRAJAN, MARCUS ULPIUS, a celebrated Roman emperor, who gained many victories over the Parthians and Germans, pushing the empire to its utmost extent on the east and north sides. He died at Silinunte, a city of Cilicia, which from him was called Trajanopolis, in the year 117.
