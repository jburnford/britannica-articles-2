VERTICILLUS

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VERTICILLUS, a mode of flowering, in which the flowers are produced in rings at each joint of the stem, with very short foot-stalks. The term is exemplified in mint, horehound, and the other plants of the natural order described above.
