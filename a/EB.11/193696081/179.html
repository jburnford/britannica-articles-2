SYMPTOMATICAL

article · 212 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SYMPTOMATICAL, in Medicine, is a term often used to denote the difference between the primary and secondary causes in diseases: thus a fever from pain is said to be symptomatical, because it rises from pain only.
