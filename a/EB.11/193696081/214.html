SYSTEM, in general, denotes an assemblage or

chain of principles and conclusions, or the whole of any doctrine, the several parts whereof are bound together, and follow or depend on each other; in which sense we say a system of philosophy, system of divinity, &c. The word is formed from the Greek συστασις, "composition, compages." System
