TACAMAHACA

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TACAMAHACA, in Pharmacy, a gum resin, obtained from the fagara octandra and populus balsamifera; and having a fragrant smell, a bitterish nauseous taste, and supposed to be stimulant and tonic in its effects.
