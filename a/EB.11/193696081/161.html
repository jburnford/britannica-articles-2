SYLVIA, a genus of birds, belonging to the order of passerines, formed by Dr Latham by limiting the motacilla to the wagtail, and arranging the other species, formerly classed under that genus, under the sylvia. He

making 13 species of the motacilla, and 175 species of the sylvia. See MOTACILLA, ORNITHOLOGY Index.
