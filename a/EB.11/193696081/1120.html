VENTRI Inspiciendo

sub_entry · 168 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

VENTRI Inspiciendo, is a writ to search a woman that saith she is with child, and thereby withholdeth lands from the next heir: the trial whereof is by a jury of women.
