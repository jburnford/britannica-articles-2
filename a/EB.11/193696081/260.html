TAHOERWA

article · 164 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

TAHOERWA, one of the Sandwich islands, is small, destitute of wood, the soil sandy and unfertile; is situated in north latitude 20° 38', in east longitude 203° 27'.
