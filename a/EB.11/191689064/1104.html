MASSILIA

MASSILIA, in Ancient Geography, a town of Gallia Narbonensis, peopled by a colony of Phocæans, from Phocæa, a city of Ionia, universally celebrated, not only for its port, commerce, and strength, but especially for its politeness of manners and for its learning. Now Marseille, a city of Provence.
