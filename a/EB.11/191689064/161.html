LITHANTHRAX

Cross-references: MINERALOGY

LITHANTHRAX, or Pit-Coal, is a black or brown, laminated, bituminous substance; not very easily inflammable, but, when once inflamed, burns longer and more intensely than any other substance. See MINERALOGY Index.
