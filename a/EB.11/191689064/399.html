LULLI

LULLI, JOHN BARTIST, the most celebrated and most excellent musician that has appeared in France since the revival of learning, was born at Florence. He was taken to France when very young by a person of quality; and he carried the art of playing on the violin to the highest perfection. Louis XIV. made him superintendent of music. Some time after Perinna having introduced operas into France, and quarrelling with his company, he resigned his privilege to Lulli.
