LOVENTINUM

LOVENTINUM, or LUENTINUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Demetee in Britain, near the mouth of the Tuerobis or Tivy. Supposed to have been afterwards swallowed up by an earthquake, and to have stood where is now the lake called Llin Sacat in Brecknockshire.
