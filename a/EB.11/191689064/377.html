LUCUS

LUCUS, in general, denotes a wood or grove sa-

cred to a deity; so called à luvendo, because a great number of lights were usually burning in honour of the god (Isidorus); a practice common with idolaters, as we learn from Scripture: hence Homer's αὐλὸν αὐλὸν.
