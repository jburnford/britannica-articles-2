LOCRI, a country of Achaia in Greece; twofold,
and divided by Mount Parnassus. The Hither was occu-
pied by the Locri Ozolæ, called also Zephyrii, or
Western, contained between Ætolia and Phocis. The
Farther Locri lay beyond Parnassus, running out to-
wards Thermopylæ, and reaching to the Euripus of
Eubœa; occupied by the Locri Opuntii, and Epicne-
midii, who were called the Eastern Locri.
