MADIAN

MADIAN, in Ancient Geography, a town of Arabia Petraea, near the Arnon; so called from one of the sons of Abraham by Ketura; in ruins in Jerome's time. Jerome mentions another MADIAN, or MIDIAN, beyond Arabia, in the desert, to the south of the Red sea; and hence Madianai, and Madianitai, the people; and Madianaea Regio, the country.
