LYMINGTON

LYMINGTON, a borough town of Hampshire in England, 97 miles south-west of London. It stands about a mile from the channel running between the main land and the isle of Wight; and has a harbour for vessels of considerable burden. The tide flows near a mile above the town. It has a market on Saturdays; and sends two members to parliament. The population in 1811 was estimated at 2641.
