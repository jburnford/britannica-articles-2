LORDOSIS

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: SURGERY

LORDOSIS, (of lordos, bent inwards), in the medical writings, a name given to a distempered state of the spine, in which it is bent inwards, or towards the anterior parts. It is used in opposition to gibbous, or hump-backed. See SURGERY.
