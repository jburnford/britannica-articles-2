MALTON

MALTON, a town of the north riding of Yorkshire in England, seated on the river Derwent, over which there is a good stone bridge. It is composed of two towns, the New and the Old, and contained 3713 inhabitants in 1811. It is well accommodated with good inns, and sends two members to parliament. W. Long. 40. N. Lat. 54. 8.
