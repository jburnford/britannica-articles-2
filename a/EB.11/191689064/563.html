MACERATA

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MACERATA, a town of Italy, in the territory of the church, and in the Marche of Ancona, with a bishop's see, an university, and about 10,000 inhabitants. It is situated near the mountain Chiento, in E. Long. 13. 37. N. Lat. 43. 15.
