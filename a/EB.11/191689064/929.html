MARENGO

article · 327 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MARENGO, a village of Italy which has risen to distinction in consequence of a bloody battle between the Austrians and French, which was fought on the 14th June 1800, in which the French say, 15,000 of the former were killed, wounded, and made prisoners. — Marengo lies 3 miles S. E. of Alessandria, in the kingdom of Sardinia.
