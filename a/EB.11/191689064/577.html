MACROBIUS

MACROBIUS, a people of Ethiopia, celebrated for their justice, and the innocency of their manners: also a people in the island of Merœ. The Hyperboreans were also called Macrobii: they generally lived to their 120th year; and from their longevity they obtained their name (μακρος βιος, long life.)
