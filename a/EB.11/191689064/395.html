LUGGERSHALI, a borough of Wiltshire, 12 miles north of Salisbury, and 75 north by west of London. It is an ancient borough by prescription, though but a small hamlet, near the forest of Chute, in a delightful country; and was the residence of several kings. It had formerly a castle. It is governed by a bailiff chosen yearly at the lord of the manor's court-feet. On the neighbouring downs there used to be horse-races. Population in 1811, 487.
