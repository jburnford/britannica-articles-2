Possile LINEN

sub_entry · 315 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: AMIANTHUS

Possile LINEN, is a kind of amianthus, which consists of flexible, parallel, soft fibres, and which has been celebrated for the use to which it has been applied, of being woven, and forming an incombustible cloth. Paper also, and wicks for lamps, have been made of it. See AMIANTHUS, ASBESTOS, and MINERALOGY Index.
