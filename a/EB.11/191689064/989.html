MARMANDE

article · 217 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MARMANDE, a town of France, in the department of Lot and Garonne, containing about 4000 inhabitants. It carries on a great trade in corn and wine, and is seated on the river Garonne, in E. Long. 0. 15. N. Lat. 44. 30.
