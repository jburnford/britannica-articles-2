HOBBY, the name of a hawk called by some authors subbuteo. See FALCO, ORNITHOLOGY Index.

It is a hawk of the lure, and not of the fist; and is very like the saker, only much less. It makes excellent sport with net and spaniels; for when the birds see the hobby, they dare not commit themselves to the wing, but lie close to the ground, and so are taken in nets.
