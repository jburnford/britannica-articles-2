GUASTALLA

article · 302 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GUASTALLA, a strong town of Italy, with the title of a duchy, remarkable for a battle between the French and Imperialists in 1734. It was ceded to the duke of Parma in 1748, and now belongs to the late empress of France, Maria Louisa. It is seated near the river Po, in E. Long. 10. 38. N. Lat. 44. 55.
