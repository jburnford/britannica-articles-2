HERSCHEL

article · 193 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HERSCHEL, the name by which the French, and most other European nations, call the planet discovered by Dr Herschel in the year 1781. The Italians call it Uranos, and the British Georgium Sidus.
