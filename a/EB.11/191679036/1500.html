HUBNER, JOHN

article · 245 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HUBNER, JOHN, a learned geographer of Germany, taught geography at Leipsic and Hamburg with extraordinary reputation; and died at Hamburg in 1732, aged 63. His principal work is A Geographical Treatise, printed at Basil in 1746, in 6 vols. 12mo.
