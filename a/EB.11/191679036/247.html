GRUBENHAGEN

GRUBENHAGEN, a principality in the duchy of Brunswick, in Lower Saxony, remarkable for its mines of silver, copper, iron, and lead. The Hartz forest, the ancient Hercynian, is a mountain covered with trees and abounding with mines.
