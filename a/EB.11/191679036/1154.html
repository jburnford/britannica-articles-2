HIERES

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HIERES, the name of some small islands lying near the coast of Provence in France, opposite to the towns of Hieres and Toulon, where the English fleet lay many months in 1744, and blocked up the French and Spanish fleets in the harbour of Toulon.
