HEAD-MOULD-SHOT

article · 501 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEAD-MOULD-SHOT, a disease in children, wherein the sutures of the skull, generally the coronal, ride; that is, have their edges shot one over another; and are so close locked together, as to compress the internal parts, the meninges, or even the brain itself. The disease usually occasions convulsions, and is supposed to admit of no cure from medicine, unless room could be given by manual operation or a divulsion of the sutures.

The head-mould-shot is the disorder opposite to the horse-shoe head.
