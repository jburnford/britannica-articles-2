HETEROGENEOUS

article · 299 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HETEROGENEOUS, or HETEROGENEAL, literally imports something of a different nature, or that consists of parts of different or dissimilar kinds; in opposition to homogeneous. The word is Greek; formed of ἱτερος, alter, "different," and γένος, genus, "kind;" q. d. composed of different kinds of parts.
