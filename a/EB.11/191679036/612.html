HARCOURT

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HARCOURT, a town of France, in the department of Calvados, about 12 miles south of Caen. From it a once noble family in France, derived their ducal title; and from it also, it is said, sprang the noble family of the same name in England.
