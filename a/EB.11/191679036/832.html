HEEL

article · 237 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEEL, in the sea-language. If a ship leans on one side, whether she be aground or afloat, then it is said she heels a-starboard, or a-port; or that she heels off-wards, or to the shore; that is, inclines more to one side than to another.
