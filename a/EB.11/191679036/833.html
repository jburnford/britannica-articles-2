HEELER

article · 195 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEELER, or Bloody-HEEL Cock, a fighting cock, that strikes or wounds much with his spurs.

The masters know such a cock, even while a chick, by the striking of his two heels together in his going.
