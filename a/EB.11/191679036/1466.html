HOUGH

Cross-references: HAM

HOUGH, HAM, in the manège, the joint of the hind leg of a beast, which connects the thigh to the leg. See HAM.

To Houan, or cut the Houghs, is to ham-string, or to disable by cutting the sinews of the ham.
