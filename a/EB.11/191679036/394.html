GUTHALUS

article · 191 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GUTHALUS, or GUTTALUS, in Ancient Geography, is thought to be the Viadrus of Ptolemy. Now the Oder, which rising in Moravia, runs through Silesia, Brandenburg, and Pomerania, into the Baltic.
