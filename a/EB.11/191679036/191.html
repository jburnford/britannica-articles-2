GRINDING, or TRITURATION

GRINDING, or TRITURATION, the act of breaking or comminuting a solid body, and reducing it into powder. See PULVERISATION and LEVIGATION.

The painters colours are grinded on a marble or porphyry, either with oil or gum-water.
