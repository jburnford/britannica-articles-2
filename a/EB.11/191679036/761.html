HAZLE Earth

HAZLE Earth, or Hazley Earth, a kind of red loam, which is said to be an excellent mixture with other sorts of earth; uniting what is too loose, cooling what is too hot, and gently retaining the moisture.

Witch-HAZLE. See HAMAMELIS.
