HORREA

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HORREA, in Roman antiquity, were public magazines of corn and salt-beef, out of which the soldiers were furnished on their march in the military roads of the empire. Horrea was also the name which they gave to their granaries.
