HEPTACHORD

article · 322 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEPTACHORD, in the ancient poetry, signified verses that were sung or played on seven chords, that is, on seven different notes. In this sense it was applied to the lyre when it had but seven strings. One of the intervals is also called an heptachord, as containing

taining the same number of degrees between the extremes.
