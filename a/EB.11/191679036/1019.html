HERENAUSEN

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HERENAUSEN, a palace of Germany near Hanover, formerly belonging to the king of Great Britain. Here are lodgings for all the court; and a garden of vast extent, in which are fine waterworks, a labyrinth, and many other curiosities worthy the observation of a traveller.
