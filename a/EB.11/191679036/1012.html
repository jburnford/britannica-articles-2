HEREDITAMENTS

article · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HEREDITAMENTS, whatever moveable things a person may have to himself and his heirs by way of inheritance; and which, if not otherwise bequeathed, descend to him who is next heir, and not to the executor as chattels do.
