HÆMAGOGOS

article · 226 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HÆMAGOGOS, among physicians, a compound medicine, consisting of fetid and aromatic simples mixed with black hellebore, and prescribed in order to promote the menstrual and hæmorrhoidal fluxes; as also to bring away the lochia.
