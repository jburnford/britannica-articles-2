HOULSWORTHY

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HOULSWORTHY, a large town of Devonshire, seated between two branches of the river Tamar, having a good market for corn and provisions. It had 1206 inhabitants in 1811. W. Long. 2. 42. N. Lat. 50. 50.
