HALIEUTICS

HALIEUTICS, HALIEUTICA, Ἀλιευτικά, formed of ἄλιος, fisherman, which is derived from ἄλις, sea; books treating of fishes, or the art of fishing. We have still extant the halieutics of Oppian.
