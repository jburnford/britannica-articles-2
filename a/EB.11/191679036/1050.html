HERMOGENES

article · 302 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HERMOGENES, the first and most celebrated architect of antiquity, was, according to Vitruvius, born at Alanbada, a city in Caria. He built a temple of Diana at Magnesia; another of Bacchus at Tros; and was the inventor of several parts of architecture. He composed a book on the subject, which is lost.
