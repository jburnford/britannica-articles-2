HARIOT

article · 253 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HARIOT, or HERRIOT, in Law, a due belonging to a lord at the death of his tenant, consisting of the best beast, either horse or cow, or ox, which he had at the time of his death; and in some manors the best goods, piece of plate, &c. are called hariots.
