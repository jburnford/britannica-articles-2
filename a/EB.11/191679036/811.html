HECATOMBOEON

article · 304 chars · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: HECATOMB

HECATOMBOEON was the first month of the Athenian year, consisting of 30 days; beginning on the

first new moon after the summer solstice, and consequently answering to the latter part of our June and the beginning of July. It had its name from the great number of hecatombs sacrificed in it. See HECATOMB.
