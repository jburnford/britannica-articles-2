HALCYON DOYS

article · 335 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HALCYON DOYS, in antiquity, a name given to seven days before and as many after the winter solstice; by reason the halcyon, invited by the calmness of the weather, laid its eggs in nests built in the rocks, close by the brink of the sea, at this season; and hence halcyon doys is a phrase expressive of times of peace and tranquillity.
