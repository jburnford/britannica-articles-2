HETEROGENEITY

article · 250 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HETEROGENEITY, in Physics, the quality or disposition which denominates a thing heterogeneous. The word is also used for the heterogeneous parts themselves; in which sense, the heterogeneities of a body are the same thing with the impurities thereof.
