HOPLITODROMOS

article · 308 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HOPLITODROMOS, (formed of ἵππος, armour; and δριμῶν, I run), in the ancient gymnastic sports, a term applied to such persons as went through those toilsome and robust exercises in complete armour; by which the exercise became much more violent, and the wearing of armour in the time of battle much more easy.
