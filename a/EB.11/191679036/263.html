GUADALUPE

GUADALUPE, a handsome town in Spain, in Estramadura, with a celebrated convent, whose structure is magnificent, and is immensely rich. It is seated on a rivulet of the same name; 45 miles south-west from Toledo. W. Long. 4. 45. N. Lat. 39. 12.
