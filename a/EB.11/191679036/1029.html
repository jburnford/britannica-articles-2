HERITAGE

article · 324 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

HERITAGE, in Scots Law, lands, houses, or any immoveable subject, in contradistinction to moveables or moveable subjects. It also sometimes signifies such immoveable property as a person succeeds to as heir to another, in contradistinction to that which he himself purchases or acquires in any other manner, called conquest.
