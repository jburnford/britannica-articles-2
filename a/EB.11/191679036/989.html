HEPTANDRIA

HEPTANDRIA, in Botany, (from ἑπτά, septem, and ἄνδρ, a man); the seventh class in Linnaeus's sexual method, consisting of plants with hermaphrodite flowers, which have seven stamens or male organs. See Classification under BOTANY.
