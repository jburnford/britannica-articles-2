HIRSCHFELD, a town of Germany, in the circle

of the Upper Rhine, and capital of a principality of Hirschfeld the same name, depending on a famous abbey which was secularized in favour of the house of Cassel. It is seated on the river Fulda, in E. Long. 9. 52. N. Lat. 51. 46.
