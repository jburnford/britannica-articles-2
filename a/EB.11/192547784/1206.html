RECLAIMING

article · 375 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RECLAIMING, or RECLAMING, in our ancient customs, a lord's pursuing, prosecuting, and recalling, his vassal, who had gone to live in another place without his permission.

Reclaiming is also used for the demanding of a person, or thing, to be delivered up to the prince or state to which it properly belongs: when, by any irregular means, it is come into another's possession.
