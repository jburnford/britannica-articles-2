POLESIA

article · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

POLESIA, a province of Poland, bounded by Polachia and Proper Lithuania on the north, and by Volhina on the south. It is one of the palatinates of Lithuania, and is commonly called Brescia, and its capital is of this name. It is full of forests and lakes.
