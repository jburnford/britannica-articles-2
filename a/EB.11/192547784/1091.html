RANGER

article · 248 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RANGER, a sworn officer of a forest, appointed by the king's letters patent; whose business is to walk through his charge, to drive back the deer out of the public, &c. and to present all trespasses within his jurisdiction at the next forest court.
