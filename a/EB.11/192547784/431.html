PRESTATION (præstatio)

sub_entry · 149 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PRESTATION (præstatio), was anciently used for other payments: Et quieti sint de præstatone muragii. Chart. Hen. VII. Sometimes also for pourveyance.
