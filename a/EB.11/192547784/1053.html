RAM. See OVIS, MAMMALIA Index.

Battering Ram, in antiquity, a military engine used to batter down the walls of besieged places. See BATTURING Ram.

Ram's Head, in a ship, is a great block belonging to the fore and main halyards. It has three shivers in it, in which the halyards are put; and in a hole at the end are reeved the ties.
