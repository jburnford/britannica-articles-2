RHINFELD

article · 268 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHINFELD, a small but strong town of Germany, formerly belonging to Austria, but now to the grand duchy of Baden. It has been often taken and retaken in the German wars; and is seated on the Rhine, over which there is a handsome bridge. E. Long. 7. 53. N. Lat. 47. 40.
