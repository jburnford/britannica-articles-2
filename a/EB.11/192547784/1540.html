RHETORIANS

article · 255 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHETORIANS, a sect of heretics in Egypt, so denominated from Rhetorius their leader. The distinguishing tenet of this heresy, as represented by Philastrinus, was, that he approved of all the heresies before him, and taught that they were all in the right.
