POLYHYMNIA

article · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

POLYHYMNA, in the pagan mythology, one of the nine muses, thus named from the Greek words πολύ, "much," and μνη, "memory." She presided over history, or rather rhetoric; and is represented with a crown of pearls and a white robe; her right hand in action as if haranguing, and holding in her left a caduceus or sceptre to show her power.
