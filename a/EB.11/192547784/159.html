PONTYPOOL

PONTYPOOL, a town of Monmouthshire in England, seated between two hills. It is a small place; but noted for its iron-mills, and great manufacture of japanned vessels, called Pontypool. W. Long. 3. 6. N. Lat. 51. 42.
