REAR-Guard

sub_entry · 342 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

REAR-Guard, is that body of an army which marches after the main-body; for the march of an army is always composed of an advanced-guard, a main body, and a rear-guard; the first and last commanded by a general. The old grand guards of the camp always form the rear-guard of the army, and are to see that every thing come safe to the new camp.
