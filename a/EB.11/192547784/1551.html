RHINEBERG

article · 175 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

RHINEBERG, a town of Germany, in the circle of the Lower Rhine, and diocese of Cologne, now belonging to Prussia. It is seated on the Rhine, in E. Long. 6. 39. N. Lat. 51. 30.
