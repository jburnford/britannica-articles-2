RETULINGEN

article · 266 chars · page image at NLS ↗

RETULINGEN, a handsome, and formerly a free and imperial town of Germany, in the kingdom of Wirtemberg; seated in a plain on the river Eschez, near the Neckar, adorned with handsome public buildings, and has a well frequented college. E. Long. 9. 10. N. Lat. 48. 31.
