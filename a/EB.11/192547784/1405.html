REPENTANCE

article · 391 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: REPENTANCE AND THEOLOGY

REPENTANCE, in general, means sorrow for any thing past. In theology it means such a sorrow for sin as produces newness of life, or such a conviction of the evil and danger of a sinful course as is sufficient to produce shame and sorrow in the review of it, and effectual resolutions of amendment. In this sense the evangelical writers use μετανοία and μετάνωια. See REPENTANCE AND THEOLOGY.
