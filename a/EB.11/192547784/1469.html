RETE MIRABILE

RETE MIRABILE, in Anatomy, a small plexus or network of vessels in the brain, surrounding the pituitary gland.

RETENTION is defined by Mr Locke to be, a faculty of the mind, whereby it keeps or retains those simple ideas it has once received, by sensation or reflection. See METAPHYSICS, Part I. Chap. ii.
