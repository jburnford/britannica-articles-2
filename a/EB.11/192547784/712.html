PUBERTY

article · 208 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PUBERTY, in Law, is fixed at the age of 12 in females, and 14 in males; after which they are reckoned to be fit for marriage. But as to crimes and punishments, the age of puberty is fixed at 14 in both sexes.
