PRÆTORIA AUGUSTA

PRÆTORIA AUGUSTA (Ptolemy), a town of Dacia. Now called Brassow by the natives, and Cronstat by the Germans (Baudrand): a town of Transylvania. E. Long. 25°. N. Lat. 47°.—Another of the Salassii, near the two gates or dehes of the Alps, the Grajce and Pennine (Pliny); a Roman colony settled by Augustus after the defeat of the Salassii by Terentius Varro, on the spot where he encamped (Strabo, Dio Cassius, Ptolemy), situated on the river Duria Major. The town is now called Aosta or Aoust, in Piedmont. E. Long. 7. 14. N. Lat. 45. 19.
