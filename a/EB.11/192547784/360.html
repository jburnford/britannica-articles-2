PRECIAE, (precious, "early"), the name of the 21st order in Linnaeus's fragments of a natural method; consisting of primrose, an early flowering plant, and a few

few genera which agree with it in habit and structure, though not always in the character or circumstance expressed in the title. See BOTANY, Natural Orders.
