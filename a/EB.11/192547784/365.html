PRECORDIA

article · 194 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

PRECORDIA, in Anatomy, a general name for the parts situated about the heart, in the forepart of the thorax; as the diaphragm, pericardium, and even the heart itself, with the spleen, lungs, &c.
