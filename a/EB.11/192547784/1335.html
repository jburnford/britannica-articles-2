REGENSBURG

article · 249 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

REGENSBURG, a handsome though small town of Switzerland, in the canton of Zurich, and capital of a bailiwick of the same name, with a strong castle, seated on a hill, which is part of Mount Jura. There is a well sunk through a rock, 36 fathoms deep.
