RESTORATIVE, in Medicine, a remedy proper for restoring and retrieving the strength and vigour both of the body and animal spirits.

All under this class, says Quincy, are rather nutritional than medicinal; and are more administered to repair the wastes of the constitution, than to alter and rectify its disorders.
