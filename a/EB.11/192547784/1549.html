Lower Circle of the RHINE

sub_entry · 178 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Lower Circle of the RHINE, consisted of the palatinate of the Rhine, and the three ecclesiastical electorates, viz. those of Cologne, Mentz, and Triers, now belonging to Prussia.
