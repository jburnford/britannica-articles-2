REBUTTER

article · 244 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

REBUTTER, (from the Fr. bonter, i. e. repellere, to put back or bar), is the answer of defendant to plaintiff's surrejoinder; and plaintiff's answer to the rebutter is called a surrebutter: but it is very rare the parties go so far in pleading.
