REFUGEES

article · 408 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

REFUGEES, a term at first applied to the French Protestants, who, by the revocation of the edict of Naniz, were constrained to fly from persecution, and take refuge in foreign countries. Since that time, however, it has been extended to all such as leave their country in time of distress; and hence, since the revolt of the British

British colonies in America, we have frequently heard of American refugees.
