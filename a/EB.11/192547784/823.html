PYROPHORUS, formed of πυρ, "fire," and φῶς, "I bear," in Chemistry, the name usually given to that substance called by some black phosphorus; a chemical preparation possessing the singular property of kindling spontaneously when exposed to the air. See CHEMISTRY Index.
