KESTREL

article · 228 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: FALCO

KESTREL, the English name of a hawk, called also the stannel and the windhover, and by authors the tinniculus and cheneris. It builds with us in hollow oaks, and feeds on partridges and other birds. See FALCO, ORNITHOLOGY INDEX.
