INGENUOUS (ingenuus), in Roman antiquity, an appellation given to persons born of free parents, who had never been slaves: for the children of the liberti,

Ingenuous or persons who had obtained their liberty, were called libertini, not ingenui; this appellation of ingenuus being reserved for their children, or the third generation.
