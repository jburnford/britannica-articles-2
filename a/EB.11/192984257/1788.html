LEECH-Rope

sub_entry · 252 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: BOLT-R

LEECH-Rope, a name given to that part of the bolt-rope to which the border or skirt of a sail is sewed. In all sails whose opposite leeches are of the same length, it is terminated above the earring, and below the clue. See BOLT-Rope, CLUE, and EARING.
