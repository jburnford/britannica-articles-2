IULUS

article · 188 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IULUS, a son of Ascanius, born in Lavinium. In the succession to the kingdom of Alba, Æneas Sylvius, the son of Æneas and Lavinia, was preferred to him. He was, however, made chief priest.
