IGNOMINIA

article · 315 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IGNOMINIA, a species of punishment amongst the Romans, whereby the offender suffered public shame, either by virtue of the praetor's edict, or by order of the censor. This punishment, besides the scandal, deprived the party of the privilege of bearing any offices, and almost all other liberties of a Roman citizen.
