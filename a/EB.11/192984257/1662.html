LAUDICOENI

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LAUDICOENI, amongst the Romans, applauders, who for reward entered the rehearsal-rooms, attended the repetition of plays, and were in waiting when orations were pronounced, in order to raise or increase the acclamation and applause.
