INTEGUMENTS

Cross-references: ANATOMY

INTEGUMENTS, in Anatomy, denote the common coverings which invest the body; as the cuticula, cutis, &c. See ANATOMY.

INTEGUMENT is also extended to the particular membranes which invest certain parts of the body; as the coats or tonics of the eye.
