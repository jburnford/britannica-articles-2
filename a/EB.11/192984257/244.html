IDIOM

article · 206 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IDIOM, among grammarians, properly signifies the peculiar genius of each language, but is often used in a synonymous sense with dialect. The word is Greek, idiōmā, "propriety;" formed of idō, "proper, own."
