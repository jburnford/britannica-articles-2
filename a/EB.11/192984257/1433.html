LADOGA

LADOGA, New, a town in the Russian government of Petersburg, seated on the Volkhof, between the canal and lake of Ladoga. Old Ladoga is higher up the river, and a place of no great extent. The former is 70 miles east of Petersburg, in N. Lat. 60° E. Long. 21° 44'.
