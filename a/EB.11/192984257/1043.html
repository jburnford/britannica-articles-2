JUSTIFICATION, in Theology, that act of grace which renders a man just in the sight of God, and worthy of eternal happiness. See THEOLOGY.

Different sects of Christians hold very different opinions concerning the doctrine of justification; some contending for justification by faith alone, and others by good works.
