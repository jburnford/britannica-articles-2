KERN, or KERNE, a term in the ancient Irish militia, signifying a foot soldier. Camden tells us, the armies of Ireland consisted of cavalry, called golloglasses; and infantry, lightly armed, called kernes.—The kerns bore swords and darts; to the last were fitted cords, by which they could recover them after they had been launched out.
