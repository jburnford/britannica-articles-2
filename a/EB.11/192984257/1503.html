LANCIANO

LANCIANO, a considerable town of Italy, in the kingdom of Naples, and in the Hither Abruzzo, with an archbishop's see; famous for its fairs, which are held in July and August. It is seated on the river Feltrino near that of Sangor. E. Long. 14. 20. N. Lat. 42. 12.
