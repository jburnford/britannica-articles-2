KINTORE

article · 216 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

KINTORE, a royal borough of Aberdeenshire in Scotland, situated on the river Don, in W. Long. 2. 6. N. Lat. 57. 10. It gives the title of earl to a branch of the noble family of Keith. The population in 1811 was 863.
