IMMOLATION

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

IMMOLATION, a ceremony used in the Roman sacrifices; it consisted in throwing upon the head of the victim some sort of corn and frankincense, together with the mala or salt cake, and a little wine.
