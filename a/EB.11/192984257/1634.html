LATINUS

Cross-references: ROME

LATINUS, king of the Latins in Italy, was the son of Faunus; and, it is said, began to reign about the 1216th year before the Christian era. Lavinia, his only daughter, married Aeneas, after that Trojan prince had killed Turnus king of the Rutuli. See ROME.
