LAUNCH

article · 306 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LAUNCH, in the sea language, signifies to put out: as, Launch the ship, that is, Put her out of dock: launch aft, or forward, speaking of things that are stowed in the hold, is Put them more forward; launch ho! is a term used when a yard is hoisted high enough, and signifies hoist no more.
