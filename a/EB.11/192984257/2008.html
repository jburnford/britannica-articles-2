LIBETHRIUS MONS, in Ancient Geography, a mountain of Boeotia, distant from Coronea 40 stadia; where

Libethrus where stood the statues of the Muses, and of the nymphs, surnamed Libethrides: a mountain probably combined with, or at least very near to, Helicon.
