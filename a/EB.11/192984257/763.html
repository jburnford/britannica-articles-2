INVISIBLE LADY

INVISIBLE LADY, an amusing experiment in Acoustics, which was exhibited in this country, first by a Frenchman, and afterwards by others; in which, from the construction of the apparatus, a lady who conversed, sung and played on musical instruments, seemed to be enclosed in a hollow metallic globe, of about a foot in diameter.
