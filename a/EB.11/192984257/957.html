JUDICIA CENTUMVIRALIA

article · 318 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JUDICIA CENTUMVIRALIA, in Roman antiquity, were trials before the Centumviri, to whom the prætor committed the decision of certain matters of inferior nature, like our justices of peace at the quarter sessions. During the judicia centumviralia, a spear was stuck up in the forum, to signify that the court was sitting.
