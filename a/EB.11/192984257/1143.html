KEMPTEN, a town of Germany, in Lower Suabia, and in Algov, and also in the territory of the abbot of Kempten, who was a prince of the empire. It belongs now to Bavaria. The inhabitants are Protestants, and amount to 5300. It is seated on the river Iller. E. Long. 10. 33. N. Lat. 47. 47.
