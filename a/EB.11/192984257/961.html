JUDICIUM Prævaricationis

sub_entry · 218 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JUDICIUM Prævaricationis, was an action brought against the prosecutor, after the criminal was acquitted, for suppressing the evidence of, or extenuating his guilt, rather than urging it home, and bringing it to light.
