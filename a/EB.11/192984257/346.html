ILLE AND VILAINE

article · 187 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ILLE AND VILAINE, a department in the northwest of France, comprising part of the ancient Brittany. It has some good pastures, but abounds in forests and heaths. Rennes is the chief town.
