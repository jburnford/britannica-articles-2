JUDOIGNE

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JUDOIGNE, a town in the kingdom of the Netherlands, in Brabant. Near this town the duke of Marlborough gained that signal victory over the French in 1706, called the battle of Ramillies. It is seated on river Gete, 13 miles south-east of Louvain, and 16 north of Namur.
