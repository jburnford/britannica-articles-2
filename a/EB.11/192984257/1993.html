LIBAW

article · 172 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LIBAW, a sea-port town of Courland, lying on the Baltic sea, consisting entirely of wooden houses. It belongs to Russia, and is situated in E. Long. 21. 27. N. Lat. 56. 27.
