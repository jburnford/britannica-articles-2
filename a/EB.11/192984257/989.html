JULIUS VICUS, in Ancient Geography, a town of the

Nemetes in Gallia Belgica; situated between the Tres Tabernæ and Noviomagus. Now Gemersheim, a town of the Lower Palatinate, on the west side of the Rhine. E. Long. 15. 8. N. Lat. 49. 12.
