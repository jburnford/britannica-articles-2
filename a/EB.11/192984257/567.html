INFLECTION

Cross-references: OPTICS

INFLECTION, called also diffraction, and deflection, in Optics, is a property of light, by reason of which, when it comes within a certain distance of any body, it will either be bent from it, or towards it; which is a kind of imperfect reflection or refraction. See OPTICS.
