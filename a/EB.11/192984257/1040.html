JUSTICIAR

article · 278 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

JUSTICIAR, in the old English laws, an officer instituted by William the Conqueror, as the chief officer of state, who principally determined in all cases, civil and criminal. He was called in Latin Capitalis justiciarius totius Angliæ. For JUSTICIAR in Scotland, see LAW INDEX.
