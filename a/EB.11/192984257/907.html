ISOLA

ISOLA, a town of Italy, in the kingdom of Naples, and in the Farther Calabria, with a bishop's see. It is a sea-port town, and is seated 15 miles south-east of St Severina. E. Long. 17. 33. N. Lat. 39. 2.
