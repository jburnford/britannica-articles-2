LAODICÆA

article · 275 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LAODICÆA on the sea, in Ancient Geography, according to Strabo, was a town of Seleucis in Syria, extremely well built, with a commodious harbour. The country about it yielded great quantities of wine. The city took its name from Laodice, mother of Seleucus the founder of it.
