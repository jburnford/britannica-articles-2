LEBUS

article · 234 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LEBUS, a town of Germany, in the circle of Upper Saxony, and in the marquisate of Brandenburg, with a bishop's see, secularized in favour of the house of Brandenburg. It is seated on the river Oder, in E. Long. 14. 44. N. Lat. 52. 28.
