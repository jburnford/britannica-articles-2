INSTITUTIONS

article · 232 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

INSTITUTIONS, in literary matters, denote a system of the elements or rules of any art or science.

Thus physical or medical institutions are such as teach the necessary praecepta to the practice of medicine, or the cure of diseases.
