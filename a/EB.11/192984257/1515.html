LANDES

article · 347 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LANDES, a department in the south-west of France. It contains 3700 square miles, with 240,000 inhabitants. It is one of the most barren districts in the kingdom, three-fourths of it consisting of heaths. The produce consists of corn, wine, olives, and wood.

There are mines of iron; and salt is made on the coast. Mont de Marsan is the chief town.
