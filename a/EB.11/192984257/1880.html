LEONTINI, or LEONTIUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of Sicily on the south side of the river Terias, 20 miles north-west of Syracuse. The territory, called Campi Leontini, was extremely fertile (Cicero): these were the Campi Lastrigoni anciently so called; the seat of the Lastrigons, according to the commentators on the poets.
