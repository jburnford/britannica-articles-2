ISERE

article · 337 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ISERE, a department in the south-east of France, containing 3440 square miles, and 472,000 inhabitants. The southern part lies among the Alps, and is mountainous; the northern part is more level. The climate is cold. The produce consists chiefly of hemp, silk, corn, wine, oil; and the pastures are extensive. Grenoble is the chief town.
