LANGPORT

article · 221 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

LANGPORT, a town in Somersetshire, 132 miles from London, is a well frequented town on the Parrot, between Bridgewater and Crewkern. Here are lighters which are constantly employed in carrying coals, &c. from Bridgewater.
