ROUND-HOUSE

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ROUND-HOUSE, a kind of prison for the nightly watch in London to secure disorderly persons till they can be carried before a magistrate.

Round-House, in a ship, the uppermost room or cabin on the stern of a ship, where the master lies.
