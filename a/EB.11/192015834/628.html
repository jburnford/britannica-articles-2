SAUCISSON

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SAUCISSON, is likewise a kind of fascine, longer than the common ones; they serve to raise batteries and to repair breaches. They are also used in making epaullements, in stopping passages, and in making traverses over a wet ditch, &c.
