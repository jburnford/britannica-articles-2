ROVIGNO

ROVIGNO, a town of Italy, in Istria, with two good harbours, and quarries of fine stone. It is seated in a territory which produces excellent wine, in a peninsula on the western coast. The population is estimated at 17,000. E. Long. 13. 53. N. Lat. 45. 14.
