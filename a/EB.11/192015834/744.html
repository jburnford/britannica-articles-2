SCHREVELIUS, CORNELIUS

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

SCHREVELIUS, CORNELIUS, a laborious Dutch critic and writer, who has published some editions of the ancient classics more distinguished for their elegance than accuracy: his Greek Lexicon is esteemed the best of all his works. He died in 1667.
