SABBATARIANS, or SEVENTH DAY BAPTISTS, a sect of Anabaptists; thus called, because they observed the Jewish or Saturday-Sabbath, from a persuasion that it was never abrogated in the New Testament by the institution of any other. 1 Sabbath.
