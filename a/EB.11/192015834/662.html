SCÆVOLA

SCÆVOLA, C. MUCIUS, a young Roman of illustrious birth, is particularly celebrated in the Roman history for a brave but unsuccessful attempt upon the life of Porsena king of Hetruria, about the year before Christ 504. See the article Rome, N° 71.
