Musterr-ROLL

sub_entry · 179 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Musterr-ROLL, that in which are entered the soldiers of every troop, company, regiment, &c. As soon as a soldier's name is written down on the roll, it is death for him to desert.
