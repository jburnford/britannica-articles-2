SANGUISORBA, GREATER WILD BURNET, a genus of plants, belonging to the tetrandria class, and in the natural method ranking under the 54th order, Miscellanea. See BOTANY INDEX. The cultivation of this plant has been greatly recommended as food for cattle. See BURNET, AGRICULTURE INDEX.
