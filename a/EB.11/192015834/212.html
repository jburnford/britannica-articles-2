ROS SOLIS

Cross-references: ROSS-SHIRE SUPPLEMENT

ROS SOLIS, Sun dew, an agreeable spirituous liquor, composed of burnt brandy, sugar, cinnamon, and milk-water; and sometimes perfumed with a little musk. It has its name from being at first prepared wholly of the juice of the plant ros solis, or drosera. See Drosera, Botany Index.
