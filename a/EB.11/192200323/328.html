MESOCHRI

article · 298 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MESOCHRI, were musicians among the ancients, who presided in concerts, and by beating a wooden desk regularly with their feet, directed the measure of the music. For the purpose of beating time, they wore wooden clogs, called by the ancients crupexia, which occasioned the sound to be better heard.
