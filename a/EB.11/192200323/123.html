MEDINA Celt, an ancient town of Spain, in Old Castile, and capital of a considerable duchy of the same name; seated near the river Xalón, in W. Long. 2. 9. N. Lat. 41. 15.

MEDINA de las Torres, a very ancient town of Spain, in Estremadura, with an old castle, and the title of a duchy. It is seated on the confines of Andalusia, at the foot of a mountain near Badajoz.
