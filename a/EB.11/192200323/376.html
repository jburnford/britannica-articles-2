METOPE

article · 235 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

METOPE, in Architecture, is the interval or square space between the triglyphs of the Doric frieze, which among the ancients used to be painted or adorned with carved work, representing the heads of oxen or utensils used in sacrifices.
