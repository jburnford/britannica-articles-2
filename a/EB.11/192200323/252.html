MENNITH, or MINNITH, Judges xi. 33. a town near

Mennith, near Heshbon (Jerome), in Arabia Petraea; in a district named Ecosipolis or twenty-towns, (Cellarius). There is also a Mennith mentioned Ezekiel xxvii. as being in a good wheat country: but whether the same with the foregoing is uncertain; though some think that the first Mennith lay in the country of Ammon, (Wells).
