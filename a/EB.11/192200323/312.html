MEROVINGIAN CHARACTER

article · 300 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MEROVIGIAN CHARACTER, derives its name from Merowee, the first king of France of that race, which reigned 333 years, from Pharamond to Charles Martel. This race is said by some to have terminated in Childeric III. A. D. 751. There are many MSS. in the French libraries still extant in this character.
