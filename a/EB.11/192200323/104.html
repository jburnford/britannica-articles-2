MECHOACAN

article · 345 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

MECHOACAN, or White Jalap, in the materia medica, the root of an American species of convolvulus, brought from Mechoacan, a province of Mexico, in thin slices like jalap, but larger, and of a whitish colour. It was first introduced into Europe about the year 1524 as a purgative: but since jalap became known, mechoacan has been little employed.
