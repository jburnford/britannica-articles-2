METHYMNA

article · 399 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

METHYMNA, in Ancient Geography, a town on the island of Lesbos. It was the second city of the island in greatness, population, and opulence. Its territory was fruitful, and the wines it produced excellent. It was the native place of Theophrastus, and of Arion the musician. When the whole island of Lesbos revolted from the power of the Athenians, Methymna alone remained firm to its ancient allies.
