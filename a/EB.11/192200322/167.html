CHYTRIUM

article · 264 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHYTRIUM, in Ancient Geography, a place in Ionia, in which formerly stood Clazomene; the Clazomenians, through fear of the Persians, removing from the continent to an adjacent island (Pausanias), Alexander reduced the island, by a mole or causeway, to a peninsula.
