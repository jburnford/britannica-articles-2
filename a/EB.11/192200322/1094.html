CONCEPTION, a town of North America, in New Spain, and in the audience of Guatemala. It is seated near the sea coast, 100 miles west of Porto-bello, and a small river that runs into the sea. W. Long. 81. 45. N. Lat. 10. 0.

CONCEPTION del Pao, a town of South America, in the province of Caraccas, containing 2300 inhabitants, who live chiefly by their cattle. It is 84 miles south-east of Caraccas. W. Long. 65. 10. N. Lat. 8. 42.
