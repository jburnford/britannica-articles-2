CONFLUENTES, in Ancient Geography, a place at the confluence of the Rhine and Moselle, supposed to be one of the 50 forts erected by Drusus on the Rhine, in Gallia Belgica: Now Coblentz, a town of Triers. E. Long. 7. 15. N. Lat. 50. 30.
