CRAB-YAWS

article · 178 chars · page image at NLS ↗

CRAB-YAWS, a name in Jamaica for a kind of ulcer on the soles of the feet, with hard callous lips, so hard that it is difficult to cut them. The ungt. cærul. fort. is their cure.
