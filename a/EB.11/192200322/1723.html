COTZ d'Or

sub_entry · 277 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COTZ d'Or, a department of France, comprising part of the ancient Burgundy. It contains 3430 square miles, and had 355,500 inhabitants in 1815. It is productive in corn, and yields excellent wine. The mineral productions are iron, marl, and millstones. Dijon is the chief town.
