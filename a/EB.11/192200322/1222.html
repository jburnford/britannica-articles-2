CONISSALÆ

article · 289 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONISSALÆ, an old term in natural history, signifying a class of fossils, which were said to be naturally and essentially compounded, not inflammable, nor soluble in water, found in detached masses, and formed of crystalline matter debased by earth. It included sand and gritty substances.
