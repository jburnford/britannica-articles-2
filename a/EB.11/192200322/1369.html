CONTRAINDICATION

article · 300 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONTRAINDICATION, in Medicine, is an indication which forbids that to be done which the main scope of a disease points out.

Suppose, e. gr. in the core of a disease a vomit were judged proper; if the patient be subject to a vomiting of blood, it is a sufficient contraindication as to its exhibition.
