CROFT

article · 290 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CROFT, a little close adjoining to a dwelling-house, and inclosed for pasture or arable land, or any other purpose.—In some ancient deeds, crusta occurs as the Latin word for a "croft;" but cum toftis etcroftis is more frequent. Croft is translated in Abbo Floricensis by predium, a "farm."
