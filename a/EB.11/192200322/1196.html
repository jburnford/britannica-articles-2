CONGESTION

article · 269 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONGESTION, in Medicine, a mass or collection of humours, crowded together and hardened in any part of the body, and there forming a preternatural tumour.

Congestion is effected by little and little; in which it differs from defluxion, which is more sudden.
