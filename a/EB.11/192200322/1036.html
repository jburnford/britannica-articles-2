COMPOSED

article · 261 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COMPOSED, or COMPOSED, is also used in general for a bordure, a pale, or a less, composed of two different colours or metals disposed alternately, separated and divided by fillets, excepting at the corners; where the junctures are made in form of a goat's foot.
