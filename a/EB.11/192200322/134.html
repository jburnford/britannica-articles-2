ICHTHYOLOGY Index.

The resorts of this fish are easily found, for they are generally holes overshadowed by trees, and this fish will be seen floating in such almost on the surface of the water in a hot day in great numbers. They are but a poor fish for the table, and are very full of bones; but they entertain the angler very much, and are of the number of those that are easily taken.
