CORRECTOR

article · 231 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CORRECTOR, in general, denotes something that mends the faults or bad qualities of others.

CORRECTOR of the Staple, a clerk belonging to the staple, whose business is to write down and record the bargains that merchants make there.
