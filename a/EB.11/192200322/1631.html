CORPUSCULE

article · 335 chars · page image at NLS ↗

CORPUSCULE, in Physics, a minute particle, or physical atom, being such as a natural body is made up of. By this word is not meant the elementary particles, nor hypothetical principles of chemists; but such particles, whether of a simple or compound nature, whose parts will not be dissolved nor dissipated by ordinary degrees of heat.
