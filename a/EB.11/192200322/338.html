CIVITA Vecchia

CIVITA Vecchia, a sea-port town of Italy in the patrimony of St Peter, with a good harbour and an arsenal. It contains about 9000 inhabitants, and was made a free port in 1741. The air is very unwholesome. E. Long. 12. 31. N. Lat. 45. 5.
