COMPOST

article · 290 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COMPOST, in Gardening, is a mixture of several earthy substances and dungs, either for the improvement of the general soil of a garden, or for that of particular plants. Almost every plant delights in some peculiar mixture of soils or compost, in which it will thrive better than in others.
