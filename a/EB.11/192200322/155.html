CHURLE, GEORLE, or CARL

Cross-references: GEORLE

CHURLE, GEORLE, or CARL, in the Saxon times, signified a tenant at will, who held of the thanes or nobles on condition of rent and service. They were of two sorts; one rented the estate like our farmers; the other tilled and manured the demesnes, and were called ploughmen. See GEORLE.
