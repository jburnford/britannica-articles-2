COMPLEX Term, or Idea

sub_entry · 313 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COMPLEX Term, or Idea, is a term compounded of several simple or incomplex ones. Thus, in the proposition, a just God cannot leave crimes unpunished; the subject of this proposition, viz. a just God, is a complex

plex term, or stands for a complex idea composed of two simple or incomplex ones, viz. God and just.
