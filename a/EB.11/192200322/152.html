CHURCHING

article · 272 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHURCHING OF WOMEN AFTER CHILDBIRTH, took its rise from the Jewish rite of purification. In the Greek church it was limited to the 40th day after delivery; but in the western parts of Europe no certain time was observed. There is an office in the liturgy for this purpose.
