CRAFT

article · 215 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CRAFT, a general name for all sorts of vessels employed to load or discharge merchant ships, or to carry alongside or return the stores of men of war. Such are lighters, hoys, barges, prames, &c. See those articles.
