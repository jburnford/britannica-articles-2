CHUPMESSAHITES

article · 391 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHUPMESSAHITES, a sect among the Mahometans, who believe that Jesus Christ is God, and the true Messiah, the Redeemer of the world; but without rendering him any public or declared worship. The word in the Turkish language signifies Protector of the Christians. Recaut says that there is abundance of these Chupmessahites among the people of fashion in Turkey, and some even in the seraglio.
