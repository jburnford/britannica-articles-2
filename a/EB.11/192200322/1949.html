GRENA

article · 201 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GREMA, a city and bishop's see of Italy, capital of a district of the Milanese, called from it Cremasco; it stands almost in the middle between Milan and Mantua, in E. Long. 10. 15. and N. Lat. 45. 20.
