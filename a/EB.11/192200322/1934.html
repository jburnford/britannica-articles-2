CREDIBILITY

article · 222 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CREDIBILITY, a species of evidence, less indeed than absolute certainty or demonstration, but greater than mere possibility; it is nearly allied to probability, and seems to be a mean between possibility and demonstration.
