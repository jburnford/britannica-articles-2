CONTIGUOUS

article · 197 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONTIGUOUS, a relative term understood of things disposed so near each other, that they join their surfaces, or touch. The houses in ancient Rome were not contiguous as ours are, but all insulated.
