CRYPTOGAMIA

CRYPTOGAMIA, (from κρυπτος, occultus, "concealed," and γαμης, nuptia, "nuptials"), the 24th class in the Linnæan system, comprehending those plants whose fructification is concealed, either through minuteness, or within the fruit. See BOTANY Index.
