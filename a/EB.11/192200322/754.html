COLLATION is also used among the Romanists for the meal or repast made in a fast day, in lieu of a supper. Only fruits are allowed in a collation: F. Lobi.

beau observes, that anciently there was not allowed even bread in the collations in Lent, nor any thing besides a few comfits and dried herbs and fruits; which custom, he adds, obtained till the year 1513. Cardinal Humbert observes farther, that in the middle of the 11th century there were no collations at all allowed in the Latin church in the time of Lent; and that the custom of collations was borrowed from the Greeks, who themselves did not take it up till about the 11th century.
