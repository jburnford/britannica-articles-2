CORBEL

article · 274 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CORBEL, in Architecture, the representation of a basket, sometimes seen on the heads of caryatides. The word is also used for the vase, or tambour, of the Corinthian column; so called from its resemblance to a basket, or because it was first formed on the model of a basket.
