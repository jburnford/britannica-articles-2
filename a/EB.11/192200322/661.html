COELOS PORTUS

article · 198 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COELOS PORTUS, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Chersonesus of Thrace, to the south of Sestos, where the Athenians erected a trophy, after a sea victory over the Lacedæmonians (Diodorus Siculus).
