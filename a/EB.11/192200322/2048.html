CROSLET

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: HERALDRY

CROSLT, in Heraldry, is when a cross is crossed again at a small distance from each of the ends. Upton says it is not so often borne by itself in arms as other crosses are, but often in diminutives, that is, in small croslts scattered about the field. See HERALDRY.
