COUPLET, a division of a hymn, ode, song, &c. wherein

Couplet
B
Courayer. wherein an equal number, or equal measure, of verses, is found in each part; which divisions, in odes, are called strophes. Couplet, by an abuse of the word, is frequently made to signify a couple of verses.
