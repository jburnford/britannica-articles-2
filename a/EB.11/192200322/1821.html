COUPÉE

article · 374 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COUPÉE, a motion in dancing, wherein one leg is a little bent, and suspended from the ground; and with the other a motion is made forwards.

The word in the original French signifies a cut. COUPLE CROSS, in Heraldry, the fourth part of a chevron, never borne but in pairs, except there be a chevron between them, saith Guillim, though Bloom gives an instance to the contrary.
