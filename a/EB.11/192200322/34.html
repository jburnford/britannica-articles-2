CHIVES

article · 199 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CHIVES, or STAMINA, in Botany, are slender thread-like substances, generally placed within the blossom, and surrounding the POINTAL, or PISTILLUM. They are formed of the woody substance of the plant.
