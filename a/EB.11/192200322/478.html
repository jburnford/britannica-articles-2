CLERK of the Outlawries

sub_entry · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CLERK of the Outlawries, an officer of the common pleas, and deputy to the attorney-general, for making out all writs of copias utlegatum, after outlawry, to which there must be the king's attorney's name.
