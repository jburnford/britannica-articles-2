CONTRIBUTIONS

article · 263 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONTRIBUTIONS, in a military sense, are impositions paid by frontier countries to secure themselves from being plundered and ruined by the enemy's army. The peasants till their ground under the faith of contributions, as securely as in the time of profound peace.
