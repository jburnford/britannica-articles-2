CORNET

article · 403 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CORNET, in the military art of the ancients, an instrument much in the nature of a trumpet; which when it only sounded, the ensigns were to march alone without the soldiers; whereas when the trumpet only sounded, the soldiers were to move without the ensigns. The cornets and buccineæ sounded the charge and retreat; and the cornets and trumpets sounded during the course of the battle. See Plate CLXIV.
