CROWN-Post

sub_entry · 241 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CROWN-Post, is a post in some building, standing upright in the middle between two principal rafters; and from which proceed struts or braces to the middle of each rafter. It is otherwise called a king-post, or king's piece, or joggle-piece.
