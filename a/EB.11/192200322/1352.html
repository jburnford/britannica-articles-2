CONTORSION

article · 286 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

CONTORSION, in general, signifies the action of twisting or wresting a member of the body out of its natural situation. Rope-dancers accustom themselves to contorsions of their limbs from their youth, to render the fibres of their articulations lax, and supple to all kinds of postures.
