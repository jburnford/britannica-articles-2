COUGH

article · 293 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COUGH, called the busk, is a disease to which young bullocks are subject. In this disorder the wind-pipe and its branches are loaded with small taper worms. Farmers count the disease incurable; but fumigations

with mercurials, as cinnabar, or with scetids, as tobacco, might prove serviceable.
