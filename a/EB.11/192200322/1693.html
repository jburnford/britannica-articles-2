COS TURCICA

article · 685 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

COS TURCICA, Turkey-stone, a species of stones which is arranged in the siliceous class. It is of a dull white, and often of an unequal colour; some parts appearing more compact than others. Its specific gravity is 2.398: it strikes fire with steel, and effervesces with acids. Mr Kirwan found that 100 parts of it contain 25 of carbonate of lime, and no iron. Cronstedt is of opinion that there are probably two sorts of stones known by this name, as that described by Wallerius neither gives fire with steel nor effervesces with acids. It is used as a whetstone; and those of the finest grains are the best hones for the most delicate cutting tools, and even for razors, lancets, &c.
