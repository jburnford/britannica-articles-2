EXUVIÆ

Cross-references: DELUGE

EXUVIÆ is also used for shells and other marine bodies, frequently found in the bowels of the earth; supposed to have been deposited there at the deluge; as being the real spoils of once living creatures. See DELUGE, CONCHOLGY, and GEOLOGY.
