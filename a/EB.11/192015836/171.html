ENCÆNIA

article · 265 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ENCÆNIA, the name of three several festivals celebrated by the Jews in memory of the dedication, or rather purification, of the temple by Judas Maccabæus, Solomon, and Zorobabel. This term is likewise used in church history for the dedication of Christian churches.
