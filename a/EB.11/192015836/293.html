EPIDAUURUM, EPIDAUURUS, or EPITAURUM, in Ancient Geography, a town of Dalmatia, on the Adriatic, built the same year, as is said, with Dyrrachium, 430 years after the destruction of Troy: a considerable town formerly, but now reduced to a small village, called Ragusi Vecchio; distant six miles from the modern Ragusi. E. Long. 19°. Lat. 42°. 20'.
