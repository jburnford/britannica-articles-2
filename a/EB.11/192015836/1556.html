FOLLICULUS

article · 262 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FOLLICULUS, (from follis, "a bag,") a species of seed-vessel first mentioned by LINNAEUS in his Delineatio Plantarum, generally consisting of one valve, which opens from bottom to top on one side, and has no suture for fastening or attaching the seeds within it.
