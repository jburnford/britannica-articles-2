Rough EMERALDS

sub_entry · 610 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Rough EMERALDS.—Those of the first and coarsest sort, called plumes, for grinding, are worth 27 shillings sterling the marc, or 8 ounces. The demi-morillons, 81. sterling per marc. Good morillons, which are only little pieces, but of fine colour, from 31. to 51. per marc. Emeralds, larger than morillons, and called of the third colour or sort, are valued at from 50. to 60. the marc. Emeralds, called of the second sort, which are in larger and finer pieces than the preceding, are worth from 61. to 75. per marc. Lastly, those of the first colour, otherwise called negres cartes, are worth from 110. to 115.
