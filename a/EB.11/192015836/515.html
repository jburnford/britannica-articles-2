ESTOPPEL

article · 246 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ESTOPPEL (formed of the French estouper, oppilare, obstipare, "to stop or block up"), in Law, an impediment or bar of action, arising from a man's own act or deed; against which a man is forbidden, by law, to speak, though it be to say the truth.
