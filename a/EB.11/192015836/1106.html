FELUCCA

article · 200 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FELUCCA, in sea affairs, a little vessel rowed with six oars, frequent in the Mediterranean; which has this peculiarity, that its helm may be applied either in the head or stern, as occasion requires.
