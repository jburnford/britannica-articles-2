EMBARCADERO

EMBARCADERO, in commerce, a Spanish term, much used along the coasts of America. It signifies a place which serves some other considerable city farther within land, for a port or place of shipping, i. e. of embarking and disembarking commodities. Thus Calao is the embarcadero of Lima. There are some embarcaderos 50 leagues from the city they serve in that capacity.
