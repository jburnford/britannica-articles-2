EROTIC

article · 236 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EROTIC (derived from ἔρως, "love;") whence ἐρωτικός), is applied to any thing which has a relation to the passion of love. In medicine we find the phrase delirium eroticum used for a kind of melancholy contracted through excess of love.
