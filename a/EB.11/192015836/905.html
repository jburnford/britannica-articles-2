FACULÆ

article · 327 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FACULÆ, in Astronomy, certain bright and shining parts, which the modern astronomers have, by means of telescopes, observed upon or about the surface of the sun; they are but seldom seen.—The word is pure Latin; being a diminutive of fax, "torch;" and supposed to be here applied from their appearing and disappearing by turns.
