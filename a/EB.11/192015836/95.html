EMMAUS, EMMAUS, or Ammaus, in Ancient Geography, a village, 60 stadia to the north-west of Jerusalem, or about seven miles: it afterwards became a town and a Roman colony, Nicopolis, (Jerome). Re-lan has another Emmaus towards Lydda, 22 miles from Jerusalem, (Itinerary); a third, near Tiberias.
