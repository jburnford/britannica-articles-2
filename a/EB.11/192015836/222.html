ENNEANDRIA

Cross-references: BOTANY

ENNEANDRIA, in Botany, (from enne, nine, and andria, a man or husband), the name of the ninth class in Linnaeus's sexual system, consisting of plants which have hermaphrodite flowers, with nine stamina or male organs. See BOTANY Index.
