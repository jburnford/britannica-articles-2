EQUIVOCATION

article · 320 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EQUIVOCATION, the using a term or expression that has a double signification. Equivocations are expedients to save telling the truth, and yet without telling a falsity. The fathers are great patrons of equivocations and mental reservations, holding that the use of such shifts and ambiguities is in many cases allowable.
