EPISTEMONARCH

article · 229 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EPISTEMONARCH, in the ancient Greek church, an officer of great dignity, who had the care of every thing relating to faith, in the quality of censor. His office answered very nearly to that of master of the sacred palace at Rome.
