EUDIOMETER

article · 314 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

Cross-references: CHEMISTRY

EUDIOMETER, an instrument for ascertaining the purity of the atmospheric air, or the quantity of pure oxygen or vital air contained in it, chiefly by means of its diminution, or the absorption of it by exposing certain substances to its action. Several kinds of eudiometers have been invented. See CHEMISTRY Index.
