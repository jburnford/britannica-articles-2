EUMARIDES

article · 184 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EUMARIDES, of ευμαρίς, "easy," among the ancients, a kind of shoes common to men and women.—The eumarides were used for pomp and delicacy, being neat, and painted with various colours.
