THE ATLANTA CHRONICLE IS A DAILY NEWSPAPER PUBLISHED EVERY DAY OF THE WEEK EXCEPT SUNDAY AND HOLIDAYS. SUBSCRIPTIONS ARE TAKEN AT ALL NEWSAGENTS AND DEALERS IN NEWSPAPERS. THE PRICE OF THE PAPER IS ONE DOLLAR PER ANNUM. THE PAPER IS SENT TO ALL NEWSAGENTS AND DEALERS IN NEWSPAPERS. THE PAPER IS SENT TO ALL NEWSAGENTS AND DEALERS IN NEWSPAPERS.

Filter
Finch.
Plutarch and Cornelius Nepos report that Lucullus the Roman general first lost his reason, and afterwards his life, by one of them. Lucretius the poet ended his life by the same way; and Caius Caligula, as Suetonius reports, was driven into a fit of madness by a filter given him by his wife Cæsonia, which story is mentioned by the same poet. Ovid likewise assures us, that this was the usual effect of such potions.

The ingredients they were made of were of various sorts; several of which applied by themselves were thought effectual.
