FIRE-LOCK, or FUSIL

sub_entry · 200 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FIRE-LOCK, or FUSIL, a small gun which fires with a flint. It is distinguished from the old musket or matchlock, which was fired with a match. The firelock is now in common use in the European armies.
