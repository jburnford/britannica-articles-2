EQUESTRIAN Order

EQUESTRIAN Order, among the Romans, signified their

quian their knights or equites; as also their troopers or horsemen in the field; the first of which orders stood in contradistinction to the senators; as the last did to the foot military, or infantry. Each of these distinctions was introduced into the state by Romulus.
