ESTOILLE'E

article · 169 chars · page image at NLS ↗

ESTOILLE'E, or CROSS ESTOILLE'E, in Heraldry, a star with only four long rays in form of a cross; and, accordingly, broad in the centre, and terminating in sharp points.
