FIBULA

FIBULA, in Surgery, an instrument in use among the ancients for the closing of gaping wounds.—Celsus speaks of the fibula as to be used when the wound was so patent as not easily to admit of being sewed. (Op. lib. vii. cap. 25. apud fin.)
