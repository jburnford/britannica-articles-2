EXODIUM

article · 219 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EXODIUM, in the ancient Greek drama, one of the four parts or divisions of a tragedy, being so much of the piece as included the catastrophe and unraveling of the plot, and answering nearly to our fourth and fifth acts.
