EXPONENTIAL Quantity

sub_entry · 220 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EXPONENTIAL Quantity, is that whose power is a variable quantity; as the expression a^x, or x^a. Exponential quantities are of several degrees and orders, according to the number of exponents or powers, one over another.
