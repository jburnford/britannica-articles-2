FIFE-Rails

sub_entry · 205 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FIFE-Rails, in a ship, are those that are placed on banisters, on each side of the top of the poop, and so along with haunces or falls. They reach down to the quarter deck, and to the stair of the gangway.
