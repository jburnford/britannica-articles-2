EPITRITUS

article · 387 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EPITRITUS, in prosody, a foot consisting of three long syllables and one short. Of these, grammarians reckon four kinds: the first consisting of an iambus and spondee, as sālūtāntēs; the second, of a trocheus and spondee, as cōncitātī; the third of a spondee and an iambus, as cōmmūnīcāns; and the fourth, of a spondee and trocheus, as īncāntārē. See the articles SPONDEUS, TROCHEUS, &c.
