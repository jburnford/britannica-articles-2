ENGRAILED

article · 242 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

ENGRAILED, or INGRAILED, in Heraldry, a term derived from the French gresly, "hail;" and signifying a thing the hail has fallen upon and broke off the edges, leaving them ragged, or with half rounds, or semicircles, struck out of their edges.
