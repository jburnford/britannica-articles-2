EVACUANTS

article · 207 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

EVACUANTS, in Pharmacy, are properly such medicines as diminish the animal fluids, by throwing out some morbid or redundant humour; or such as thin, attenuate, and promote the motion and circulation thereof.
