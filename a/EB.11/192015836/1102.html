FELT

article · 211 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FELT, in Commerce, a sort of stuff deriving all its consistence merely from being fulled, or wrought with lees and size, without either spinning or weaving. Felt is made either of wool alone or of wool and hair.
