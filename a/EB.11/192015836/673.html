Books and accounts are kept in piastres, soldi, and denari. The piastre here consists of 6 lires, and the lire contains 20 soldi, and the soldi 12 denari; and consequently exchange money is 6 times better than lire money. The course of exchange is from 48d. to 58d. sterling per piastre.
