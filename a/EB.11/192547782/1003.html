GOES

GOES, or TER GOES, a strong and considerable town of the United Provinces, in Zealand, and capital of the island of South Beverland. It communicates with the sea by a canal; and is 10 miles east of Middleburgh, and 30 north of Ghent. Population 3700. E. Long. 3. 50. N. Lat. 51. 33.
