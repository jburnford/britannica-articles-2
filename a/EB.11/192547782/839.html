GIGG, GIGA, or JIG

sub_entry · 517 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GIGG, GIGA, or JIG, in Music and Dancing, a gay, brisk, sprightly composition, and yet in full measure, as well as the allemand, which is more serious. Menage takes the word to arise from the Italian grieg, a musical instrument mentioned by Dante. Others suppose it to be derived from the Teutonic giga, or ghuighe, "a fiddle." This is a favourite air in most nations of Europe: its characteristic is double time, marked \frac{2}{2}, or \frac{2}{4}: it consists of two strains, without any determinate number of bars.
