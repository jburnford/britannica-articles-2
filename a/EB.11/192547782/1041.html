GOMORRAH

article · 256 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GOMORRAH, in Ancient Geography, one of the cities of the plain or of the vale of Siddim in Judea, destroyed together with Sodom by fire from heaven, on account of the wickedness of the people. To determine its particular situation at present is impossible.
