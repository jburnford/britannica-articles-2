GANGLION

article · 369 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GANGLION, in Anatomy, denotes a knot frequently found in the course of the nerves, and which is not morbid; for wherever any nerve sends out a branch, or receives one from another, or where two nerves join together, there is generally a ganglion or plexus, as may be seen at the beginning of all the nerves of the medulla spinalis, and in many other places of the body.
