FORUM Appii

FORUM Appii (Cicero, Luke); a town of the Volscii, in Latium, on the Via Appia; a little beyond the Tres Tabernæ; set down in the Jerusalem Itinerary as situated near the river Nymphæus: now entirely extinct.
