GALLOWAY, a county of Scotland, which gives the title of Earl to a branch of the noble family of Stuart. It is divided into two districts; the western, called Upper Galloway, being the same with Wigtonshire; and the eastern, or stewardry of Kirkcudbright, called Lower Galloway. See KIRKCUDBRIGHT and WIGTONSHIRE.
