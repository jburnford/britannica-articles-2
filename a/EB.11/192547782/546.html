GAMBIA

Cross-references: CHEMISTRY

GAMBIA, a large river of Negroland in Africa, running from east to west to the Atlantic ocean; it was once erroneously supposed to be a branch of the Niger.

GAMBOGE is a concreted vegetable juice, partly of a gummy and partly of a resinous nature, chiefly brought in large cakes or rolls from Cambaja in the East Indies. See CHEMISTRY and MATERIA MEDICA Index.
