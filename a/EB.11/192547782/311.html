FRUIT

article · 213 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRUIT, in its general sense, includes whatever the earth produces for the nourishment and support of animals; as herbs, grain, pulse, hay, corn, and flax, every thing expressed by the Latins under the name fruges.
