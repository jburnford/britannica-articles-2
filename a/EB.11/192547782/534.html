GALLOPER

article · 301 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GALLOPER, in artillery, is the name of a carriage which serves for a pound and a half gun. This carriage has shafts so as to be drawn without a limber, and is thought by some to be more convenient and preferable to other field carriages; and it may likewise serve for our light three and six pounders.
