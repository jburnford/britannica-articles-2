GIFT

article · 317 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GIFT, Donum, in Law, is a conveyance which passeth either lands or goods; and is of a larger extent than a grant, being applied to things moveable and immoveable; yet as to things immoveable, when taken strictly, it is applicable only to lands and tenements given in tail; but gift and grant are too often confounded.
