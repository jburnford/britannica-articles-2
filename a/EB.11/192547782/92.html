FORUM Gallorum

FORUM Gallorum, a small town of the Cispadana, on the Via Emilia, eight miles from Mintina, beyond the river Scultenna. Here Antony defeated Pansa, and was in his turn defeated by Hirtius: Now Castelfranco, in the territory of Bologna.—Another Forum Gallorum, a town of the Vascones in the Hither Spain: Now Gurrea, a small town of Arragon.
