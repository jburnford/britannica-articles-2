FRAUSTADT

FRAUSTADT, a town of Silesia, on the frontiers of Poland, remarkable for a battle gained by the Swedes over the Saxons in 1706. It is 70 miles N. N. W. of Breslau. E. Long. 15. 50. N. Lat. 51. 45.
