GLEAD, or GLADE, a name used in the northern parts of the kingdom for the kite. See FALCO, ORNITHOLOGY Index.

GLEAM is popularly used for a ray or beam of light. Among falconers a hawk is said to gleam when she casts or throws up filth from the gorge.
