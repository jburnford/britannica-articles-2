GODALMING

GODALMING, a town of England, in the county of Surrey, 35 miles from London. It is situated on the Wye, which has been rendered navigable for barges from Guildford, whence the communication is open to the Thames. The church is much admired for its neat and lofty spire. Here are manufactories of mixed and blue kerseys, of stockings, blankets, &c. Population 3543 in 1811. W. Long. o. 31. N. Lat. 51. 1.
