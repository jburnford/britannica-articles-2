GADARA

article · 243 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GADARA, in Ancient Geography, a town of the Peraea, or Transjordan, in the Decapolis, a very strong place. Restored by Pompey after its demolition by the Jews (Josephus). After Herod's death, it was joined to the province of Syria by Augustus.
