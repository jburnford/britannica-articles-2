FORNACALIA

article · 196 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FORNACALIA, or FORNICALIA, in Roman anti-
quity, a festival instituted by Numa, in honour
of Fornax, the goddess of ovens; wherein certain
cakes were made, and offered in sacrifice before the
ovens.
