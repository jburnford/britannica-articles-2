FRAME

article · 315 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRAME, among painters, a kind of square, consisting of four long slips of wood joined together, whose intermediate space is divided by threads into several little squares like a net; and hence sometimes called reticu-

la. It serves to reduce figures from great to small; or, to augment their size from small to great.
