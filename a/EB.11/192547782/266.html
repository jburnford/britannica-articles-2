FRIGATE-Built

sub_entry · 298 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRIGATE-Built, denotes the disposition of the decks of such merchant ships as have a descent of four or five steps from the quarter-deck and forecastle into the waist, in contradistinction to those whose decks are on a continued line for the whole length of the ship, which are called galley-built.
