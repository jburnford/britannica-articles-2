GIULA

article · 220 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GIULA, a strong town of Upper Hungary, on the frontiers of Transylvania. It was taken by the Turks in 1566, and retaken by the Imperialists in 1596. It is seated on the river Keresblan, in E. Long. 21. 1. N. Lat. 46. 25.
