GALLY

article · 289 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GALLY, in printing, a frame into which the compositor empties the lines out of his composing-stick, and in which he ties up the page when it is completed.

The gally is formed of an oblong square board, with a ledge on three sides, and a groove to admit a false bottom called a gally slice.
