FRAMLINGHAM

FRAMLINGHAM, a town in Sussex, 88 miles from London. It is a large old place, with a castle, supposed to have been built by some of the first kings of the East Angles; the walls yet standing, are 44 feet high, 8 thick, with 13 towers 14 feet above them, two of which are watch-towers. Population 1965 in 1811.
