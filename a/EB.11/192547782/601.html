GAS, in Chemistry, a general name for all permanently elastic fluids, which are obtained by chemical processes, as azotic gas, muratic acid gas, nitrous gas. See CHEMISTRY Index. It is derived from the German gascht or gast, signifying the ebullition attending the expulsion of elastic fluids from substances in a state of effervescence. It was first employed by Van Helmont.
