GERUND

GERUND, in Grammar, a verbal noun of the neuter gender, partaking of the nature of a participle, declinable only in the singular number, through all the cases except the vocative; as nom. amandum, gen. amandi, dat. amando, accus. amandum, abl. amando. The word is formed of the Latin gerundivus, and that from the verb gerere, "to bear." The gerund expresses not only the time, but also the manner of an action; as, "he fell in running post."
