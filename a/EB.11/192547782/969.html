GLYNN

article · 277 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

GLYNN, a county in the lower district of Georgia, in America, bounded on the east by the ocean, on the north by the river Alatomaha, by which it is separated from Liberty county, and on the south by Camden. The principal produce is cotton and rice. The chief town is Brunswick.
