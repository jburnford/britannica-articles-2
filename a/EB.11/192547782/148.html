FRENUM Penis

sub_entry · 317 chars · lineage ↗ · page image at NLS ↗

FRENUM Penis, a slender ligament, whereby the prepuce is tied to the lower parts of the glans of the penis. Nature varies in the make of this part; it being so short in some, that unless divided it would not admit of perfect erection. There is also a kind of little frænum, fastened to the lower part of the clitoris.
